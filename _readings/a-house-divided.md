<p><span class="v">19</span>Then he came into a house.  <span class="v">20</span>The multitude came together again, so that they could not so much as eat bread.  <span class="v">21</span>When his friends heard it, they went out to seize him: for they said, “He is insane.” </p>
<p><span class="v">22</span>The scribes who came down from Jerusalem said, “He has Beelzebul,” and, “By the prince of the demons he casts out the demons.” </p>
<p><span class="v">23</span>He summoned them, and said to them in parables, “How can Satan cast out Satan?  <span class="v">24</span>If a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand.  <span class="v">25</span>If a house is divided against itself, that house cannot stand.  <span class="v">26</span>If Satan has risen up against himself, and is divided, he can’t stand, but has an end.  <span class="v">27</span>But no one can enter into the house of the strong man to plunder, unless he first binds the strong man; and then he will plunder his house.”</p>
