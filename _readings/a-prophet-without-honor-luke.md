<p><span class="v">16</span>He came to Nazareth, where he had been brought up. He entered, as was his custom, into the synagogue on the Sabbath day, and stood up to read.  <span class="v">17</span>The book of the prophet Isaiah was handed to him.</p>
<p>He opened the book, and found the place where it was written,  <span class="v">18</span>“The Spirit of the Lord is on me, because he has anointed me to preach good news to the poor. He has sent me to heal the broken hearted, to proclaim release to the captives, recovering of sight to the blind, to deliver those who are crushed,  <span class="v">19</span>and to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord.” </p>
<p><span class="v">20</span>He closed the book, gave it back to the attendant, and sat down. The eyes of all in the synagogue were fastened on him.  <span class="v">21</span>He began to tell them, “Today, this Scripture has been fulfilled in your hearing.” </p>
<p><span class="v">22</span>All testified about him, and wondered at the gracious words which proceeded out of his mouth, and they said, “Isn’t this Joseph’s son?” </p>
<p><span class="v">23</span>He said to them, “Doubtless you will tell me this parable, ‘Physician, heal yourself! Whatever we have heard done at Capernaum, do also here in your hometown.’”  <span class="v">24</span>He said, “Most certainly I tell you, no prophet is acceptable in his hometown.  <span class="v">25</span>But truly I tell you, there were many widows in Israel in the days of Elijah, when the sky was shut up three years and six months, when a great famine came over all the land.  <span class="v">26</span>Elijah was sent to none of them, except to Zarephath, in the land of Sidon, to a woman who was a widow.  <span class="v">27</span>There were many lepers in Israel in the time of Elisha the prophet, yet not one of them was cleansed, except Naaman, the Syrian.” </p>
<p><span class="v">28</span>They were all filled with wrath in the synagogue, as they heard these things.  <span class="v">29</span>They rose up, threw him out of the city, and led him to the brow of the hill that their city was built on, that they might throw him off the cliff.  <span class="v">30</span>But he, passing through the middle of them, went his way. </p>
