<p><span class="v">1</span>Then six days before the Passover, Jesus came to Bethany, where Lazarus was, who had been dead, whom he raised from the dead.  <span class="v">2</span>So they made him a supper there. Martha served, but Lazarus was one of those who sat at the table with him. </p>
<p><span class="v">3</span>Mary, therefore, took a pound of ointment of pure nard, very precious, and anointed the feet of Jesus, and wiped his feet with her hair. The house was filled with the fragrance of the ointment. </p>
<p><span class="v">4</span>Then Judas Iscariot, Simon’s son, one of his disciples, who would betray him, said,  <span class="v">5</span>“Why wasn’t this ointment sold for three hundred denarii, and given to the poor?” </p>
<p><span class="v">6</span>Now he said this, not because he cared for the poor, but because he was a thief, and having the money box, used to steal what was put into it. </p>
<p><span class="v">7</span>But Jesus said, “Leave her alone. She has kept this for the day of my burial.  <span class="v">8</span>For you always have the poor with you, but you don’t always have me.” </p>
<p><span class="v">9</span>A large crowd therefore of the Jews learned that he was there, and they came, not for Jesus’ sake only, but that they might see Lazarus also, whom he had raised from the dead. </p>
<p><span class="v">10</span>But the chief priests conspired to put Lazarus to death also,  <span class="v">11</span>because on account of him many of the Jews went away and believed in Jesus. </p>
<h4>The Triumphal Entry</h4>
<p><span class="v">12</span>On the next day a great multitude had come to the feast. When they heard that Jesus was coming to Jerusalem,  <span class="v">13</span>they took the branches of the palm trees, and went out to meet him, and cried out, “Hosanna! Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord, the King of Israel!” </p>
<p><span class="v">14</span>Jesus, having found a young donkey, sat on it. As it is written,  <span class="v">15</span>“Don’t be afraid, daughter of Zion. Behold, your King comes, sitting on a donkey’s colt.”  <span class="v">16</span>His disciples didn’t understand these things at first, but when Jesus was glorified, then they remembered that these things were written about him, and that they had done these things to him. </p>
<p><span class="v">17</span>The multitude therefore that was with him when he called Lazarus out of the tomb, and raised him from the dead, was testifying about it.  <span class="v">18</span>For this cause also the multitude went and met him, because they heard that he had done this sign. </p>
