<p><span class="v">27</span>“Don’t work for the food which perishes, but for the food which remains to eternal life, which the Son of Man will give to you. For God the Father has sealed him.” </p>
<p><span class="v">28</span>They said therefore to him, “What must we do, that we may work the works of God?” </p>
<p><span class="v">29</span>Jesus answered them, “This is the work of God, that you believe in him whom he has sent.” </p>
<p><span class="v">30</span>They said therefore to him, “What then do you do for a sign, that we may see, and believe you? What work do you do?  <span class="v">31</span>Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness. As it is written, ‘He gave them bread out of heaven to eat.’” </p>
<p><span class="v">32</span>Jesus therefore said to them, “Most certainly, I tell you, it wasn’t Moses who gave you the bread out of heaven, but my Father gives you the true bread out of heaven.  <span class="v">33</span>For the bread of God is that which comes down out of heaven, and gives life to the world.” </p>
<p><span class="v">34</span>They said therefore to him, “Lord, always give us this bread.” </p>
<p><span class="v">35</span>Jesus said to them, “I am the bread of life. He who comes to me will not be hungry, and he who believes in me will never be thirsty.  <span class="v">36</span>But I told you that you have seen me, and yet you don’t believe.  <span class="v">37</span>All those whom the Father gives me will come to me. He who comes to me I will in no way throw out.  <span class="v">38</span>For I have come down from heaven, not to do my own will, but the will of him who sent me.  <span class="v">39</span>This is the will of my Father who sent me, that of all he has given to me I should lose nothing, but should raise him up at the last day.  <span class="v">40</span>This is the will of the one who sent me, that everyone who sees the Son, and believes in him, should have eternal life; and I will raise him up at the last day.” </p>
<p><span class="v">41</span>The Jews therefore murmured concerning him, because he said, “I am the bread which came down out of heaven.” </p>
<p><span class="v">42</span>They said, “Isn’t this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? How then does he say, ‘I have come down out of heaven?’” </p>
<p><span class="v">43</span>Therefore Jesus answered them, “Don’t murmur among yourselves.  <span class="v">44</span>No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws him, and I will raise him up in the last day.  <span class="v">45</span>It is written in the prophets, ‘They will all be taught by God.’ Therefore everyone who hears from the Father, and has learned, comes to me.  <span class="v">46</span>Not that anyone has seen the Father, except he who is from God. He has seen the Father.  <span class="v">47</span>Most certainly, I tell you, he who believes in me has eternal life. </p>
<p><span class="v">48</span>“I am the bread of life.  <span class="v">49</span>Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, and they died.  <span class="v">50</span>This is the bread which comes down out of heaven, that anyone may eat of it and not die.  <span class="v">51</span>I am the living bread which came down out of heaven. If anyone eats of this bread, he will live forever. Yes, the bread which I will give for the life of the world is my flesh.” </p>
<p><span class="v">52</span>The Jews therefore contended with one another, saying, “How can this man give us his flesh to eat?” </p>
<p><span class="v">53</span>Jesus therefore said to them, “Most certainly I tell you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you don’t have life in yourselves.  <span class="v">54</span>He who eats my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day.  <span class="v">55</span>For my flesh is food indeed, and my blood is drink indeed.”</p>
