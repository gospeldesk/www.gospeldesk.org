<p><span class="v">1</span>After these things, Jesus revealed himself again to the disciples at the sea of Tiberias. He revealed himself this way.  <span class="v">2</span>Simon Peter, Thomas called Didymus, Nathanael of Cana in Galilee, and the sons of Zebedee, and two others of his disciples were together.  <span class="v">3</span>Simon Peter said to them, “I’m going fishing.”</p>
<p>They told him, “We are also coming with you.”</p>
<p>They immediately went out, and entered into the boat. That night, they caught nothing.  <span class="v">4</span>But when day had already come, Jesus stood on the beach, yet the disciples didn’t know that it was Jesus.  <span class="v">5</span>Jesus therefore said to them, “Children, have you anything to eat?”</p>
<p>They answered him, “No.” </p>
<p><span class="v">6</span>He said to them, “Cast the net on the right side of the boat, and you will find some.”</p>
<p>They cast it therefore, and now they weren’t able to draw it in for the multitude of fish.  <span class="v">7</span>That disciple therefore whom Jesus loved said to Peter, “It’s the Lord!”</p>
<p>So when Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he wrapped his coat around him (for he was naked), and threw himself into the sea.  <span class="v">8</span>But the other disciples came in the little boat (for they were not far from the land, but about two hundred cubits away), dragging the net full of fish. </p>
<p><span class="v">9</span>So when they got out on the land, they saw a fire of coals there, and fish laid on it, and bread. </p>
<p><span class="v">10</span>Jesus said to them, “Bring some of the fish which you have just caught.” </p>
<p><span class="v">11</span>Simon Peter went up, and drew the net to land, full of great fish, one hundred fifty-three; and even though there were so many, the net wasn’t torn. </p>
<p><span class="v">12</span>Jesus said to them, “Come and eat breakfast.” None of the disciples dared inquire of him, “Who are you?” knowing that it was the Lord.  <span class="v">13</span>Then Jesus came and took the bread, gave it to them, and the fish likewise.  <span class="v">14</span>This is now the third time that Jesus was revealed to his disciples, after he had risen from the dead. </p>
