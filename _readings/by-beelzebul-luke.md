<p><span class="v">14</span>He was casting out a demon, and it was mute. When the demon had gone out, the mute man spoke; and the multitudes marveled.  <span class="v">15</span>But some of them said, “He casts out demons by Beelzebul, the prince of the demons.”  <span class="v">16</span>Others, testing him, sought from him a sign from heaven. </p>
<p><span class="v">17</span>But he, knowing their thoughts, said to them, “Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation. A house divided against itself falls.  <span class="v">18</span>If Satan also is divided against himself, how will his kingdom stand? For you say that I cast out demons by Beelzebul.  <span class="v">19</span>But if I cast out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your children cast them out? Therefore will they be your judges.  <span class="v">20</span>But if I by God’s finger cast out demons, then God’s Kingdom has come to you. </p>
<p><span class="v">21</span>“When the strong man, fully armed, guards his own dwelling, his goods are safe.  <span class="v">22</span>But when someone stronger attacks him and overcomes him, he takes from him his whole armor in which he trusted, and divides his plunder. </p>
<p><span class="v">23</span>“He that is not with me is against me. He who doesn’t gather with me scatters.  <span class="v">24</span>The unclean spirit, when he has gone out of the man, passes through dry places, seeking rest, and finding none, he says, ‘I will turn back to my house from which I came out.’  <span class="v">25</span>When he returns, he finds it swept and put in order.  <span class="v">26</span>Then he goes, and takes seven other spirits more evil than himself, and they enter in and dwell there. The last state of that man becomes worse than the first.” </p>
