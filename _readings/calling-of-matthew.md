<p><span class="v">9</span>As Jesus passed by from there, he saw a man called Matthew sitting at the tax collection office. He said to him, “Follow me.”</p>
<p>He got up and followed him. </p>
<p><span class="v">10</span>As he sat in the house, behold, many tax collectors and sinners came and sat down with Jesus and his disciples.  <span class="v">11</span>When the Pharisees saw it, they said to his disciples, “Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?” </p>
<p><span class="v">12</span>When Jesus heard it, he said to them, “Those who are healthy have no need for a physician, but those who are sick do.  <span class="v">13</span>But you go and learn what this means: ‘I desire mercy, and not sacrifice,’ for I came not to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.” </p>
