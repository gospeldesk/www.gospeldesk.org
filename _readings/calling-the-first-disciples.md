<p><span class="v">35</span>Again, the next day, John was standing with two of his disciples,  <span class="v">36</span>and he looked at Jesus as he walked, and said, “Behold, the Lamb of God!”  <span class="v">37</span>The two disciples heard him speak, and they followed Jesus. </p>
<p><span class="v">38</span>Jesus turned, and saw them following, and said to them, “What are you looking for?”</p>
<p>They said to him, “Rabbi” (which is to say, being interpreted, Teacher), “where are you staying?” </p>
<p><span class="v">39</span>He said to them, “Come, and see.”</p>
<p>They came and saw where he was staying, and they stayed with him that day. It was about the tenth hour.  <span class="v">40</span>One of the two who heard John, and followed him, was Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother.  <span class="v">41</span>He first found his own brother, Simon, and said to him, “We have found the Messiah!” (which is, being interpreted, Christ ).  <span class="v">42</span>He brought him to Jesus.</p>
<p>Jesus looked at him, and said, “You are Simon the son of Jonah. You shall be called Cephas” (which is by interpretation, Peter). </p>
<p><span class="v">43</span>On the next day, he was determined to go out into Galilee, and he found Philip. Jesus said to him, “Follow me.” </p>
<p><span class="v">44</span>Now Philip was from Bethsaida, of the city of Andrew and Peter.  <span class="v">45</span>Philip found Nathanael, and said to him, “We have found him, of whom Moses in the law, and the prophets, wrote: Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.” </p>
<p><span class="v">46</span>Nathanael said to him, “Can any good thing come out of Nazareth?”</p>
<p>Philip said to him, “Come and see.” </p>
<p><span class="v">47</span>Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him, and said about him, “Behold, an Israelite indeed, in whom is no deceit!” </p>
<p><span class="v">48</span>Nathanael said to him, “How do you know me?”</p>
<p>Jesus answered him, “Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you.” </p>
<p><span class="v">49</span>Nathanael answered him, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are King of Israel!” </p>
<p><span class="v">50</span>Jesus answered him, “Because I told you, ‘I saw you underneath the fig tree,’ do you believe? You will see greater things than these!”  <span class="v">51</span>He said to him, “Most certainly, I tell you all, hereafter you will see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man.” </p>
