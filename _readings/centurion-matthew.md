<p><span class="v">5</span>When he came into Capernaum, a centurion came to him, asking him,  <span class="v">6</span>and saying, “Lord, my servant lies in the house paralyzed, grievously tormented.”  <span class="v">7</span>Jesus said to him, “I will come and heal him.” </p>
<p><span class="v">8</span>The centurion answered, “Lord, I’m not worthy for you to come under my roof. Just say the word, and my servant will be healed. </p>
<p><span class="v">9</span>For I am also a man under authority, having under myself soldiers. I tell this one, ‘Go,’ and he goes; and tell another, ‘Come,’ and he comes; and tell my servant, ‘Do this,’ and he does it.”  <span class="v">10</span>When Jesus heard it, he marveled, and said to those who followed, “Most certainly I tell you, I haven’t found so great a faith, not even in Israel. </p>
<p><span class="v">11</span>I tell you that many will come from the east and the west, and will sit down with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the Kingdom of Heaven,  <span class="v">12</span>but the children of the Kingdom will be thrown out into the outer darkness. There will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.” </p>
<p><span class="v">13</span>Jesus said to the centurion, “Go your way. Let it be done for you as you have believed.” His servant was healed in that hour. </p>
