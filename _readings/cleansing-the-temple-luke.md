<p><span class="v">45</span>He entered into the temple, and began to drive out those who bought and sold in it,  <span class="v">46</span>saying to them, “It is written, ‘My house is a house of prayer,’ but you have made it a ‘den of robbers’!”  <span class="v">47</span>He was teaching daily in the temple, but the chief priests and the scribes and the leading men among the people sought to destroy him.  <span class="v">48</span>They couldn’t find what they might do, for all the people hung on to every word that he said. </p>
