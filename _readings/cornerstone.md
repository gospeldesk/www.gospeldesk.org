<p><span class="v">43</span>“Therefore I tell you, God’s Kingdom will be taken away from you, and will be given to a nation producing its fruit.  <span class="v">44</span>He who falls on this stone will be broken to pieces, but on whomever it will fall, it will scatter him as dust.” </p>
<p><span class="v">45</span>When the chief priests and the Pharisees heard his parables, they perceived that he spoke about them.  <span class="v">46</span>When they sought to seize him, they feared the multitudes, because they considered him to be a prophet. </p>
