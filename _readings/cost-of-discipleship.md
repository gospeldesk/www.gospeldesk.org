<p><span class="v">23</span>Jesus looked around, and said to his disciples, “How difficult it is for those who have riches to enter into God’s Kingdom!” </p>
<p><span class="v">24</span>The disciples were amazed at his words.</p>
<p>But Jesus answered again, “Children, how hard is it for those who trust in riches to enter into God’s Kingdom!  <span class="v">25</span>It is easier for a camel to go through a needle’s eye than for a rich man to enter into God’s Kingdom.” </p>
<p><span class="v">26</span>They were exceedingly astonished, saying to him, “Then who can be saved?” </p>
<p><span class="v">27</span>Jesus, looking at them, said, “With men it is impossible, but not with God, for all things are possible with God.” </p>
<p><span class="v">28</span>Peter began to tell him, “Behold, we have left all, and have followed you.” </p>
<p><span class="v">29</span>Jesus said, “Most certainly I tell you, there is no one who has left house, or brothers, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or land, for my sake, and for the sake of the Good News,  <span class="v">30</span>but he will receive one hundred times more now in this time, houses, brothers, sisters, mothers, children, and land, with persecutions; and in the age to come eternal life.  <span class="v">31</span>But many who are first will be last; and the last first.” </p>
<h4>Jesus Prophesies His Death</h4>
<p><span class="v">32</span>They were on the way, going up to Jerusalem; and Jesus was going in front of them, and they were amazed; and those who followed were afraid.</p>
<p>He again took the twelve, and began to tell them the things that were going to happen to him.  <span class="v">33</span>“Behold, we are going up to Jerusalem. The Son of Man will be delivered to the chief priests and the scribes. They will condemn him to death, and will deliver him to the Gentiles.  <span class="v">34</span>They will mock him, spit on him, scourge him, and kill him. On the third day he will rise again.” </p>
<h4>Servant Leadership</h4>
<p><span class="v">35</span>James and John, the sons of Zebedee, came near to him, saying, “Teacher, we want you to do for us whatever we will ask.” </p>
<p><span class="v">36</span>He said to them, “What do you want me to do for you?” </p>
<p><span class="v">37</span>They said to him, “Grant to us that we may sit, one at your right hand, and one at your left hand, in your glory.” </p>
<p><span class="v">38</span>But Jesus said to them, “You don’t know what you are asking. Are you able to drink the cup that I drink, and to be baptized with the baptism that I am baptized with?” </p>
<p><span class="v">39</span>They said to him, “We are able.”</p>
<p>Jesus said to them, “You shall indeed drink the cup that I drink, and you shall be baptized with the baptism that I am baptized with;  <span class="v">40</span>but to sit at my right hand and at my left hand is not mine to give, but for whom it has been prepared.” </p>
<p><span class="v">41</span>When the ten heard it, they began to be indignant towards James and John. </p>
<p><span class="v">42</span>Jesus summoned them, and said to them, “You know that they who are recognized as rulers over the nations lord it over them, and their great ones exercise authority over them.  <span class="v">43</span>But it shall not be so among you, but whoever wants to become great among you shall be your servant.  <span class="v">44</span>Whoever of you wants to become first among you, shall be bondservant of all.  <span class="v">45</span>For the Son of Man also came not to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.” </p>
