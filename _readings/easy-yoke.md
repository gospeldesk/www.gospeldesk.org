<p><span class="v">27</span>“All things have been delivered to me by my Father. No one knows the Son, except the Father; neither does anyone know the Father, except the Son, and he to whom the Son desires to reveal him. </p>
<p><span class="v">28</span>“Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest.  <span class="v">29</span>Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls.  <span class="v">30</span>For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.” </p>
