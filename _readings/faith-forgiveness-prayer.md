<p><span class="v">22</span>Jesus answered them, “Have faith in God.  <span class="v">23</span>For most certainly I tell you, whoever may tell this mountain, ‘Be taken up and cast into the sea,’ and doesn’t doubt in his heart, but believes that what he says is happening; he shall have whatever he says.  <span class="v">24</span>Therefore I tell you, all things whatever you pray and ask for, believe that you have received them, and you shall have them. </p>
<p><span class="v">25</span>“Whenever you stand praying, forgive, if you have anything against anyone; so that your Father, who is in heaven, may also forgive you your transgressions.  <span class="v">26</span>But if you do not forgive, neither will your Father in heaven forgive your transgressions.” </p>
