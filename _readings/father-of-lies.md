<p><span class="v">42</span>Therefore Jesus said to them, “If God were your father, you would love me, for I came out and have come from God. For I haven’t come of myself, but he sent me.  <span class="v">43</span>Why don’t you understand my speech? Because you can’t hear my word.  <span class="v">44</span>You are of your father, the devil, and you want to do the desires of your father. He was a murderer from the beginning, and doesn’t stand in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaks a lie, he speaks on his own; for he is a liar, and the father of lies.  <span class="v">45</span>But because I tell the truth, you don’t believe me.  <span class="v">46</span>Which of you convicts me of sin? If I tell the truth, why do you not believe me?  <span class="v">47</span>He who is of God hears the words of God. For this cause you don’t hear, because you are not of God.” </p>
<p><span class="v">48</span>Then the Jews answered him, “Don’t we say well that you are a Samaritan, and have a demon?” </p>
<p><span class="v">49</span>Jesus answered, “I don’t have a demon, but I honor my Father, and you dishonor me.  <span class="v">50</span>But I don’t seek my own glory. There is one who seeks and judges.  <span class="v">51</span>Most certainly, I tell you, if a person keeps my word, he will never see death.” </p>
