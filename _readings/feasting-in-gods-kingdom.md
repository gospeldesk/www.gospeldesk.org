<p><span class="v">12</span>He also said to the one who had invited him, “When you make a dinner or a supper, don’t call your friends, nor your brothers, nor your kinsmen, nor rich neighbors, or perhaps they might also return the favor, and pay you back.  <span class="v">13</span>But when you make a feast, ask the poor, the maimed, the lame, or the blind;  <span class="v">14</span>and you will be blessed, because they don’t have the resources to repay you. For you will be repaid in the resurrection of the righteous.” </p>
<p><span class="v">15</span>When one of those who sat at the table with him heard these things, he said to him, “Blessed is he who will feast in God’s Kingdom!” </p>
