<p><span class="v">16</span>Passing along by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew the brother of Simon casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen.  <span class="v">17</span>Jesus said to them, “Come after me, and I will make you into fishers for men.”  <span class="v">18</span>Immediately they left their nets, and followed him. </p>
<p><span class="v">19</span>Going on a little further from there, he saw James the son of Zebedee, and John, his brother, who were also in the boat mending the nets.  <span class="v">20</span>Immediately he called them, and they left their father, Zebedee, in the boat with the hired servants, and went after him. </p>
<p><span class="v">21</span>They went into Capernaum, and immediately on the Sabbath day he entered into the synagogue and taught.  <span class="v">22</span>They were astonished at his teaching, for he taught them as having authority, and not as the scribes. </p>
