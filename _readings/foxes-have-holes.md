<p><span class="v">57</span>As they went on the way, a certain man said to him, “I want to follow you wherever you go, Lord.” </p>
<p><span class="v">58</span>Jesus said to him, “The foxes have holes, and the birds of the sky have nests, but the Son of Man has no place to lay his head.” </p>
<p><span class="v">59</span>He said to another, “Follow me!”</p>
<p>But he said, “Lord, allow me first to go and bury my father.” </p>
<p><span class="v">60</span>But Jesus said to him, “Leave the dead to bury their own dead, but you go and announce God’s Kingdom.” </p>
<p><span class="v">61</span>Another also said, “I want to follow you, Lord, but first allow me to say good-bye to those who are at my house.” </p>
<p><span class="v">62</span>But Jesus said to him, “No one, having put his hand to the plow, and looking back, is fit for God’s Kingdom.” </p>
