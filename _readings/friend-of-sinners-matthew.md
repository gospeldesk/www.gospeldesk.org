<p><span class="v">16</span>“But to what shall I compare this generation? It is like children sitting in the marketplaces, who call to their companions  <span class="v">17</span>and say, ‘We played the flute for you, and you didn’t dance. We mourned for you, and you didn’t lament.’  <span class="v">18</span>For John came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, ‘He has a demon.’  <span class="v">19</span>The Son of Man came eating and drinking, and they say, ‘Behold, a gluttonous man and a drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’ But wisdom is justified by her children.” </p>
<p><span class="v">20</span>Then he began to denounce the cities in which most of his mighty works had been done, because they didn’t repent. </p>
