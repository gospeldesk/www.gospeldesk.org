<p><span class="v">23</span>Jesus himself, when he began to teach, was about thirty years old, being the son (as was supposed) of Joseph, the son of Heli,  <span class="v">24</span>the son of Matthat, the son of Levi, the son of Melchi, the son of Jannai, the son of Joseph,  <span class="v">25</span>the son of Mattathias, the son of Amos, the son of Nahum, the son of Esli, the son of Naggai,  <span class="v">26</span>the son of Maath, the son of Mattathias, the son of Semein, the son of Joseph, the son of Judah,  <span class="v">27</span>the son of Joanan, the son of Rhesa, the son of Zerubbabel, the son of Shealtiel, the son of Neri,  <span class="v">28</span>the son of Melchi, the son of Addi, the son of Cosam, the son of Elmodam, the son of Er,  <span class="v">29</span>the son of Jose, the son of Eliezer, the son of Jorim, the son of Matthat, the son of Levi,  <span class="v">30</span>the son of Simeon, the son of Judah, the son of Joseph, the son of Jonan, the son of Eliakim,  <span class="v">31</span>the son of Melea, the son of Menan, the son of Mattatha, the son of Nathan, the son of David,  <span class="v">32</span>the son of Jesse, the son of Obed, the son of Boaz, the son of Salmon, the son of Nahshon,  <span class="v">33</span>the son of Amminadab, the son of Aram, the son of Hezron, the son of Perez, the son of Judah,  <span class="v">34</span>the son of Jacob, the son of Isaac, the son of Abraham, the son of Terah, the son of Nahor,  <span class="v">35</span>the son of Serug, the son of Reu, the son of Peleg, the son of Eber, the son of Shelah,  <span class="v">36</span>the son of Cainan, the son of Arphaxad, the son of Shem, the son of Noah, the son of Lamech,  <span class="v">37</span>the son of Methuselah, the son of Enoch, the son of Jared, the son of Mahalaleel, the son of Cainan,  <span class="v">38</span>the son of Enos, the son of Seth, the son of Adam, the son of God. </p>
<p><span class="v">1</span>Jesus, full of the Holy Spirit, returned from the Jordan, and was led by the Spirit into the wilderness. </p>
