<p><span class="v">39</span>Jesus said, “I came into this world for judgment, that those who don’t see may see; and that those who see may become blind.” </p>
<p><span class="v">40</span>Those of the Pharisees who were with him heard these things, and said to him, “Are we also blind?” </p>
<p><span class="v">41</span>Jesus said to them, “If you were blind, you would have no sin; but now you say, ‘We see.’ Therefore your sin remains. </p>
<p><span class="v">1</span>“Most certainly, I tell you, one who doesn’t enter by the door into the sheep fold, but climbs up some other way, the same is a thief and a robber.  <span class="v">2</span>But one who enters in by the door is the shepherd of the sheep.  <span class="v">3</span>The gatekeeper opens the gate for him, and the sheep listen to his voice. He calls his own sheep by name, and leads them out.  <span class="v">4</span>Whenever he brings out his own sheep, he goes before them, and the sheep follow him, for they know his voice.  <span class="v">5</span>They will by no means follow a stranger, but will flee from him; for they don’t know the voice of strangers.” </p>
<p><span class="v">6</span>Jesus spoke this parable to them, but they didn’t understand what he was telling them.  <span class="v">7</span>Jesus therefore said to them again, “Most certainly, I tell you, I am the sheep’s door.  <span class="v">8</span>All who came before me are thieves and robbers, but the sheep didn’t listen to them.  <span class="v">9</span>I am the door. If anyone enters in by me, he will be saved, and will go in and go out, and will find pasture.  <span class="v">9</span>I am the door. If anyone enters in by me, he will be saved, and will go in and go out, and will find pasture.  <span class="v">10</span>The thief only comes to steal, kill, and destroy. I came that they may have life, and may have it abundantly.  <span class="v">11</span>I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.  <span class="v">12</span>He who is a hired hand, and not a shepherd, who doesn’t own the sheep, sees the wolf coming, leaves the sheep, and flees. The wolf snatches the sheep, and scatters them.  <span class="v">13</span>The hired hand flees because he is a hired hand, and doesn’t care for the sheep.  <span class="v">14</span>I am the good shepherd. I know my own, and I’m known by my own;  <span class="v">15</span>even as the Father knows me, and I know the Father. I lay down my life for the sheep.  <span class="v">16</span>I have other sheep, which are not of this fold. I must bring them also, and they will hear my voice. They will become one flock with one shepherd.”</p>
