<p><span class="v">44</span>“Let these words sink into your ears, for the Son of Man will be delivered up into the hands of men.” </p>
<p><span class="v">45</span>But they didn’t understand this saying. It was concealed from them, that they should not perceive it, and they were afraid to ask him about this saying. </p>
<p><span class="v">46</span>There arose an argument among them about which of them was the greatest.  <span class="v">47</span>Jesus, perceiving the reasoning of their hearts, took a little child, and set him by his side,  <span class="v">48</span>and said to them, “Whoever receives this little child in my name receives me. Whoever receives me receives him who sent me. For whoever is least among you all, this one will be great.” </p>
<p><span class="v">49</span>John answered, “Master, we saw someone casting out demons in your name, and we forbade him, because he doesn’t follow with us.”  <span class="v">50</span>Jesus said to him, “Don’t forbid him, for he who is not against us is for us.” </p>
