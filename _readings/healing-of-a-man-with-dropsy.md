<p><span class="v">1</span>When he went into the house of one of the rulers of the Pharisees on a Sabbath to eat bread, they were watching him.  <span class="v">2</span>Behold, a certain man who had dropsy was in front of him. </p>
<p><span class="v">3</span>Jesus, answering, spoke to the lawyers and Pharisees, saying, “Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath?” </p>
<p><span class="v">4</span>But they were silent.</p>
<p>He took him, and healed him, and let him go.  <span class="v">5</span>He answered them, “Which of you, if your son or an ox fell into a well, wouldn’t immediately pull him out on a Sabbath day?” </p>
<p><span class="v">6</span>They couldn’t answer him regarding these things. </p>
<h4>Parable of the Best Seat</h4>
<p><span class="v">7</span>He spoke a parable to those who were invited, when he noticed how they chose the best seats, and said to them,  <span class="v">8</span>“When you are invited by anyone to a marriage feast, don’t sit in the best seat, since perhaps someone more honorable than you might be invited by him,  <span class="v">9</span>and he who invited both of you would come and tell you, ‘Make room for this person.’ Then you would begin, with shame, to take the lowest place.  <span class="v">10</span>But when you are invited, go and sit in the lowest place, so that when he who invited you comes, he may tell you, ‘Friend, move up higher.’ Then you will be honored in the presence of all who sit at the table with you.  <span class="v">11</span>For everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.” </p>
