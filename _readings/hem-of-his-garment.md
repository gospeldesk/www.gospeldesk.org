<p><span class="v">18</span>While he told these things to them, behold, a ruler came and worshiped him, saying, “My daughter has just died, but come and lay your hand on her, and she will live.”  <span class="v">19</span>Jesus got up and followed him, as did his disciples.  <span class="v">20</span>Behold, a woman who had an issue of blood for twelve years came behind him, and touched the fringe of his garment;  <span class="v">21</span>for she said within herself, “If I just touch his garment, I will be made well.”  <span class="v">22</span>But Jesus, turning around and seeing her, said, “Daughter, cheer up! Your faith has made you well.” And the woman was made well from that hour.  <span class="v">23</span>When Jesus came into the ruler’s house, and saw the flute players, and the crowd in noisy disorder,  <span class="v">24</span>he said to them, “Make room, because the girl isn’t dead, but sleeping.” They were ridiculing him.  <span class="v">25</span>But when the crowd was put out, he entered in, took her by the hand, and the girl arose.  <span class="v">26</span>The report of this went out into all that land. </p>
