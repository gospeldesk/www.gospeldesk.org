<p><span class="v">7</span>Now Herod the tetrarch heard of all that was done by him; and he was very perplexed, because it was said by some that John had risen from the dead,  <span class="v">8</span>and by some that Elijah had appeared, and by others that one of the old prophets had risen again.  <span class="v">9</span>Herod said, “John I beheaded, but who is this, about whom I hear such things?” He sought to see him. </p>
<p><span class="v">10</span>The apostles, when they had returned, told him what things they had done. He took them, and withdrew apart to a desert region of a city called Bethsaida.  <span class="v">11</span>But the multitudes, perceiving it, followed him. He welcomed them, and spoke to them of God’s Kingdom, and he cured those who needed healing. </p>
