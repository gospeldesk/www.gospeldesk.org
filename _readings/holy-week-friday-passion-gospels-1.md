<p><span class="v">31</span>When he had gone out, Jesus said, “Now the Son of Man has been glorified, and God has been glorified in him.  <span class="v">32</span>If God has been glorified in him, God will also glorify him in himself, and he will glorify him immediately.  <span class="v">33</span>Little children, I will be with you a little while longer. You will seek me, and as I said to the Jews, ‘Where I am going, you can’t come,’ so now I tell you.  <span class="v">34</span>A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another. Just as I have loved you, you also love one another.  <span class="v">35</span>By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another.”  <span class="v">36</span>Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, where are you going?” Jesus answered, “Where I am going, you can’t follow now, but you will follow afterwards.”  <span class="v">37</span>Peter said to him, “Lord, why can’t I follow you now? I will lay down my life for you.”  <span class="v">38</span>Jesus answered him, “Will you lay down your life for me? Most certainly I tell you, the rooster won’t crow until you have denied me three times.  <span class="v">1</span>“Don’t let your heart be troubled. Believe in God. Believe also in me.  <span class="v">2</span>In my Father’s house are many homes. If it weren’t so, I would have told you. I am going to prepare a place for you.  <span class="v">3</span>If I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and will receive you to myself; that where I am, you may be there also.  <span class="v">4</span>Where I go, you know, and you know the way.”  <span class="v">5</span>Thomas said to him, “Lord, we don’t know where you are going. How can we know the way?”  <span class="v">6</span>Jesus said to him, “I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father, except through me.  <span class="v">7</span>If you had known me, you would have known my Father also. From now on, you know him, and have seen him.”  <span class="v">8</span>Philip said to him, “Lord, show us the Father, and that will be enough for us.”  <span class="v">9</span>Jesus said to him, “Have I been with you such a long time, and do you not know me, Philip? He who has seen me has seen the Father. How do you say, ‘Show us the Father?’  <span class="v">10</span>Don’t you believe that I am in the Father, and the Father in me? The words that I tell you, I speak not from myself; but the Father who lives in me does his works.  <span class="v">11</span>Believe me that I am in the Father, and the Father in me; or else believe me for the very works’ sake.  <span class="v">12</span>Most certainly I tell you, he who believes in me, the works that I do, he will do also; and he will do greater works than these, because I am going to my Father.  <span class="v">13</span>Whatever you will ask in my name, that will I do, that the Father may be glorified in the Son.  <span class="v">14</span>If you will ask anything in my name, I will do it.  <span class="v">15</span>If you love me, keep my commandments.  <span class="v">16</span>I will pray to the Father, and he will give you another Counselor, that he may be with you forever,—  <span class="v">17</span>the Spirit of truth, whom the world can’t receive; for it doesn’t see him, neither knows him. You know him, for he lives with you, and will be in you.  <span class="v">18</span>I will not leave you orphans. I will come to you.  <span class="v">19</span>Yet a little while, and the world will see me no more; but you will see me. Because I live, you will live also.  <span class="v">20</span>In that day you will know that I am in my Father, and you in me, and I in you.  <span class="v">21</span>One who has my commandments, and keeps them, that person is one who loves me. One who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him, and will reveal myself to him.”  <span class="v">22</span>Judas (not Iscariot) said to him, “Lord, what has happened that you are about to reveal yourself to us, and not to the world?”  <span class="v">23</span>Jesus answered him, “If a man loves me, he will keep my word. My Father will love him, and we will come to him, and make our home with him.  <span class="v">24</span>He who doesn’t love me doesn’t keep my words. The word which you hear isn’t mine, but the Father’s who sent me.  <span class="v">25</span>I have said these things to you, while still living with you.  <span class="v">26</span>But the Counselor, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, he will teach you all things, and will remind you of all that I said to you.  <span class="v">27</span>Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, give I to you. Don’t let your heart be troubled, neither let it be fearful.  <span class="v">28</span>You heard how I told you, ‘I go away, and I come to you.’ If you loved me, you would have rejoiced, because I said ‘I am going to my Father;’ for the Father is greater than I.  <span class="v">29</span>Now I have told you before it happens so that, when it happens, you may believe.  <span class="v">30</span>I will no more speak much with you, for the prince of the world comes, and he has nothing in me.  <span class="v">31</span>But that the world may know that I love the Father, and as the Father commanded me, even so I do. Arise, let us go from here.  <span class="v">1</span>“I am the true vine, and my Father is the farmer.  <span class="v">2</span>Every branch in me that doesn’t bear fruit, he takes away. Every branch that bears fruit, he prunes, that it may bear more fruit.  <span class="v">3</span>You are already pruned clean because of the word which I have spoken to you.  <span class="v">4</span>Remain in me, and I in you. As the branch can’t bear fruit by itself, unless it remains in the vine, so neither can you, unless you remain in me.  <span class="v">5</span>I am the vine. You are the branches. He who remains in me, and I in him, the same bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing.  <span class="v">6</span>If a man doesn’t remain in me, he is thrown out as a branch, and is withered; and they gather them, throw them into the fire, and they are burned.  <span class="v">7</span>If you remain in me, and my words remain in you, you will ask whatever you desire, and it will be done for you.  <span class="v">8</span>“In this is my Father glorified, that you bear much fruit; and so you will be my disciples.  <span class="v">9</span>Even as the Father has loved me, I also have loved you. Remain in my love.  <span class="v">10</span>If you keep my commandments, you will remain in my love; even as I have kept my Father’s commandments, and remain in his love.  <span class="v">11</span>I have spoken these things to you, that my joy may remain in you, and that your joy may be made full.  <span class="v">12</span>“This is my commandment, that you love one another, even as I have loved you.  <span class="v">13</span>Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friends.  <span class="v">14</span>You are my friends, if you do whatever I command you.  <span class="v">15</span>No longer do I call you servants, for the servant doesn’t know what his lord does. But I have called you friends, for everything that I heard from my Father, I have made known to you.  <span class="v">16</span>You didn’t choose me, but I chose you, and appointed you, that you should go and bear fruit, and that your fruit should remain; that whatever you will ask of the Father in my name, he may give it to you.  <span class="v">17</span>“I command these things to you, that you may love one another.  <span class="v">18</span>If the world hates you, you know that it has hated me before it hated you.  <span class="v">19</span>If you were of the world, the world would love its own. But because you are not of the world, since I chose you out of the world, therefore the world hates you.  <span class="v">20</span>Remember the word that I said to you: ‘A servant is not greater than his lord.’ If they persecuted me, they will also persecute you. If they kept my word, they will also keep yours.  <span class="v">21</span>But all these things will they do to you for my name’s sake, because they don’t know him who sent me.  <span class="v">22</span>If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have had sin; but now they have no excuse for their sin.  <span class="v">23</span>He who hates me, hates my Father also.  <span class="v">24</span>If I hadn’t done among them the works which no one else did, they wouldn’t have had sin. But now have they seen and also hated both me and my Father.  <span class="v">25</span>But this happened so that the word may be fulfilled which was written in their law, ‘They hated me without a cause.’  <span class="v">26</span>“When the Counselor has come, whom I will send to you from the Father, the Spirit of truth, who proceeds from the Father, he will testify about me.  <span class="v">27</span>You will also testify, because you have been with me from the beginning.  <span class="v">1</span>“I have said these things to you so that you wouldn’t be caused to stumble.  <span class="v">2</span>They will put you out of the synagogues. Yes, the time comes that whoever kills you will think that he offers service to God.  <span class="v">3</span>They will do these things because they have not known the Father, nor me.  <span class="v">4</span>But I have told you these things, so that when the time comes, you may remember that I told you about them. I didn’t tell you these things from the beginning, because I was with you.  <span class="v">5</span>But now I am going to him who sent me, and none of you asks me, ‘Where are you going?’  <span class="v">6</span>But because I have told you these things, sorrow has filled your heart.  <span class="v">7</span>Nevertheless I tell you the truth: It is to your advantage that I go away, for if I don’t go away, the Counselor won’t come to you. But if I go, I will send him to you.  <span class="v">8</span>When he has come, he will convict the world about sin, about righteousness, and about judgment;  <span class="v">9</span>about sin, because they don’t believe in me;  <span class="v">10</span>about righteousness, because I am going to my Father, and you won’t see me any more;  <span class="v">11</span>about judgment, because the prince of this world has been judged.  <span class="v">12</span>“I have yet many things to tell you, but you can’t bear them now.  <span class="v">13</span>However when he, the Spirit of truth, has come, he will guide you into all truth, for he will not speak from himself; but whatever he hears, he will speak. He will declare to you things that are coming.  <span class="v">14</span>He will glorify me, for he will take from what is mine, and will declare it to you.  <span class="v">15</span>All things whatever the Father has are mine; therefore I said that he takes of mine, and will declare it to you.  <span class="v">16</span>A little while, and you will not see me. Again a little while, and you will see me.”  <span class="v">17</span>Some of his disciples therefore said to one another, “What is this that he says to us, ‘A little while, and you won’t see me, and again a little while, and you will see me;’ and, ‘Because I go to the Father’ ?”  <span class="v">18</span>They said therefore, “What is this that he says, ‘A little while’ ? We don’t know what he is saying.”  <span class="v">19</span>Therefore Jesus perceived that they wanted to ask him, and he said to them, “Do you inquire among yourselves concerning this, that I said, ‘A little while, and you won’t see me, and again a little while, and you will see me?’  <span class="v">20</span>Most certainly I tell you, that you will weep and lament, but the world will rejoice. You will be sorrowful, but your sorrow will be turned into joy.  <span class="v">21</span>A woman, when she gives birth, has sorrow, because her time has come. But when she has delivered the child, she doesn’t remember the anguish any more, for the joy that a human being is born into the world.  <span class="v">22</span>Therefore you now have sorrow, but I will see you again, and your heart will rejoice, and no one will take your joy away from you.  <span class="v">23</span>“In that day you will ask me no questions. Most certainly I tell you, whatever you may ask of the Father in my name, he will give it to you.  <span class="v">24</span>Until now, you have asked nothing in my name. Ask, and you will receive, that your joy may be made full.  <span class="v">25</span>I have spoken these things to you in figures of speech. But the time is coming when I will no more speak to you in figures of speech, but will tell you plainly about the Father.  <span class="v">26</span>In that day you will ask in my name; and I don’t say to you, that I will pray to the Father for you,  <span class="v">27</span>for the Father himself loves you, because you have loved me, and have believed that I came from God.  <span class="v">28</span>I came from the Father, and have come into the world. Again, I leave the world, and go to the Father.”  <span class="v">29</span>His disciples said to him, “Behold, now you are speaking plainly, and using no figures of speech.  <span class="v">30</span>Now we know that you know all things, and don’t need for anyone to question you. By this we believe that you came from God.”  <span class="v">31</span>Jesus answered them, “Do you now believe?  <span class="v">32</span>Behold, the time is coming, yes, and has now come, that you will be scattered, everyone to his own place, and you will leave me alone. Yet I am not alone, because the Father is with me.  <span class="v">33</span>I have told you these things, that in me you may have peace. In the world you have oppression; but cheer up! I have overcome the world.”  <span class="v">1</span>Jesus said these things, and lifting up his eyes to heaven, he said, “Father, the time has come. Glorify your Son, that your Son may also glorify you;  <span class="v">2</span>even as you gave him authority over all flesh, so he will give eternal life to all whom you have given him.  <span class="v">3</span>This is eternal life, that they should know you, the only true God, and him whom you sent, Jesus Christ.  <span class="v">4</span>I glorified you on the earth. I have accomplished the work which you have given me to do.  <span class="v">5</span>Now, Father, glorify me with your own self with the glory which I had with you before the world existed.  <span class="v">6</span>I revealed your name to the people whom you have given me out of the world. They were yours, and you have given them to me. They have kept your word.  <span class="v">7</span>Now they have known that all things whatever you have given me are from you,  <span class="v">8</span>for the words which you have given me I have given to them, and they received them, and knew for sure that I came from you, and they have believed that you sent me.  <span class="v">9</span>I pray for them. I don’t pray for the world, but for those whom you have given me, for they are yours.  <span class="v">10</span>All things that are mine are yours, and yours are mine, and I am glorified in them.  <span class="v">11</span>I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I am coming to you. Holy Father, keep them through your name which you have given me, that they may be one, even as we are.  <span class="v">12</span>While I was with them in the world, I kept them in your name. Those whom you have given me I have kept. None of them is lost, except the son of destruction, that the Scripture might be fulfilled.  <span class="v">13</span>But now I come to you, and I say these things in the world, that they may have my joy made full in themselves.  <span class="v">14</span>I have given them your word. The world hated them, because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.  <span class="v">15</span>I pray not that you would take them from the world, but that you would keep them from the evil one.  <span class="v">16</span>They are not of the world even as I am not of the world.  <span class="v">17</span>Sanctify them in your truth. Your word is truth.  <span class="v">18</span>As you sent me into the world, even so I have sent them into the world.  <span class="v">19</span>For their sakes I sanctify myself, that they themselves also may be sanctified in truth.  <span class="v">20</span>Not for these only do I pray, but for those also who will believe in me through their word,  <span class="v">21</span>that they may all be one; even as you, Father, are in me, and I in you, that they also may be one in us; that the world may believe that you sent me.  <span class="v">22</span>The glory which you have given me, I have given to them; that they may be one, even as we are one;  <span class="v">23</span>I in them, and you in me, that they may be perfected into one; that the world may know that you sent me, and loved them, even as you loved me.  <span class="v">24</span>Father, I desire that they also whom you have given me be with me where I am, that they may see my glory, which you have given me, for you loved me before the foundation of the world.  <span class="v">25</span>Righteous Father, the world hasn’t known you, but I knew you; and these knew that you sent me.  <span class="v">26</span>I made known to them your name, and will make it known; that the love with which you loved me may be in them, and I in them.”  <span class="v">1</span>When Jesus had spoken these words, he went out with his disciples over the brook Kidron, where there was a garden, into which he and his disciples entered. </p>
