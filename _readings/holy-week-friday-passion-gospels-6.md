<p><span class="v">16</span>The soldiers led him away within the court, which is the Praetorium; and they called together the whole cohort.  <span class="v">17</span>They clothed him with purple, and weaving a crown of thorns, they put it on him.  <span class="v">18</span>They began to salute him, “Hail, King of the Jews!”  <span class="v">19</span>They struck his head with a reed, and spat on him, and bowing their knees, did homage to him.  <span class="v">20</span>When they had mocked him, they took the purple off him, and put his own garments on him. They led him out to crucify him.  <span class="v">21</span>They compelled one passing by, coming from the country, Simon of Cyrene, the father of Alexander and Rufus, to go with them, that he might bear his cross.  <span class="v">22</span>They brought him to the place called Golgotha, which is, being interpreted, “The place of a skull.”  <span class="v">23</span>They offered him wine mixed with myrrh to drink, but he didn’t take it.  <span class="v">24</span>Crucifying him, they parted his garments among them, casting lots on them, what each should take.  <span class="v">25</span>It was the third hour, and they crucified him.  <span class="v">26</span>The superscription of his accusation was written over him, “THE KING OF THE JEWS.”  <span class="v">27</span>With him they crucified two robbers; one on his right hand, and one on his left.  <span class="v">28</span>The Scripture was fulfilled, which says, “He was counted with transgressors.”  <span class="v">29</span>Those who passed by blasphemed him, wagging their heads, and saying, “Ha! You who destroy the temple, and build it in three days,  <span class="v">30</span>save yourself, and come down from the cross!”  <span class="v">31</span>Likewise, also the chief priests mocking among themselves with the scribes said, “He saved others. He can’t save himself.  <span class="v">32</span>Let the Christ, the King of Israel, now come down from the cross, that we may see and believe him.” Those who were crucified with him also insulted him. </p>
