<p><span class="v">32</span>There were also others, two criminals, led with him to be put to death.  <span class="v">33</span>When they came to the place that is called The Skull, they crucified him there with the criminals, one on the right and the other on the left.  <span class="v">34</span>Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they don’t know what they are doing.” Dividing his garments among them, they cast lots.  <span class="v">35</span>The people stood watching. The rulers with them also scoffed at him, saying, “He saved others. Let him save himself, if this is the Christ of God, his chosen one!”  <span class="v">36</span>The soldiers also mocked him, coming to him and offering him vinegar,  <span class="v">37</span>and saying, “If you are the King of the Jews, save yourself!”  <span class="v">38</span>An inscription was also written over him in letters of Greek, Latin, and Hebrew: “THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS.”  <span class="v">39</span>One of the criminals who was hanged insulted him, saying, “If you are the Christ, save yourself and us!”  <span class="v">40</span>But the other answered, and rebuking him said, “Don’t you even fear God, seeing you are under the same condemnation?  <span class="v">41</span>And we indeed justly, for we receive the due reward for our deeds, but this man has done nothing wrong.”  <span class="v">42</span>He said to Jesus, “Lord, remember me when you come into your Kingdom.”  <span class="v">43</span>Jesus said to him, “Assuredly I tell you, today you will be with me in Paradise.”  <span class="v">44</span>It was now about the sixth hour, and darkness came over the whole land until the ninth hour.  <span class="v">45</span>The sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was torn in two.  <span class="v">46</span>Jesus, crying with a loud voice, said, “Father, into your hands I commit my spirit!” Having said this, he breathed his last.  <span class="v">47</span>When the centurion saw what was done, he glorified God, saying, “Certainly this was a righteous man.”  <span class="v">48</span>All the multitudes that came together to see this, when they saw the things that were done, returned home beating their breasts.  <span class="v">49</span>All his acquaintances, and the women who followed with him from Galilee, stood at a distance, watching these things. </p>
