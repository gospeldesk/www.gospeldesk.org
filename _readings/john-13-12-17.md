<p><span class="v">12</span>So when he had washed their feet, put his outer garment back on, and sat down again, he said to them, “Do you know what I have done to you?  <span class="v">13</span>You call me, ‘Teacher’ and ‘Lord.’ You say so correctly, for so I am.  <span class="v">14</span>If I then, the Lord and the Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another’s feet.  <span class="v">15</span>For I have given you an example, that you also should do as I have done to you.  <span class="v">16</span>Most certainly I tell you, a servant is not greater than his lord, neither one who is sent greater than he who sent him.  <span class="v">17</span>If you know these things, blessed are you if you do them. </p>
