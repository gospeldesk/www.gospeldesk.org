<p><span class="v">1</span>“Don’t let your heart be troubled. Believe in God. Believe also in me.  <span class="v">2</span>In my Father’s house are many homes. If it weren’t so, I would have told you. I am going to prepare a place for you.  <span class="v">3</span>If I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and will receive you to myself; that where I am, you may be there also.  <span class="v">4</span>Where I go, you know, and you know the way.”  <span class="v">5</span>Thomas said to him, “Lord, we don’t know where you are going. How can we know the way?”  <span class="v">6</span>Jesus said to him, “I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father, except through me.  <span class="v">7</span>If you had known me, you would have known my Father also. From now on, you know him, and have seen him.”  <span class="v">8</span>Philip said to him, “Lord, show us the Father, and that will be enough for us.”  <span class="v">9</span>Jesus said to him, “Have I been with you such a long time, and do you not know me, Philip? He who has seen me has seen the Father. How do you say, ‘Show us the Father?’  <span class="v">10</span>Don’t you believe that I am in the Father, and the Father in me? The words that I tell you, I speak not from myself; but the Father who lives in me does his works.  <span class="v">11</span>Believe me that I am in the Father, and the Father in me; or else believe me for the very works’ sake. </p>
