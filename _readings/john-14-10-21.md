<p><span class="v">10</span>Don’t you believe that I am in the Father, and the Father in me? The words that I tell you, I speak not from myself; but the Father who lives in me does his works.  <span class="v">11</span>Believe me that I am in the Father, and the Father in me; or else believe me for the very works’ sake.  <span class="v">12</span>Most certainly I tell you, he who believes in me, the works that I do, he will do also; and he will do greater works than these, because I am going to my Father.  <span class="v">13</span>Whatever you will ask in my name, that will I do, that the Father may be glorified in the Son.  <span class="v">14</span>If you will ask anything in my name, I will do it.  <span class="v">15</span>If you love me, keep my commandments.  <span class="v">16</span>I will pray to the Father, and he will give you another Counselor, that he may be with you forever,—  <span class="v">17</span>the Spirit of truth, whom the world can’t receive; for it doesn’t see him, neither knows him. You know him, for he lives with you, and will be in you.  <span class="v">18</span>I will not leave you orphans. I will come to you.  <span class="v">19</span>Yet a little while, and the world will see me no more; but you will see me. Because I live, you will live also.  <span class="v">20</span>In that day you will know that I am in my Father, and you in me, and I in you.  <span class="v">21</span>One who has my commandments, and keeps them, that person is one who loves me. One who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him, and will reveal myself to him.” </p>
