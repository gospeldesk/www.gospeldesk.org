<p><span class="v">17</span>“I command these things to you, that you may love one another.  <span class="v">18</span>If the world hates you, you know that it has hated me before it hated you.  <span class="v">19</span>If you were of the world, the world would love its own. But because you are not of the world, since I chose you out of the world, therefore the world hates you.  <span class="v">20</span>Remember the word that I said to you: ‘A servant is not greater than his lord.’ If they persecuted me, they will also persecute you. If they kept my word, they will also keep yours.  <span class="v">21</span>But all these things will they do to you for my name’s sake, because they don’t know him who sent me.  <span class="v">22</span>If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have had sin; but now they have no excuse for their sin.  <span class="v">23</span>He who hates me, hates my Father also.  <span class="v">24</span>If I hadn’t done among them the works which no one else did, they wouldn’t have had sin. But now have they seen and also hated both me and my Father.  <span class="v">25</span>But this happened so that the word may be fulfilled which was written in their law, ‘They hated me without a cause.’  <span class="v">26</span>“When the Counselor has come, whom I will send to you from the Father, the Spirit of truth, who proceeds from the Father, he will testify about me.  <span class="v">27</span>You will also testify, because you have been with me from the beginning.  <span class="v">1</span>“I have said these things to you so that you wouldn’t be caused to stumble.  <span class="v">2</span>They will put you out of the synagogues. Yes, the time comes that whoever kills you will think that he offers service to God. </p>
