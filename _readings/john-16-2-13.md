<p><span class="v">2</span>They will put you out of the synagogues. Yes, the time comes that whoever kills you will think that he offers service to God.  <span class="v">3</span>They will do these things because they have not known the Father, nor me.  <span class="v">4</span>But I have told you these things, so that when the time comes, you may remember that I told you about them. I didn’t tell you these things from the beginning, because I was with you.  <span class="v">5</span>But now I am going to him who sent me, and none of you asks me, ‘Where are you going?’  <span class="v">6</span>But because I have told you these things, sorrow has filled your heart.  <span class="v">7</span>Nevertheless I tell you the truth: It is to your advantage that I go away, for if I don’t go away, the Counselor won’t come to you. But if I go, I will send him to you.  <span class="v">8</span>When he has come, he will convict the world about sin, about righteousness, and about judgment;  <span class="v">9</span>about sin, because they don’t believe in me;  <span class="v">10</span>about righteousness, because I am going to my Father, and you won’t see me any more;  <span class="v">11</span>about judgment, because the prince of this world has been judged.  <span class="v">12</span>“I have yet many things to tell you, but you can’t bear them now.  <span class="v">13</span>However when he, the Spirit of truth, has come, he will guide you into all truth, for he will not speak from himself; but whatever he hears, he will speak. He will declare to you things that are coming. </p>
