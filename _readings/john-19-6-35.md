<p><span class="v">6</span>When therefore the chief priests and the officers saw him, they shouted, saying, “Crucify! Crucify!” Pilate said to them, “Take him yourselves, and crucify him, for I find no basis for a charge against him.”  <span class="v">7</span>The Jews answered him, “We have a law, and by our law he ought to die, because he made himself the Son of God.”  <span class="v">8</span>When therefore Pilate heard this saying, he was more afraid.  <span class="v">9</span>He entered into the Praetorium again, and said to Jesus, “Where are you from?” But Jesus gave him no answer.  <span class="v">10</span>Pilate therefore said to him, “Aren’t you speaking to me? Don’t you know that I have power to release you, and have power to crucify you?”  <span class="v">11</span>Jesus answered, “You would have no power at all against me, unless it were given to you from above. Therefore he who delivered me to you has greater sin.”  <span class="v">13</span>When Pilate therefore heard these words, he brought Jesus out, and sat down on the judgment seat at a place called “The Pavement”, but in Hebrew, “Gabbatha.”  <span class="v">14</span>Now it was the Preparation Day of the Passover, at about the sixth hour. He said to the Jews, “Behold, your King!”  <span class="v">15</span>They cried out, “Away with him! Away with him! Crucify him!” Pilate said to them, “Shall I crucify your King?” The chief priests answered, “We have no king but Caesar!”  <span class="v">16</span>So then he delivered him to them to be crucified. So they took Jesus and led him away.  <span class="v">17</span>He went out, bearing his cross, to the place called “The Place of a Skull”, which is called in Hebrew, “Golgotha”,  <span class="v">18</span>where they crucified him, and with him two others, on either side one, and Jesus in the middle.  <span class="v">19</span>Pilate wrote a title also, and put it on the cross. There was written, “JESUS OF NAZARETH, THE KING OF THE JEWS.”  <span class="v">20</span>Therefore many of the Jews read this title, for the place where Jesus was crucified was near the city; and it was written in Hebrew, in Latin, and in Greek.  <span class="v">25</span>But there were standing by the cross of Jesus his mother, and his mother’s sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene.  <span class="v">26</span>Therefore when Jesus saw his mother, and the disciple whom he loved standing there, he said to his mother, “Woman, behold, your son!”  <span class="v">27</span>Then he said to the disciple, “Behold, your mother!” From that hour, the disciple took her to his own home.  <span class="v">28</span>After this, Jesus, seeing that all things were now finished, that the Scripture might be fulfilled, said, “I am thirsty.”  <span class="v">30</span>When Jesus therefore had received the vinegar, he said, “It is finished.” He bowed his head, and gave up his spirit.  <span class="v">31</span>Therefore the Jews, because it was the Preparation Day, so that the bodies wouldn’t remain on the cross on the Sabbath (for that Sabbath was a special one), asked of Pilate that their legs might be broken, and that they might be taken away.  <span class="v">32</span>Therefore the soldiers came, and broke the legs of the first, and of the other who was crucified with him;  <span class="v">33</span>but when they came to Jesus, and saw that he was already dead, they didn’t break his legs.  <span class="v">34</span>However one of the soldiers pierced his side with a spear, and immediately blood and water came out.  <span class="v">35</span>He who has seen has testified, and his testimony is true. He knows that he tells the truth, that you may believe. </p>
