<p><span class="v">1</span>Now there was a man of the Pharisees named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews.  <span class="v">2</span>The same came to him by night, and said to him, “Rabbi, we know that you are a teacher come from God, for no one can do these signs that you do, unless God is with him.”  <span class="v">3</span>Jesus answered him, “Most certainly, I tell you, unless one is born anew, he can’t see God’s Kingdom.”  <span class="v">4</span>Nicodemus said to him, “How can a man be born when he is old? Can he enter a second time into his mother’s womb, and be born?”  <span class="v">5</span>Jesus answered, “Most certainly I tell you, unless one is born of water and spirit, he can’t enter into God’s Kingdom.  <span class="v">6</span>That which is born of the flesh is flesh. That which is born of the Spirit is spirit.  <span class="v">7</span>Don’t marvel that I said to you, ‘You must be born anew.’  <span class="v">8</span>The wind blows where it wants to, and you hear its sound, but don’t know where it comes from and where it is going. So is everyone who is born of the Spirit.”  <span class="v">9</span>Nicodemus answered him, “How can these things be?”  <span class="v">10</span>Jesus answered him, “Are you the teacher of Israel, and don’t understand these things?  <span class="v">11</span>Most certainly I tell you, we speak that which we know, and testify of that which we have seen, and you don’t receive our witness.  <span class="v">12</span>If I told you earthly things and you don’t believe, how will you believe if I tell you heavenly things?  <span class="v">13</span>No one has ascended into heaven, but he who descended out of heaven, the Son of Man, who is in heaven.  <span class="v">14</span>As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of Man be lifted up,  <span class="v">15</span>that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.  <span class="v">16</span>For God so loved the world, that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish, but have eternal life.  <span class="v">17</span>For God didn’t send his Son into the world to judge the world, but that the world should be saved through him.  <span class="v">18</span>He who believes in him is not judged. He who doesn’t believe has been judged already, because he has not believed in the name of the one and only Son of God.  <span class="v">19</span>This is the judgment, that the light has come into the world, and men loved the darkness rather than the light; for their works were evil.  <span class="v">20</span>For everyone who does evil hates the light, and doesn’t come to the light, lest his works would be exposed.  <span class="v">21</span>But he who does the truth comes to the light, that his works may be revealed, that they have been done in God.” </p>
