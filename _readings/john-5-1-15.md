<p><span class="v">1</span>After these things, there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.  <span class="v">2</span>Now in Jerusalem by the sheep gate, there is a pool, which is called in Hebrew, “Bethesda”, having five porches.  <span class="v">3</span>In these lay a great multitude of those who were sick, blind, lame, or paralyzed, waiting for the moving of the water;  <span class="v">4</span>for an angel went down at certain times into the pool, and stirred up the water. Whoever stepped in first after the stirring of the water was healed of whatever disease he had.  <span class="v">5</span>A certain man was there, who had been sick for thirty-eight years.  <span class="v">6</span>When Jesus saw him lying there, and knew that he had been sick for a long time, he asked him, “Do you want to be made well?”  <span class="v">7</span>The sick man answered him, “Sir, I have no one to put me into the pool when the water is stirred up, but while I’m coming, another steps down before me.”  <span class="v">8</span>Jesus said to him, “Arise, take up your mat, and walk.”  <span class="v">9</span>Immediately, the man was made well, and took up his mat and walked. Now it was the Sabbath on that day.  <span class="v">10</span>So the Jews said to him who was cured, “It is the Sabbath. It is not lawful for you to carry the mat.”  <span class="v">11</span>He answered them, “He who made me well, the same said to me, ‘Take up your mat, and walk.’ ”  <span class="v">12</span>Then they asked him, “Who is the man who said to you, ‘Take up your mat, and walk’ ?”  <span class="v">13</span>But he who was healed didn’t know who it was, for Jesus had withdrawn, a crowd being in the place.  <span class="v">14</span>Afterward Jesus found him in the temple, and said to him, “Behold, you are made well. Sin no more, so that nothing worse happens to you.”  <span class="v">15</span>The man went away, and told the Jews that it was Jesus who had made him well. </p>
