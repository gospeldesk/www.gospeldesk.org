<p><span class="v">16</span>For this cause the Jews persecuted Jesus, and sought to kill him, because he did these things on the Sabbath.  <span class="v">17</span>But Jesus answered them, “My Father is still working, so I am working, too.”  <span class="v">18</span>For this cause therefore the Jews sought all the more to kill him, because he not only broke the Sabbath, but also called God his own Father, making himself equal with God.  <span class="v">19</span>Jesus therefore answered them, “Most certainly, I tell you, the Son can do nothing of himself, but what he sees the Father doing. For whatever things he does, these the Son also does likewise.  <span class="v">20</span>For the Father has affection for the Son, and shows him all things that he himself does. He will show him greater works than these, that you may marvel.  <span class="v">21</span>For as the Father raises the dead and gives them life, even so the Son also gives life to whom he desires.  <span class="v">22</span>For the Father judges no one, but he has given all judgment to the Son,  <span class="v">23</span>that all may honor the Son, even as they honor the Father. He who doesn’t honor the Son doesn’t honor the Father who sent him.  <span class="v">24</span>“Most certainly I tell you, he who hears my word, and believes him who sent me, has eternal life, and doesn’t come into judgment, but has passed out of death into life. </p>
