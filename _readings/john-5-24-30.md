<p><span class="v">24</span>“Most certainly I tell you, he who hears my word, and believes him who sent me, has eternal life, and doesn’t come into judgment, but has passed out of death into life.  <span class="v">25</span>Most certainly, I tell you, the hour comes, and now is, when the dead will hear the Son of God’s voice; and those who hear will live.  <span class="v">26</span>For as the Father has life in himself, even so he gave to the Son also to have life in himself.  <span class="v">27</span>He also gave him authority to execute judgment, because he is a son of man.  <span class="v">28</span>Don’t marvel at this, for the hour comes, in which all that are in the tombs will hear his voice,  <span class="v">29</span>and will come out; those who have done good, to the resurrection of life; and those who have done evil, to the resurrection of judgment.  <span class="v">30</span>I can of myself do nothing. As I hear, I judge, and my judgment is righteous; because I don’t seek my own will, but the will of my Father who sent me. </p>
