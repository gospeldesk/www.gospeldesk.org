<p><span class="v">27</span>Don’t work for the food which perishes, but for the food which remains to eternal life, which the Son of Man will give to you. For God the Father has sealed him.”  <span class="v">28</span>They said therefore to him, “What must we do, that we may work the works of God?”  <span class="v">29</span>Jesus answered them, “This is the work of God, that you believe in him whom he has sent.”  <span class="v">30</span>They said therefore to him, “What then do you do for a sign, that we may see, and believe you? What work do you do?  <span class="v">31</span>Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness. As it is written, ‘He gave them bread out of heaven to eat.’”  <span class="v">32</span>Jesus therefore said to them, “Most certainly, I tell you, it wasn’t Moses who gave you the bread out of heaven, but my Father gives you the true bread out of heaven.  <span class="v">33</span>For the bread of God is that which comes down out of heaven, and gives life to the world.”  <span class="v">34</span>They said therefore to him, “Lord, always give us this bread.” </p>
