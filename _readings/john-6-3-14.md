<p><span class="v">3</span>Jesus went up into the mountain, and he sat there with his disciples.  <span class="v">4</span>Now the Passover, the feast of the Jews, was at hand.  <span class="v">5</span>Jesus therefore lifting up his eyes, and seeing that a great multitude was coming to him, said to Philip, “Where are we to buy bread, that these may eat?”  <span class="v">6</span>This he said to test him, for he himself knew what he would do.  <span class="v">7</span>Philip answered him, “Two hundred denarii worth of bread is not sufficient for them, that everyone of them may receive a little.”  <span class="v">8</span>One of his disciples, Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, said to him,  <span class="v">9</span>“There is a boy here who has five barley loaves and two fish, but what are these among so many?”  <span class="v">10</span>Jesus said, “Have the people sit down.” Now there was much grass in that place. So the men sat down, in number about five thousand.  <span class="v">11</span>Jesus took the loaves; and having given thanks, he distributed to the disciples, and the disciples to those who were sitting down; likewise also of the fish as much as they desired.  <span class="v">12</span>When they were filled, he said to his disciples, “Gather up the broken pieces which are left over, that nothing be lost.”  <span class="v">13</span>So they gathered them up, and filled twelve baskets with broken pieces from the five barley loaves, which were left over by those who had eaten.  <span class="v">14</span>When therefore the people saw the sign which Jesus did, they said, “This is truly the prophet who comes into the world.” </p>
