<p><span class="v">35</span>Jesus said to them, “I am the bread of life. He who comes to me will not be hungry, and he who believes in me will never be thirsty.  <span class="v">36</span>But I told you that you have seen me, and yet you don’t believe.  <span class="v">37</span>All those whom the Father gives me will come to me. He who comes to me I will in no way throw out.  <span class="v">38</span>For I have come down from heaven, not to do my own will, but the will of him who sent me.  <span class="v">39</span>This is the will of my Father who sent me, that of all he has given to me I should lose nothing, but should raise him up at the last day. </p>
