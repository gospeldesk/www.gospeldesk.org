<p><span class="v">37</span>Now on the last and greatest day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried out, “If anyone is thirsty, let him come to me and drink!  <span class="v">38</span>He who believes in me, as the Scripture has said, from within him will flow rivers of living water.”  <span class="v">39</span>But he said this about the Spirit, which those believing in him were to receive. For the Holy Spirit was not yet given, because Jesus wasn’t yet glorified.  <span class="v">40</span>Many of the multitude therefore, when they heard these words, said, “This is truly the prophet.”  <span class="v">41</span>Others said, “This is the Christ.” But some said, “What, does the Christ come out of Galilee?  <span class="v">42</span>Hasn’t the Scripture said that the Christ comes of the offspring of David, and from Bethlehem, the village where David was?”  <span class="v">43</span>So there arose a division in the multitude because of him.  <span class="v">44</span>Some of them would have arrested him, but no one laid hands on him.  <span class="v">45</span>The officers therefore came to the chief priests and Pharisees, and they said to them, “Why didn’t you bring him?”  <span class="v">46</span>The officers answered, “No man ever spoke like this man!”  <span class="v">47</span>The Pharisees therefore answered them, “You aren’t also led astray, are you?  <span class="v">48</span>Have any of the rulers believed in him, or of the Pharisees?  <span class="v">49</span>But this multitude that doesn’t know the law is accursed.”  <span class="v">50</span>Nicodemus (he who came to him by night, being one of them) said to them,  <span class="v">51</span>“Does our law judge a man, unless it first hears from him personally and knows what he does?”  <span class="v">52</span>They answered him, “Are you also from Galilee? Search, and see that no prophet has arisen out of Galilee.”  <span class="v">53</span>Everyone went to his own house,  <span class="v">12</span>Again, therefore, Jesus spoke to them, saying, “I am the light of the world. He who follows me will not walk in the darkness, but will have the light of life.” </p>
