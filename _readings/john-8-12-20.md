<p><span class="v">12</span>Again, therefore, Jesus spoke to them, saying, “I am the light of the world. He who follows me will not walk in the darkness, but will have the light of life.”  <span class="v">13</span>The Pharisees therefore said to him, “You testify about yourself. Your testimony is not valid.”  <span class="v">14</span>Jesus answered them, “Even if I testify about myself, my testimony is true, for I know where I came from, and where I am going; but you don’t know where I came from, or where I am going.  <span class="v">15</span>You judge according to the flesh. I judge no one.  <span class="v">16</span>Even if I do judge, my judgment is true, for I am not alone, but I am with the Father who sent me.  <span class="v">17</span>It’s also written in your law that the testimony of two people is valid.  <span class="v">18</span>I am one who testifies about myself, and the Father who sent me testifies about me.”  <span class="v">19</span>They said therefore to him, “Where is your Father?” Jesus answered, “You know neither me, nor my Father. If you knew me, you would know my Father also.”  <span class="v">20</span>Jesus spoke these words in the treasury, as he taught in the temple. Yet no one arrested him, because his hour had not yet come. </p>
