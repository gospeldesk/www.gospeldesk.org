<p><span class="v">1</span>As he passed by, he saw a man blind from birth.  <span class="v">2</span>His disciples asked him, “Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?”  <span class="v">3</span>Jesus answered, “Neither did this man sin, nor his parents; but, that the works of God might be revealed in him.  <span class="v">4</span>I must work the works of him who sent me, while it is day. The night is coming, when no one can work.  <span class="v">5</span>While I am in the world, I am the light of the world.”  <span class="v">6</span>When he had said this, he spat on the ground, made mud with the saliva, anointed the blind man’s eyes with the mud,  <span class="v">7</span>and said to him, “Go, wash in the pool of Siloam” (which means “Sent”). So he went away, washed, and came back seeing.  <span class="v">8</span>The neighbors therefore, and those who saw that he was blind before, said, “Isn’t this he who sat and begged?”  <span class="v">9</span>Others were saying, “It is he.” Still others were saying, “He looks like him.” He said, “I am he.”  <span class="v">10</span>They therefore were asking him, “How were your eyes opened?”  <span class="v">11</span>He answered, “A man called Jesus made mud, anointed my eyes, and said to me, ‘Go to the pool of Siloam, and wash.’ So I went away and washed, and I received sight.”  <span class="v">12</span>Then they asked him, “Where is he?” He said, “I don’t know.”  <span class="v">13</span>They brought him who had been blind to the Pharisees.  <span class="v">14</span>It was a Sabbath when Jesus made the mud and opened his eyes.  <span class="v">15</span>Again therefore the Pharisees also asked him how he received his sight. He said to them, “He put mud on my eyes, I washed, and I see.”  <span class="v">16</span>Some therefore of the Pharisees said, “This man is not from God, because he doesn’t keep the Sabbath.” Others said, “How can a man who is a sinner do such signs?” There was division among them.  <span class="v">17</span>Therefore they asked the blind man again, “What do you say about him, because he opened your eyes?” He said, “He is a prophet.”  <span class="v">18</span>The Jews therefore did not believe concerning him, that he had been blind, and had received his sight, until they called the parents of him who had received his sight,  <span class="v">19</span>and asked them, “Is this your son, whom you say was born blind? How then does he now see?”  <span class="v">20</span>His parents answered them, “We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind;  <span class="v">21</span>but how he now sees, we don’t know; or who opened his eyes, we don’t know. He is of age. Ask him. He will speak for himself.”  <span class="v">22</span>His parents said these things because they feared the Jews; for the Jews had already agreed that if any man would confess him as Christ, he would be put out of the synagogue.  <span class="v">23</span>Therefore his parents said, “He is of age. Ask him.”  <span class="v">24</span>So they called the man who was blind a second time, and said to him, “Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner.”  <span class="v">25</span>He therefore answered, “I don’t know if he is a sinner. One thing I do know: that though I was blind, now I see.”  <span class="v">26</span>They said to him again, “What did he do to you? How did he open your eyes?”  <span class="v">27</span>He answered them, “I told you already, and you didn’t listen. Why do you want to hear it again? You don’t also want to become his disciples, do you?”  <span class="v">28</span>They insulted him and said, “You are his disciple, but we are disciples of Moses.  <span class="v">29</span>We know that God has spoken to Moses. But as for this man, we don’t know where he comes from.”  <span class="v">30</span>The man answered them, “How amazing! You don’t know where he comes from, yet he opened my eyes.  <span class="v">31</span>We know that God doesn’t listen to sinners, but if anyone is a worshiper of God, and does his will, he listens to him.  <span class="v">32</span>Since the world began it has never been heard of that anyone opened the eyes of someone born blind.  <span class="v">33</span>If this man were not from God, he could do nothing.”  <span class="v">34</span>They answered him, “You were altogether born in sins, and do you teach us?” They threw him out.  <span class="v">35</span>Jesus heard that they had thrown him out, and finding him, he said, “Do you believe in the Son of God?”  <span class="v">36</span>He answered, “Who is he, Lord, that I may believe in him?”  <span class="v">37</span>Jesus said to him, “You have both seen him, and it is he who speaks with you.”  <span class="v">38</span>He said, “Lord, I believe!” and he worshiped him. </p>
