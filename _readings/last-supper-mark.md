<p><span class="v">10</span>Judas Iscariot, who was one of the twelve, went away to the chief priests, that he might deliver him to them.  <span class="v">11</span>They, when they heard it, were glad, and promised to give him money. He sought how he might conveniently deliver him. </p>
<p><span class="v">12</span>On the first day of unleavened bread, when they sacrificed the Passover, his disciples asked him, “Where do you want us to go and prepare that you may eat the Passover?” </p>
<p><span class="v">13</span>He sent two of his disciples, and said to them, “Go into the city, and there you will meet a man carrying a pitcher of water. Follow him,  <span class="v">14</span>and wherever he enters in, tell the master of the house, ‘The Teacher says, “Where is the guest room, where I may eat the Passover with my disciples?”’  <span class="v">15</span>He will himself show you a large upper room furnished and ready. Get ready for us there.” </p>
<p><span class="v">16</span>His disciples went out, and came into the city, and found things as he had said to them, and they prepared the Passover. </p>
<p><span class="v">17</span>When it was evening he came with the twelve.  <span class="v">18</span>As they sat and were eating, Jesus said, “Most certainly I tell you, one of you will betray me—he who eats with me.” </p>
<p><span class="v">19</span>They began to be sorrowful, and to ask him one by one, “Surely not I?” And another said, “Surely not I?” </p>
<p><span class="v">20</span>He answered them, “It is one of the twelve, he who dips with me in the dish.  <span class="v">21</span>For the Son of Man goes, even as it is written about him, but woe to that man by whom the Son of Man is betrayed! It would be better for that man if he had not been born.” </p>
<p><span class="v">22</span>As they were eating, Jesus took bread, and when he had blessed, he broke it, and gave to them, and said, “Take, eat. This is my body.”  <span class="v">23</span>He took the cup, and when he had given thanks, he gave to them. They all drank of it.  <span class="v">24</span>He said to them, “This is my blood of the new covenant, which is poured out for many.  <span class="v">25</span>Most certainly I tell you, I will no more drink of the fruit of the vine, until that day when I drink it anew in God’s Kingdom.” </p>
<p><span class="v">26</span>When they had sung a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.  <span class="v">27</span>Jesus said to them, “All of you will be made to stumble because of me tonight, for it is written, ‘I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered.’  <span class="v">28</span>However, after I am raised up, I will go before you into Galilee.” </p>
<p><span class="v">29</span>But Peter said to him, “Although all will be offended, yet I will not.” </p>
<p><span class="v">30</span>Jesus said to him, “Most certainly I tell you, that you today, even this night, before the rooster crows twice, you will deny me three times.” </p>
<p><span class="v">31</span>But he spoke all the more, “If I must die with you, I will not deny you.” They all said the same thing. </p>
<p><span class="v">32</span>They came to a place which was named Gethsemane. He said to his disciples, “Sit here, while I pray.”  <span class="v">33</span>He took with him Peter, James, and John, and began to be greatly troubled and distressed.  <span class="v">34</span>He said to them, “My soul is exceedingly sorrowful, even to death. Stay here, and watch.” </p>
<p><span class="v">35</span>He went forward a little, and fell on the ground, and prayed that, if it were possible, the hour might pass away from him.  <span class="v">36</span>He said, “Abba, Father, all things are possible to you. Please remove this cup from me. However, not what I desire, but what you desire.” </p>
<p><span class="v">37</span>He came and found them sleeping, and said to Peter, “Simon, are you sleeping? Couldn’t you watch one hour?  <span class="v">38</span>Watch and pray, that you may not enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.” </p>
<p><span class="v">39</span>Again he went away, and prayed, saying the same words.  <span class="v">40</span>Again he returned, and found them sleeping, for their eyes were very heavy, and they didn’t know what to answer him. </p>
<p><span class="v">41</span>He came the third time, and said to them, “Sleep on now, and take your rest. It is enough. The hour has come. Behold, the Son of Man is betrayed into the hands of sinners.  <span class="v">42</span>Arise, let us be going. Behold, he who betrays me is at hand.” </p>
