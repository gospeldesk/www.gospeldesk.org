<p><span class="v">5</span>As some were talking about the temple and how it was decorated with beautiful stones and gifts, he said,  <span class="v">6</span>“As for these things which you see, the days will come, in which there will not be left here one stone on another that will not be thrown down.” </p>
<p><span class="v">7</span>They asked him, “Teacher, so when will these things be? What is the sign that these things are about to happen?” </p>
<p><span class="v">8</span>He said, “Watch out that you don’t get led astray, for many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am he ,’ and, ‘The time is at hand.’ Therefore don’t follow them.  <span class="v">9</span>When you hear of wars and disturbances, don’t be terrified, for these things must happen first, but the end won’t come immediately.” </p>
<p><span class="v">10</span>Then he said to them, “Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom.  <span class="v">11</span>There will be great earthquakes, famines, and plagues in various places. There will be terrors and great signs from heaven.  <span class="v">12</span>But before all these things, they will lay their hands on you and will persecute you, delivering you up to synagogues and prisons, bringing you before kings and governors for my name’s sake.  <span class="v">13</span>It will turn out as a testimony for you.  <span class="v">14</span>Settle it therefore in your hearts not to meditate beforehand how to answer,  <span class="v">15</span>for I will give you a mouth and wisdom which all your adversaries will not be able to withstand or to contradict.  <span class="v">16</span>You will be handed over even by parents, brothers, relatives, and friends. They will cause some of you to be put to death.  <span class="v">17</span>You will be hated by all men for my name’s sake.  <span class="v">18</span>And not a hair of your head will perish.  <span class="v">19</span>“By your endurance you will win your lives. </p>
<p><span class="v">20</span>“But when you see Jerusalem surrounded by armies, then know that its desolation is at hand.  <span class="v">21</span>Then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains. Let those who are in the middle of her depart. Let those who are in the country not enter therein.  <span class="v">22</span>For these are days of vengeance, that all things which are written may be fulfilled.  <span class="v">23</span>Woe to those who are pregnant and to those who nurse infants in those days! For there will be great distress in the land, and wrath to this people.  <span class="v">24</span>They will fall by the edge of the sword, and will be led captive into all the nations. Jerusalem will be trampled down by the Gentiles, until the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled.  <span class="v">25</span>There will be signs in the sun, moon, and stars; and on the earth anxiety of nations, in perplexity for the roaring of the sea and the waves;  <span class="v">26</span>men fainting for fear, and for expectation of the things which are coming on the world: for the powers of the heavens will be shaken.  <span class="v">27</span>Then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.  <span class="v">28</span>But when these things begin to happen, look up, and lift up your heads, because your redemption is near.” </p>
<p><span class="v">29</span>He told them a parable. “See the fig tree, and all the trees.  <span class="v">30</span>When they are already budding, you see it and know by your own selves that the summer is already near.  <span class="v">31</span>Even so you also, when you see these things happening, know that God’s Kingdom is near.  <span class="v">32</span>Most certainly I tell you, this generation will not pass away until all things are accomplished.  <span class="v">33</span>Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will by no means pass away.  <span class="v">33</span>Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will by no means pass away. </p>
<p><span class="v">34</span>“So be careful, or your hearts will be loaded down with carousing, drunkenness, and cares of this life, and that day will come on you suddenly.  <span class="v">35</span>For it will come like a snare on all those who dwell on the surface of all the earth.  <span class="v">36</span>Therefore be watchful all the time, praying that you may be counted worthy to escape all these things that will happen, and to stand before the Son of Man.” </p>
