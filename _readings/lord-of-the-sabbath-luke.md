<p><span class="v">1</span>Now on the second Sabbath after the first, he was going through the grain fields. His disciples plucked the heads of grain and ate, rubbing them in their hands.  <span class="v">2</span>But some of the Pharisees said to them, “Why do you do that which is not lawful to do on the Sabbath day?”  <span class="v">3</span>Jesus, answering them, said, “Haven’t you read what David did when he was hungry, he, and those who were with him;  <span class="v">4</span>how he entered into God’s house, and took and ate the show bread, and gave also to those who were with him, which is not lawful to eat except for the priests alone?”  <span class="v">5</span>He said to them, “The Son of Man is lord of the Sabbath.” </p>
<h4>A Man With a Withered Hand</h4>
<p><span class="v">6</span>It also happened on another Sabbath that he entered into the synagogue and taught. There was a man there, and his right hand was withered.  <span class="v">7</span>The scribes and the Pharisees watched him, to see whether he would heal on the Sabbath, that they might find an accusation against him.  <span class="v">8</span>But he knew their thoughts; and he said to the man who had the withered hand, “Rise up, and stand in the middle.” He arose and stood.  <span class="v">9</span>Then Jesus said to them, “I will ask you something: Is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good, or to do harm? To save a life, or to kill?”  <span class="v">10</span>He looked around at them all, and said to the man, “Stretch out your hand.” He did, and his hand was restored as sound as the other.  <span class="v">11</span>But they were filled with rage, and talked with one another about what they might do to Jesus. </p>
