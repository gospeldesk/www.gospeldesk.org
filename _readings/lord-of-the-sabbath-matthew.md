<p><span class="v">1</span>At that time, Jesus went on the Sabbath day through the grain fields. His disciples were hungry and began to pluck heads of grain and to eat.  <span class="v">2</span>But the Pharisees, when they saw it, said to him, “Behold, your disciples do what is not lawful to do on the Sabbath.” </p>
<p><span class="v">3</span>But he said to them, “Haven’t you read what David did, when he was hungry, and those who were with him;  <span class="v">4</span>how he entered into God’s house, and ate the show bread, which was not lawful for him to eat, neither for those who were with him, but only for the priests?  <span class="v">5</span>Or have you not read in the law, that on the Sabbath day, the priests in the temple profane the Sabbath, and are guiltless?  <span class="v">6</span>But I tell you that one greater than the temple is here.  <span class="v">7</span>But if you had known what this means, ‘I desire mercy, and not sacrifice,’ you wouldn’t have condemned the guiltless.  <span class="v">8</span>For the Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath.” </p>
<h4>A Man With a Withered Hand</h4>
<p><span class="v">9</span>He departed there, and went into their synagogue.  <span class="v">10</span>And behold there was a man with a withered hand. They asked him, “Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath day?” that they might accuse him. </p>
<p><span class="v">11</span>He said to them, “What man is there among you, who has one sheep, and if this one falls into a pit on the Sabbath day, won’t he grab on to it, and lift it out?  <span class="v">12</span>Of how much more value then is a man than a sheep! Therefore it is lawful to do good on the Sabbath day.”  <span class="v">13</span>Then he told the man, “Stretch out your hand.”</p>
<p>He stretched it out; and it was restored whole, just like the other. </p>
