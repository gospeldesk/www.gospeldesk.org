<p><span class="v">14</span>He was casting out a demon, and it was mute. When the demon had gone out, the mute man spoke; and the multitudes marveled.  <span class="v">15</span>But some of them said, “He casts out demons by Beelzebul, the prince of the demons.”  <span class="v">16</span>Others, testing him, sought from him a sign from heaven.  <span class="v">17</span>But he, knowing their thoughts, said to them, “Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation. A house divided against itself falls.  <span class="v">18</span>If Satan also is divided against himself, how will his kingdom stand? For you say that I cast out demons by Beelzebul.  <span class="v">19</span>But if I cast out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your children cast them out? Therefore will they be your judges.  <span class="v">20</span>But if I by God’s finger cast out demons, then God’s Kingdom has come to you.  <span class="v">21</span>“When the strong man, fully armed, guards his own dwelling, his goods are safe.  <span class="v">22</span>But when someone stronger attacks him and overcomes him, he takes from him his whole armor in which he trusted, and divides his plunder.  <span class="v">23</span>“He that is not with me is against me. He who doesn’t gather with me scatters. </p>
