<p><span class="v">34</span>The lamp of the body is the eye. Therefore when your eye is good, your whole body is also full of light; but when it is evil, your body also is full of darkness.  <span class="v">35</span>Therefore see whether the light that is in you isn’t darkness.  <span class="v">36</span>If therefore your whole body is full of light, having no part dark, it will be wholly full of light, as when the lamp with its bright shining gives you light.”  <span class="v">37</span>Now as he spoke, a certain Pharisee asked him to dine with him. He went in, and sat at the table.  <span class="v">38</span>When the Pharisee saw it, he marveled that he had not first washed himself before dinner.  <span class="v">39</span>The Lord said to him, “Now you Pharisees cleanse the outside of the cup and of the platter, but your inward part is full of extortion and wickedness.  <span class="v">40</span>You foolish ones, didn’t he who made the outside make the inside also?  <span class="v">41</span>But give for gifts to the needy those things which are within, and behold, all things will be clean to you. </p>
