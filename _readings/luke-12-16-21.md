<p><span class="v">16</span>He spoke a parable to them, saying, “The ground of a certain rich man produced abundantly.  <span class="v">17</span>He reasoned within himself, saying, ‘What will I do, because I don’t have room to store my crops?’  <span class="v">18</span>He said, ‘This is what I will do. I will pull down my barns, and build bigger ones, and there I will store all my grain and my goods.  <span class="v">19</span>I will tell my soul, “Soul, you have many goods laid up for many years. Take your ease, eat, drink, be merry.”’  <span class="v">20</span>“But God said to him, ‘You foolish one, tonight your soul is required of you. The things which you have prepared—whose will they be?’  <span class="v">21</span>So is he who lays up treasure for himself, and is not rich toward God.” </p>
