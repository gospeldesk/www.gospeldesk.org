<p><span class="v">1</span>Now there were some present at the same time who told him about the Galileans, whose blood Pilate had mixed with their sacrifices.  <span class="v">2</span>Jesus answered them, “Do you think that these Galileans were worse sinners than all the other Galileans, because they suffered such things?  <span class="v">3</span>I tell you, no, but unless you repent, you will all perish in the same way.  <span class="v">4</span>Or those eighteen, on whom the tower in Siloam fell, and killed them; do you think that they were worse offenders than all the men who dwell in Jerusalem?  <span class="v">5</span>I tell you, no, but, unless you repent, you will all perish in the same way.”  <span class="v">6</span>He spoke this parable. “A certain man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard, and he came seeking fruit on it, and found none.  <span class="v">7</span>He said to the vine dresser, ‘Behold, these three years I have come looking for fruit on this fig tree, and found none. Cut it down. Why does it waste the soil?’  <span class="v">8</span>He answered, ‘Lord, leave it alone this year also, until I dig around it, and fertilize it.  <span class="v">9</span>If it bears fruit, fine; but if not, after that, you can cut it down.’” </p>
