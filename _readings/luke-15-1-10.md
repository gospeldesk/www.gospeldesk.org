<p><span class="v">1</span>Now all the tax collectors and sinners were coming close to him to hear him.  <span class="v">2</span>The Pharisees and the scribes murmured, saying, “This man welcomes sinners, and eats with them.”  <span class="v">3</span>He told them this parable.  <span class="v">4</span>“Which of you men, if you had one hundred sheep, and lost one of them, wouldn’t leave the ninety-nine in the wilderness, and go after the one that was lost, until he found it?  <span class="v">5</span>When he has found it, he carries it on his shoulders, rejoicing.  <span class="v">6</span>When he comes home, he calls together his friends and his neighbors, saying to them, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found my sheep which was lost!’  <span class="v">7</span>I tell you that even so there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents, than over ninety-nine righteous people who need no repentance.  <span class="v">8</span>Or what woman, if she had ten drachma coins, if she lost one drachma coin, wouldn’t light a lamp, sweep the house, and seek diligently until she found it?  <span class="v">9</span>When she has found it, she calls together her friends and neighbors, saying, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found the drachma which I had lost.’  <span class="v">10</span>Even so, I tell you, there is joy in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner repenting.” </p>
