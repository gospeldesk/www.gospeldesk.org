<p><span class="v">15</span>They were also bringing their babies to him, that he might touch them. But when the disciples saw it, they rebuked them.  <span class="v">16</span>Jesus summoned them, saying, “Allow the little children to come to me, and don’t hinder them, for God’s Kingdom belongs to such as these.  <span class="v">17</span>Most certainly, I tell you, whoever doesn’t receive God’s Kingdom like a little child, he will in no way enter into it.”  <span class="v">26</span>Those who heard it said, “Then who can be saved?”  <span class="v">27</span>But he said, “The things which are impossible with men are possible with God.”  <span class="v">28</span>Peter said, “Look, we have left everything, and followed you.”  <span class="v">29</span>He said to them, “Most certainly I tell you, there is no one who has left house, or wife, or brothers, or parents, or children, for God’s Kingdom’s sake,  <span class="v">30</span>who will not receive many times more in this time, and in the world to come, eternal life.” </p>
