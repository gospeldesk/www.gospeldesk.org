<p><span class="v">9</span>He spoke also this parable to certain people who were convinced of their own righteousness, and who despised all others.  <span class="v">10</span>“Two men went up into the temple to pray; one was a Pharisee, and the other was a tax collector.  <span class="v">11</span>The Pharisee stood and prayed to himself like this: ‘God, I thank you, that I am not like the rest of men, extortionists, unrighteous, adulterers, or even like this tax collector.  <span class="v">12</span>I fast twice a week. I give tithes of all that I get.’  <span class="v">13</span>But the tax collector, standing far away, wouldn’t even lift up his eyes to heaven, but beat his breast, saying, ‘God, be merciful to me, a sinner!’  <span class="v">14</span>I tell you, this man went down to his house justified rather than the other; for everyone who exalts himself will be humbled, but he who humbles himself will be exalted.” </p>
