<p><span class="v">20</span>The shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things that they had heard and seen, just as it was told them.  <span class="v">21</span>When eight days were fulfilled for the circumcision of the child, his name was called Jesus, which was given by the angel before he was conceived in the womb.  <span class="v">40</span>The child was growing, and was becoming strong in spirit, being filled with wisdom, and the grace of God was upon him.  <span class="v">41</span>His parents went every year to Jerusalem at the feast of the Passover.  <span class="v">42</span>When he was twelve years old, they went up to Jerusalem according to the custom of the feast,  <span class="v">43</span>and when they had fulfilled the days, as they were returning, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem. Joseph and his mother didn’t know it,  <span class="v">44</span>but supposing him to be in the company, they went a day’s journey, and they looked for him among their relatives and acquaintances.  <span class="v">45</span>When they didn’t find him, they returned to Jerusalem, looking for him.  <span class="v">46</span>After three days they found him in the temple, sitting in the middle of the teachers, both listening to them, and asking them questions.  <span class="v">47</span>All who heard him were amazed at his understanding and his answers.  <span class="v">48</span>When they saw him, they were astonished, and his mother said to him, “Son, why have you treated us this way? Behold, your father and I were anxiously looking for you.”  <span class="v">49</span>He said to them, “Why were you looking for me? Didn’t you know that I must be in my Father’s house?”  <span class="v">50</span>They didn’t understand the saying which he spoke to them.  <span class="v">51</span>And he went down with them, and came to Nazareth. He was subject to them, and his mother kept all these sayings in her heart.  <span class="v">52</span>And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men. </p>
