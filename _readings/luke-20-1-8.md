<p><span class="v">1</span>On one of those days, as he was teaching the people in the temple and preaching the Good News, the priests and scribes came to him with the elders.  <span class="v">2</span>They asked him, “Tell us: by what authority do you do these things? Or who is giving you this authority?”  <span class="v">3</span>He answered them, “I also will ask you one question. Tell me:  <span class="v">4</span>the baptism of John, was it from heaven, or from men?”  <span class="v">5</span>They reasoned with themselves, saying, “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ he will say, ‘Why didn’t you believe him?’  <span class="v">6</span>But if we say, ‘From men,’ all the people will stone us, for they are persuaded that John was a prophet.”  <span class="v">7</span>They answered that they didn’t know where it was from.  <span class="v">8</span>Jesus said to them, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.” </p>
