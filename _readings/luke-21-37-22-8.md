<p><span class="v">37</span>Every day Jesus was teaching in the temple, and every night he would go out and spend the night on the mountain that is called Olivet.  <span class="v">38</span>All the people came early in the morning to him in the temple to hear him.  <span class="v">1</span>Now the feast of unleavened bread, which is called the Passover, was approaching.  <span class="v">2</span>The chief priests and the scribes sought how they might put him to death, for they feared the people.  <span class="v">3</span>Satan entered into Judas, who was also called Iscariot, who was counted with the twelve.  <span class="v">4</span>He went away, and talked with the chief priests and captains about how he might deliver him to them.  <span class="v">5</span>They were glad, and agreed to give him money.  <span class="v">6</span>He consented, and sought an opportunity to deliver him to them in the absence of the multitude.  <span class="v">7</span>The day of unleavened bread came, on which the Passover must be sacrificed.  <span class="v">8</span>Jesus sent Peter and John, saying, “Go and prepare the Passover for us, that we may eat.” </p>
