<p><span class="v">8</span>He said, “Watch out that you don’t get led astray, for many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am he ,’ and, ‘The time is at hand.’ Therefore don’t follow them.  <span class="v">9</span>When you hear of wars and disturbances, don’t be terrified, for these things must happen first, but the end won’t come immediately.”  <span class="v">25</span>There will be signs in the sun, moon, and stars; and on the earth anxiety of nations, in perplexity for the roaring of the sea and the waves;  <span class="v">26</span>men fainting for fear, and for expectation of the things which are coming on the world: for the powers of the heavens will be shaken.  <span class="v">27</span>Then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.  <span class="v">33</span>Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will by no means pass away.  <span class="v">34</span>“So be careful, or your hearts will be loaded down with carousing, drunkenness, and cares of this life, and that day will come on you suddenly.  <span class="v">35</span>For it will come like a snare on all those who dwell on the surface of all the earth.  <span class="v">36</span>Therefore be watchful all the time, praying that you may be counted worthy to escape all these things that will happen, and to stand before the Son of Man.” </p>
