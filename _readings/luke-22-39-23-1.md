<p><span class="v">39</span>He came out, and went, as his custom was, to the Mount of Olives. His disciples also followed him.  <span class="v">40</span>When he was at the place, he said to them, “Pray that you don’t enter into temptation.”  <span class="v">41</span>He was withdrawn from them about a stone’s throw, and he knelt down and prayed,  <span class="v">42</span>saying, “Father, if you are willing, remove this cup from me. Nevertheless, not my will, but yours, be done.”  <span class="v">43</span>An angel from heaven appeared to him, strengthening him.  <span class="v">44</span>Being in agony he prayed more earnestly. His sweat became like great drops of blood falling down on the ground.  <span class="v">45</span>When he rose up from his prayer, he came to the disciples, and found them sleeping because of grief,  <span class="v">46</span>and said to them, “Why do you sleep? Rise and pray that you may not enter into temptation.”  <span class="v">47</span>While he was still speaking, behold, a multitude, and he who was called Judas, one of the twelve, was leading them. He came near to Jesus to kiss him.  <span class="v">48</span>But Jesus said to him, “Judas, do you betray the Son of Man with a kiss?”  <span class="v">49</span>When those who were around him saw what was about to happen, they said to him, “Lord, shall we strike with the sword?”  <span class="v">50</span>A certain one of them struck the servant of the high priest, and cut off his right ear.  <span class="v">51</span>But Jesus answered, “Let me at least do this” —and he touched his ear, and healed him.  <span class="v">52</span>Jesus said to the chief priests, captains of the temple, and elders, who had come against him, “Have you come out as against a robber, with swords and clubs?  <span class="v">53</span>When I was with you in the temple daily, you didn’t stretch out your hands against me. But this is your hour, and the power of darkness.”  <span class="v">54</span>They seized him, and led him away, and brought him into the high priest’s house. But Peter followed from a distance.  <span class="v">55</span>When they had kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard, and had sat down together, Peter sat among them.  <span class="v">56</span>A certain servant girl saw him as he sat in the light, and looking intently at him, said, “This man also was with him.”  <span class="v">57</span>He denied Jesus, saying, “Woman, I don’t know him.”  <span class="v">58</span>After a little while someone else saw him, and said, “You also are one of them!” But Peter answered, “Man, I am not!”  <span class="v">59</span>After about one hour passed, another confidently affirmed, saying, “Truly this man also was with him, for he is a Galilean!”  <span class="v">60</span>But Peter said, “Man, I don’t know what you are talking about!” Immediately, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.  <span class="v">61</span>The Lord turned, and looked at Peter. Then Peter remembered the Lord’s word, how he said to him, “Before the rooster crows you will deny me three times.”  <span class="v">62</span>He went out, and wept bitterly.  <span class="v">63</span>The men who held Jesus mocked him and beat him.  <span class="v">64</span>Having blindfolded him, they struck him on the face and asked him, “Prophesy! Who is the one who struck you?”  <span class="v">65</span>They spoke many other things against him, insulting him.  <span class="v">66</span>As soon as it was day, the assembly of the elders of the people were gathered together, both chief priests and scribes, and they led him away into their council, saying,  <span class="v">67</span>“If you are the Christ, tell us.” But he said to them, “If I tell you, you won’t believe,  <span class="v">68</span>and if I ask, you will in no way answer me or let me go.  <span class="v">69</span>From now on, the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the power of God.”  <span class="v">70</span>They all said, “Are you then the Son of God?” He said to them, “You say it, because I am.”  <span class="v">71</span>They said, “Why do we need any more witness? For we ourselves have heard from his own mouth!”  <span class="v">1</span>The whole company of them rose up and brought him before Pilate. </p>
