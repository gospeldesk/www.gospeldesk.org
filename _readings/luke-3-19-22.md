<p><span class="v">19</span>but Herod the tetrarch, being reproved by him for Herodias, his brother’s wife, and for all the evil things which Herod had done,  <span class="v">20</span>added this also to them all, that he shut up John in prison.  <span class="v">21</span>Now when all the people were baptized, Jesus also had been baptized, and was praying. The sky was opened,  <span class="v">22</span>and the Holy Spirit descended in a bodily form like a dove on him; and a voice came out of the sky, saying “You are my beloved Son. In you I am well pleased.” </p>
