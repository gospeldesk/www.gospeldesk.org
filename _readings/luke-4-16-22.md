<p><span class="v">16</span>He came to Nazareth, where he had been brought up. He entered, as was his custom, into the synagogue on the Sabbath day, and stood up to read.  <span class="v">17</span>The book of the prophet Isaiah was handed to him. He opened the book, and found the place where it was written,  <span class="v">18</span>“The Spirit of the Lord is on me, because he has anointed me to preach good news to the poor. He has sent me to heal the broken hearted, to proclaim release to the captives, recovering of sight to the blind, to deliver those who are crushed,  <span class="v">19</span>and to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord.”  <span class="v">20</span>He closed the book, gave it back to the attendant, and sat down. The eyes of all in the synagogue were fastened on him.  <span class="v">21</span>He began to tell them, “Today, this Scripture has been fulfilled in your hearing.”  <span class="v">22</span>All testified about him, and wondered at the gracious words which proceeded out of his mouth, and they said, “Isn’t this Joseph’s son?” </p>
