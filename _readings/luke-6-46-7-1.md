<p><span class="v">46</span>“Why do you call me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ and don’t do the things which I say?  <span class="v">47</span>Everyone who comes to me, and hears my words, and does them, I will show you who he is like.  <span class="v">48</span>He is like a man building a house, who dug and went deep, and laid a foundation on the rock. When a flood arose, the stream broke against that house, and could not shake it, because it was founded on the rock.  <span class="v">49</span>But he who hears, and doesn’t do, is like a man who built a house on the earth without a foundation, against which the stream broke, and immediately it fell, and the ruin of that house was great.”  <span class="v">1</span>After he had finished speaking in the hearing of the people, he entered into Capernaum. </p>
