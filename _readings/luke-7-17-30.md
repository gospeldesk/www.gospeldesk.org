<p><span class="v">17</span>This report went out concerning him in the whole of Judea, and in all the surrounding region.  <span class="v">18</span>The disciples of John told him about all these things.  <span class="v">19</span>John, calling to himself two of his disciples, sent them to Jesus, saying, “Are you the one who is coming, or should we look for another?”  <span class="v">20</span>When the men had come to him, they said, “John the Baptizer has sent us to you, saying, ‘Are you he who comes, or should we look for another?’”  <span class="v">21</span>In that hour he cured many of diseases and plagues and evil spirits; and to many who were blind he gave sight.  <span class="v">22</span>Jesus answered them, “Go and tell John the things which you have seen and heard: that the blind receive their sight, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have good news preached to them.  <span class="v">23</span>Blessed is he who finds no occasion for stumbling in me.”  <span class="v">24</span>When John’s messengers had departed, he began to tell the multitudes about John, “What did you go out into the wilderness to see? A reed shaken by the wind?  <span class="v">25</span>But what did you go out to see? A man clothed in soft clothing? Behold, those who are gorgeously dressed, and live delicately, are in kings’ courts.  <span class="v">26</span>But what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I tell you, and much more than a prophet.  <span class="v">27</span>This is he of whom it is written, ‘Behold, I send my messenger before your face, who will prepare your way before you.’  <span class="v">28</span>“For I tell you, among those who are born of women there is not a greater prophet than John the Baptizer, yet he who is least in God’s Kingdom is greater than he.”  <span class="v">29</span>When all the people and the tax collectors heard this, they declared God to be just, having been baptized with John’s baptism.  <span class="v">30</span>But the Pharisees and the lawyers rejected the counsel of God, not being baptized by him themselves. </p>
