<p><span class="v">18</span>As he was praying alone, the disciples were with him, and he asked them, “Who do the multitudes say that I am?”  <span class="v">19</span>They answered, “‘John the Baptizer,’ but others say, ‘Elijah,’ and others, that one of the old prophets is risen again.”  <span class="v">20</span>He said to them, “But who do you say that I am?” Peter answered, “The Christ of God.”  <span class="v">21</span>But he warned them, and commanded them to tell this to no one,  <span class="v">22</span>saying, “The Son of Man must suffer many things, and be rejected by the elders, chief priests, and scribes, and be killed, and the third day be raised up.” </p>
