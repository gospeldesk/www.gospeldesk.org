Bring your son here.”  <span class="v">42</span>While he was still coming, the demon threw him down and convulsed him violently. But Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit, and healed the boy, and gave him back to his father.  <span class="v">43</span>They were all astonished at the majesty of God.
