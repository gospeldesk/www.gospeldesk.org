<p><span class="v">2</span>Pharisees came to him testing him, and asked him, “Is it lawful for a man to divorce his wife?”  <span class="v">3</span>He answered, “What did Moses command you?”  <span class="v">4</span>They said, “Moses allowed a certificate of divorce to be written, and to divorce her.”  <span class="v">5</span>But Jesus said to them, “For your hardness of heart, he wrote you this commandment.  <span class="v">6</span>But from the beginning of the creation, God made them male and female.  <span class="v">7</span>For this cause a man will leave his father and mother, and will join to his wife,  <span class="v">8</span>and the two will become one flesh, so that they are no longer two, but one flesh.  <span class="v">9</span>What therefore God has joined together, let no man separate.”  <span class="v">10</span>In the house, his disciples asked him again about the same matter.  <span class="v">11</span>He said to them, “Whoever divorces his wife, and marries another, commits adultery against her.  <span class="v">12</span>If a woman herself divorces her husband, and marries another, she commits adultery.” </p>
