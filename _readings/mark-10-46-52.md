<p><span class="v">46</span>They came to Jericho. As he went out from Jericho, with his disciples and a great multitude, the son of Timaeus, Bartimaeus, a blind beggar, was sitting by the road.  <span class="v">47</span>When he heard that it was Jesus the Nazarene, he began to cry out, and say, “Jesus, you son of David, have mercy on me!”  <span class="v">48</span>Many rebuked him, that he should be quiet, but he cried out much more, “You son of David, have mercy on me!”  <span class="v">49</span>Jesus stood still, and said, “Call him.” They called the blind man, saying to him, “Cheer up! Get up. He is calling you!”  <span class="v">50</span>He, casting away his cloak, sprang up, and came to Jesus.  <span class="v">51</span>Jesus asked him, “What do you want me to do for you?” The blind man said to him, “Rabboni, that I may see again.”  <span class="v">52</span>Jesus said to him, “Go your way. Your faith has made you well.” Immediately he received his sight, and followed Jesus on the way. </p>
