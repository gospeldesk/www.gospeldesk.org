<p><span class="v">38</span>In his teaching he said to them, “Beware of the scribes, who like to walk in long robes, and to get greetings in the marketplaces,  <span class="v">39</span>and the best seats in the synagogues, and the best places at feasts:  <span class="v">40</span>those who devour widows’ houses, and for a pretense make long prayers. These will receive greater condemnation.”  <span class="v">41</span>Jesus sat down opposite the treasury, and saw how the multitude cast money into the treasury. Many who were rich cast in much.  <span class="v">42</span>A poor widow came, and she cast in two small brass coins, which equal a quadrans coin.  <span class="v">43</span>He called his disciples to himself, and said to them, “Most certainly I tell you, this poor widow gave more than all those who are giving into the treasury,  <span class="v">44</span>for they all gave out of their abundance, but she, out of her poverty, gave all that she had to live on.” </p>
