<p><span class="v">1</span>As he went out of the temple, one of his disciples said to him, “Teacher, see what kind of stones and what kind of buildings!”  <span class="v">2</span>Jesus said to him, “Do you see these great buildings? There will not be left here one stone on another, which will not be thrown down.”  <span class="v">3</span>As he sat on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew asked him privately,  <span class="v">4</span>“Tell us, when will these things be? What is the sign that these things are all about to be fulfilled?”  <span class="v">5</span>Jesus, answering, began to tell them, “Be careful that no one leads you astray.  <span class="v">6</span>For many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am he!’ and will lead many astray.  <span class="v">7</span>“When you hear of wars and rumors of wars, don’t be troubled. For those must happen, but the end is not yet.  <span class="v">8</span>For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. There will be earthquakes in various places. There will be famines and troubles. These things are the beginning of birth pains. </p>
