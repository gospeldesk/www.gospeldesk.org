<p><span class="v">24</span>But in those days, after that oppression, the sun will be darkened, the moon will not give its light,  <span class="v">25</span>the stars will be falling from the sky, and the powers that are in the heavens will be shaken.  <span class="v">26</span>Then they will see the Son of Man coming in clouds with great power and glory.  <span class="v">27</span>Then he will send out his angels, and will gather together his chosen ones from the four winds, from the ends of the earth to the ends of the sky.  <span class="v">28</span>“Now from the fig tree, learn this parable. When the branch has now become tender, and produces its leaves, you know that the summer is near;  <span class="v">29</span>even so you also, when you see these things coming to pass, know that it is near, at the doors.  <span class="v">30</span>Most certainly I say to you, this generation will not pass away until all these things happen.  <span class="v">31</span>Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away. </p>
