<p><span class="v">43</span>Immediately, while he was still speaking, Judas, one of the twelve, came—and with him a multitude with swords and clubs, from the chief priests, the scribes, and the elders.  <span class="v">44</span>Now he who betrayed him had given them a sign, saying, “Whomever I will kiss, that is he. Seize him, and lead him away safely.”  <span class="v">45</span>When he had come, immediately he came to him, and said, “Rabbi! Rabbi!” and kissed him.  <span class="v">46</span>They laid their hands on him, and seized him.  <span class="v">47</span>But a certain one of those who stood by drew his sword, and struck the servant of the high priest, and cut off his ear.  <span class="v">48</span>Jesus answered them, “Have you come out, as against a robber, with swords and clubs to seize me?  <span class="v">49</span>I was daily with you in the temple teaching, and you didn’t arrest me. But this is so that the Scriptures might be fulfilled.”  <span class="v">50</span>They all left him, and fled.  <span class="v">51</span>A certain young man followed him, having a linen cloth thrown around himself over his naked body. The young men grabbed him,  <span class="v">52</span>but he left the linen cloth, and fled from them naked.  <span class="v">53</span>They led Jesus away to the high priest. All the chief priests, the elders, and the scribes came together with him.  <span class="v">54</span>Peter had followed him from a distance, until he came into the court of the high priest. He was sitting with the officers, and warming himself in the light of the fire.  <span class="v">55</span>Now the chief priests and the whole council sought witnesses against Jesus to put him to death, and found none.  <span class="v">56</span>For many gave false testimony against him, and their testimony didn’t agree with each other.  <span class="v">57</span>Some stood up, and gave false testimony against him, saying,  <span class="v">58</span>“We heard him say, ‘I will destroy this temple that is made with hands, and in three days I will build another made without hands.’”  <span class="v">59</span>Even so, their testimony did not agree.  <span class="v">60</span>The high priest stood up in the middle, and asked Jesus, “Have you no answer? What is it which these testify against you?”  <span class="v">61</span>But he stayed quiet, and answered nothing. Again the high priest asked him, “Are you the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?”  <span class="v">62</span>Jesus said, “I am. You will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power, and coming with the clouds of the sky.”  <span class="v">63</span>The high priest tore his clothes, and said, “What further need have we of witnesses?  <span class="v">64</span>You have heard the blasphemy! What do you think?” They all condemned him to be worthy of death.  <span class="v">65</span>Some began to spit on him, and to cover his face, and to beat him with fists, and to tell him, “Prophesy!” The officers struck him with the palms of their hands.  <span class="v">66</span>As Peter was in the courtyard below, one of the maids of the high priest came,  <span class="v">67</span>and seeing Peter warming himself, she looked at him, and said, “You were also with the Nazarene, Jesus!”  <span class="v">68</span>But he denied it, saying, “I neither know, nor understand what you are saying.” He went out on the porch, and the rooster crowed.  <span class="v">69</span>The maid saw him, and began again to tell those who stood by, “This is one of them.”  <span class="v">70</span>But he again denied it. After a little while again those who stood by said to Peter, “You truly are one of them, for you are a Galilean, and your speech shows it.”  <span class="v">71</span>But he began to curse, and to swear, “I don’t know this man of whom you speak!”  <span class="v">72</span>The rooster crowed the second time. Peter remembered the word, how that Jesus said to him, “Before the rooster crows twice, you will deny me three times.” When he thought about that, he wept.  <span class="v">1</span>Immediately in the morning the chief priests, with the elders and scribes, and the whole council, held a consultation, and bound Jesus, and carried him away, and delivered him up to Pilate. </p>
