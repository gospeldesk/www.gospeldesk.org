<p><span class="v">22</span>They brought him to the place called Golgotha, which is, being interpreted, “The place of a skull.”  <span class="v">25</span>It was the third hour, and they crucified him.  <span class="v">33</span>When the sixth hour had come, there was darkness over the whole land until the ninth hour.  <span class="v">34</span>At the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, “Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani?” which is, being interpreted, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?”  <span class="v">35</span>Some of those who stood by, when they heard it, said, “Behold, he is calling Elijah.”  <span class="v">36</span>One ran, and filling a sponge full of vinegar, put it on a reed, and gave it to him to drink, saying, “Let him be. Let’s see whether Elijah comes to take him down.”  <span class="v">37</span>Jesus cried out with a loud voice, and gave up the spirit.  <span class="v">38</span>The veil of the temple was torn in two from the top to the bottom.  <span class="v">39</span>When the centurion, who stood by opposite him, saw that he cried out like this and breathed his last, he said, “Truly this man was the Son of God!”  <span class="v">40</span>There were also women watching from afar, among whom were both Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the less and of Joses, and Salome;  <span class="v">41</span>who, when he was in Galilee, followed him, and served him; and many other women who came up with him to Jerusalem. </p>
