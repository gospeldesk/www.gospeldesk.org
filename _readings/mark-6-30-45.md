<p><span class="v">30</span>The apostles gathered themselves together to Jesus, and they told him all things, whatever they had done, and whatever they had taught.  <span class="v">31</span>He said to them, “You come apart into a deserted place, and rest awhile.” For there were many coming and going, and they had no leisure so much as to eat.  <span class="v">32</span>They went away in the boat to a deserted place by themselves.  <span class="v">33</span>They saw them going, and many recognized him and ran there on foot from all the cities. They arrived before them and came together to him.  <span class="v">34</span>Jesus came out, saw a great multitude, and he had compassion on them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd, and he began to teach them many things.  <span class="v">35</span>When it was late in the day, his disciples came to him, and said, “This place is deserted, and it is late in the day.  <span class="v">36</span>Send them away, that they may go into the surrounding country and villages, and buy themselves bread, for they have nothing to eat.”  <span class="v">37</span>But he answered them, “You give them something to eat.” They asked him, “Shall we go and buy two hundred denarii worth of bread, and give them something to eat?”  <span class="v">38</span>He said to them, “How many loaves do you have? Go see.” When they knew, they said, “Five, and two fish.”  <span class="v">39</span>He commanded them that everyone should sit down in groups on the green grass.  <span class="v">40</span>They sat down in ranks, by hundreds and by fifties.  <span class="v">41</span>He took the five loaves and the two fish, and looking up to heaven, he blessed and broke the loaves, and he gave to his disciples to set before them, and he divided the two fish among them all.  <span class="v">42</span>They all ate, and were filled.  <span class="v">43</span>They took up twelve baskets full of broken pieces and also of the fish.  <span class="v">44</span>Those who ate the loaves were five thousand men.  <span class="v">45</span>Immediately he made his disciples get into the boat, and to go ahead to the other side, to Bethsaida, while he himself sent the multitude away. </p>
