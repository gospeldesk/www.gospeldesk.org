<p><span class="v">2</span>After six days Jesus took with him Peter, James, and John, and brought them up onto a high mountain privately by themselves, and he was changed into another form in front of them.  <span class="v">3</span>His clothing became glistening, exceedingly white, like snow, such as no launderer on earth can whiten them.  <span class="v">4</span>Elijah and Moses appeared to them, and they were talking with Jesus.  <span class="v">5</span>Peter answered Jesus, “Rabbi, it is good for us to be here. Let’s make three tents: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”  <span class="v">6</span>For he didn’t know what to say, for they were very afraid.  <span class="v">7</span>A cloud came, overshadowing them, and a voice came out of the cloud, “This is my beloved Son. Listen to him.”  <span class="v">8</span>Suddenly looking around, they saw no one with them any more, except Jesus only.  <span class="v">9</span>As they were coming down from the mountain, he commanded them that they should tell no one what things they had seen, until after the Son of Man had risen from the dead. </p>
