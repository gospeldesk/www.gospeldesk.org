<p><span class="v">2</span>Now when John heard in the prison the works of Christ, he sent two of his disciples  <span class="v">3</span>and said to him, “Are you he who comes, or should we look for another?”  <span class="v">4</span>Jesus answered them, “Go and tell John the things which you hear and see:  <span class="v">5</span>the blind receive their sight, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised up, and the poor have good news preached to them.  <span class="v">6</span>Blessed is he who finds no occasion for stumbling in me.”  <span class="v">7</span>As these went their way, Jesus began to say to the multitudes concerning John, “What did you go out into the wilderness to see? A reed shaken by the wind?  <span class="v">8</span>But what did you go out to see? A man in soft clothing? Behold, those who wear soft clothing are in kings’ houses.  <span class="v">9</span>But why did you go out? To see a prophet? Yes, I tell you, and much more than a prophet.  <span class="v">10</span>For this is he, of whom it is written, ‘Behold, I send my messenger before your face, who will prepare your way before you.’  <span class="v">11</span>Most certainly I tell you, among those who are born of women there has not arisen anyone greater than John the Baptizer; yet he who is least in the Kingdom of Heaven is greater than he.  <span class="v">12</span>From the days of John the Baptizer until now, the Kingdom of Heaven suffers violence, and the violent take it by force.  <span class="v">13</span>For all the prophets and the law prophesied until John.  <span class="v">14</span>If you are willing to receive it, this is Elijah, who is to come.  <span class="v">15</span>He who has ears to hear, let him hear. </p>
