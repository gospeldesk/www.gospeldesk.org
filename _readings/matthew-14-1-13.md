<p><span class="v">1</span>At that time, Herod the tetrarch heard the report concerning Jesus,  <span class="v">2</span>and said to his servants, “This is John the Baptizer. He is risen from the dead. That is why these powers work in him.”  <span class="v">3</span>For Herod had laid hold of John, and bound him, and put him in prison for the sake of Herodias, his brother Philip’s wife.  <span class="v">4</span>For John said to him, “It is not lawful for you to have her.”  <span class="v">5</span>When he would have put him to death, he feared the multitude, because they counted him as a prophet.  <span class="v">6</span>But when Herod’s birthday came, the daughter of Herodias danced among them and pleased Herod.  <span class="v">7</span>Whereupon he promised with an oath to give her whatever she should ask.  <span class="v">8</span>She, being prompted by her mother, said, “Give me here on a platter the head of John the Baptizer.”  <span class="v">9</span>The king was grieved, but for the sake of his oaths, and of those who sat at the table with him, he commanded it to be given,  <span class="v">10</span>and he sent and beheaded John in the prison.  <span class="v">11</span>His head was brought on a platter, and given to the young lady: and she brought it to her mother.  <span class="v">12</span>His disciples came, and took the body, and buried it; and they went and told Jesus.  <span class="v">13</span>Now when Jesus heard this, he withdrew from there in a boat, to a deserted place apart. When the multitudes heard it, they followed him on foot from the cities. </p>
