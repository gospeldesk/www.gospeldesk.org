<p><span class="v">21</span>Jesus went out from there, and withdrew into the region of Tyre and Sidon.  <span class="v">22</span>Behold, a Canaanite woman came out from those borders, and cried, saying, “Have mercy on me, Lord, you son of David! My daughter is severely possessed by a demon!”  <span class="v">23</span>But he answered her not a word. His disciples came and begged him, saying, “Send her away; for she cries after us.”  <span class="v">24</span>But he answered, “I wasn’t sent to anyone but the lost sheep of the house of Israel.”  <span class="v">25</span>But she came and worshiped him, saying, “Lord, help me.”  <span class="v">26</span>But he answered, “It is not appropriate to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs.”  <span class="v">27</span>But she said, “Yes, Lord, but even the dogs eat the crumbs which fall from their masters’ table.”  <span class="v">28</span>Then Jesus answered her, “Woman, great is your faith! Be it done to you even as you desire.” And her daughter was healed from that hour. </p>
