<p><span class="v">1</span>After six days, Jesus took with him Peter, James, and John his brother, and brought them up into a high mountain by themselves.  <span class="v">2</span>He was transfigured before them. His face shone like the sun, and his garments became as white as the light.  <span class="v">3</span>Behold, Moses and Elijah appeared to them talking with him.  <span class="v">4</span>Peter answered, and said to Jesus, “Lord, it is good for us to be here. If you want, let’s make three tents here: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”  <span class="v">5</span>While he was still speaking, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them. Behold, a voice came out of the cloud, saying, “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. Listen to him.”  <span class="v">6</span>When the disciples heard it, they fell on their faces, and were very afraid.  <span class="v">7</span>Jesus came and touched them and said, “Get up, and don’t be afraid.”  <span class="v">8</span>Lifting up their eyes, they saw no one, except Jesus alone.  <span class="v">9</span>As they were coming down from the mountain, Jesus commanded them, saying, “Don’t tell anyone what you saw, until the Son of Man has risen from the dead.” </p>
