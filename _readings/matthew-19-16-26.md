<p><span class="v">16</span>Behold, one came to him and said, “Good teacher, what good thing shall I do, that I may have eternal life?”  <span class="v">17</span>He said to him, “Why do you call me good? No one is good but one, that is, God. But if you want to enter into life, keep the commandments.”  <span class="v">18</span>He said to him, “Which ones?” Jesus said, “‘You shall not murder.’ ‘You shall not commit adultery.’ ‘You shall not steal.’ ‘You shall not offer false testimony.’  <span class="v">19</span>‘Honor your father and your mother.’ And, ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’”  <span class="v">20</span>The young man said to him, “All these things I have observed from my youth. What do I still lack?”  <span class="v">21</span>Jesus said to him, “If you want to be perfect, go, sell what you have, and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven; and come, follow me.”  <span class="v">22</span>But when the young man heard the saying, he went away sad, for he was one who had great possessions.  <span class="v">23</span>Jesus said to his disciples, “Most certainly I say to you, a rich man will enter into the Kingdom of Heaven with difficulty.  <span class="v">24</span>Again I tell you, it is easier for a camel to go through a needle’s eye, than for a rich man to enter into God’s Kingdom.”  <span class="v">25</span>When the disciples heard it, they were exceedingly astonished, saying, “Who then can be saved?”  <span class="v">26</span>Looking at them, Jesus said, “With men this is impossible, but with God all things are possible.” </p>
