<p><span class="v">35</span>One of them, a lawyer, asked him a question, testing him.  <span class="v">36</span>“Teacher, which is the greatest commandment in the law?”  <span class="v">37</span>Jesus said to him, “‘You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.’  <span class="v">38</span>This is the first and great commandment.  <span class="v">39</span>A second likewise is this, ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’  <span class="v">40</span>The whole law and the prophets depend on these two commandments.”  <span class="v">41</span>Now while the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them a question,  <span class="v">42</span>saying, “What do you think of the Christ? Whose son is he?” They said to him, “Of David.”  <span class="v">43</span>He said to them, “How then does David in the Spirit call him Lord, saying,  <span class="v">44</span>‘The Lord said to my Lord, sit on my right hand, until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet?’  <span class="v">45</span>“If then David calls him Lord, how is he his son?”  <span class="v">46</span>No one was able to answer him a word, neither did any man dare ask him any more questions from that day forward. </p>
