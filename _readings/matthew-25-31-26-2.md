<p><span class="v">31</span>“But when the Son of Man comes in his glory, and all the holy angels with him, then he will sit on the throne of his glory.  <span class="v">32</span>Before him all the nations will be gathered, and he will separate them one from another, as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats.  <span class="v">33</span>He will set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on the left.  <span class="v">34</span>Then the King will tell those on his right hand, ‘Come, blessed of my Father, inherit the Kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world;  <span class="v">35</span>for I was hungry, and you gave me food to eat. I was thirsty, and you gave me drink. I was a stranger, and you took me in.  <span class="v">36</span>I was naked, and you clothed me. I was sick, and you visited me. I was in prison, and you came to me.’  <span class="v">37</span>“Then the righteous will answer him, saying, ‘Lord, when did we see you hungry, and feed you; or thirsty, and give you a drink?  <span class="v">38</span>When did we see you as a stranger, and take you in; or naked, and clothe you?  <span class="v">39</span>When did we see you sick, or in prison, and come to you?’  <span class="v">40</span>“The King will answer them, ‘Most certainly I tell you, because you did it to one of the least of these my brothers, you did it to me.’  <span class="v">41</span>Then he will say also to those on the left hand, ‘Depart from me, you cursed, into the eternal fire which is prepared for the devil and his angels;  <span class="v">42</span>for I was hungry, and you didn’t give me food to eat; I was thirsty, and you gave me no drink;  <span class="v">43</span>I was a stranger, and you didn’t take me in; naked, and you didn’t clothe me; sick, and in prison, and you didn’t visit me.’  <span class="v">44</span>“Then they will also answer, saying, ‘Lord, when did we see you hungry, or thirsty, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and didn’t help you?’  <span class="v">45</span>“Then he will answer them, saying, ‘Most certainly I tell you, because you didn’t do it to one of the least of these, you didn’t do it to me.’  <span class="v">46</span>These will go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life.”  <span class="v">1</span>When Jesus had finished all these words, he said to his disciples,  <span class="v">2</span>“You know that after two days the Passover is coming, and the Son of Man will be delivered up to be crucified.” </p>
