<p><span class="v">1</span>He entered into a boat, and crossed over, and came into his own city.  <span class="v">2</span>Behold, they brought to him a man who was paralyzed, lying on a bed. Jesus, seeing their faith, said to the paralytic, “Son, cheer up! Your sins are forgiven you.”  <span class="v">3</span>Behold, some of the scribes said to themselves, “This man blasphemes.”  <span class="v">4</span>Jesus, knowing their thoughts, said, “Why do you think evil in your hearts?  <span class="v">5</span>For which is easier, to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven;’ or to say, ‘Get up, and walk?’  <span class="v">6</span>But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins-” (then he said to the paralytic), “Get up, and take up your mat, and go to your house.”  <span class="v">7</span>He arose and departed to his house.  <span class="v">8</span>But when the multitudes saw it, they marveled and glorified God, who had given such authority to men. </p>
