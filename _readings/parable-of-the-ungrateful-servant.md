<p><span class="v">23</span>“Therefore the Kingdom of Heaven is like a certain king, who wanted to reconcile accounts with his servants.  <span class="v">24</span>When he had begun to reconcile, one was brought to him who owed him ten thousand talents.  <span class="v">25</span>But because he couldn’t pay, his lord commanded him to be sold, with his wife, his children, and all that he had, and payment to be made.  <span class="v">26</span>The servant therefore fell down and knelt before him, saying, ‘Lord, have patience with me, and I will repay you all!’  <span class="v">27</span>The lord of that servant, being moved with compassion, released him, and forgave him the debt. </p>
<p><span class="v">28</span>“But that servant went out, and found one of his fellow servants, who owed him one hundred denarii, and he grabbed him, and took him by the throat, saying, ‘Pay me what you owe!’ </p>
<p><span class="v">29</span>“So his fellow servant fell down at his feet and begged him, saying, ‘Have patience with me, and I will repay you!’  <span class="v">30</span>He would not, but went and cast him into prison, until he should pay back that which was due.  <span class="v">31</span>So when his fellow servants saw what was done, they were exceedingly sorry, and came and told their lord all that was done.  <span class="v">32</span>Then his lord called him in, and said to him, ‘You wicked servant! I forgave you all that debt, because you begged me.  <span class="v">33</span>Shouldn’t you also have had mercy on your fellow servant, even as I had mercy on you?’  <span class="v">34</span>His lord was angry, and delivered him to the tormentors, until he should pay all that was due to him.  <span class="v">35</span>So my heavenly Father will also do to you, if you don’t each forgive your brother from your hearts for his misdeeds.” </p>
