<p><span class="v">44</span>“Again, the Kingdom of Heaven is like treasure hidden in the field, which a man found, and hid. In his joy, he goes and sells all that he has, and buys that field. </p>
<p><span class="v">45</span>“Again, the Kingdom of Heaven is like a man who is a merchant seeking fine pearls,  <span class="v">46</span>who having found one pearl of great price, he went and sold all that he had, and bought it. </p>
<p><span class="v">47</span>“Again, the Kingdom of Heaven is like a dragnet, that was cast into the sea, and gathered some fish of every kind,  <span class="v">48</span>which, when it was filled, they drew up on the beach. They sat down, and gathered the good into containers, but the bad they threw away.  <span class="v">49</span>So will it be in the end of the world. The angels will come and separate the wicked from among the righteous,  <span class="v">50</span>and will cast them into the furnace of fire. There will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth.” </p>
<p><span class="v">51</span>Jesus said to them, “Have you understood all these things?”</p>
<p>They answered him, “Yes, Lord.” </p>
<p><span class="v">52</span>He said to them, “Therefore every scribe who has been made a disciple in the Kingdom of Heaven is like a man who is a householder, who brings out of his treasure new and old things.” </p>
<p><span class="v">53</span>When Jesus had finished these parables, he departed from there.  <span class="v">54</span>Coming into his own country, he taught them in their synagogue, so that they were astonished, and said, “Where did this man get this wisdom, and these mighty works? </p>
