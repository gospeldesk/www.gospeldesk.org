<p><span class="v">1</span>When he finished praying in a certain place, one of his disciples said to him, “Lord, teach us to pray, just as John also taught his disciples.” </p>
<p><span class="v">2</span>He said to them, “When you pray, say, ‘Our Father in heaven, may your name be kept holy. May your Kingdom come. May your will be done on earth, as it is in heaven.  <span class="v">3</span>Give us day by day our daily bread.  <span class="v">4</span>Forgive us our sins, for we ourselves also forgive everyone who is indebted to us. Bring us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one.’” </p>
<p><span class="v">5</span>He said to them, “Which of you, if you go to a friend at midnight, and tell him, ‘Friend, lend me three loaves of bread,  <span class="v">6</span>for a friend of mine has come to me from a journey, and I have nothing to set before him,’  <span class="v">7</span>and he from within will answer and say, ‘Don’t bother me. The door is now shut, and my children are with me in bed. I can’t get up and give it to you’?  <span class="v">8</span>I tell you, although he will not rise and give it to him because he is his friend, yet because of his persistence, he will get up and give him as many as he needs. </p>
<p><span class="v">9</span>“I tell you, keep asking, and it will be given you. Keep seeking, and you will find. Keep knocking, and it will be opened to you.  <span class="v">10</span>For everyone who asks receives. He who seeks finds. To him who knocks it will be opened. </p>
<p><span class="v">11</span>“Which of you fathers, if your son asks for bread, will give him a stone? Or if he asks for a fish, he won’t give him a snake instead of a fish, will he?  <span class="v">12</span>Or if he asks for an egg, he won’t give him a scorpion, will he?  <span class="v">13</span>If you then, being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him?” </p>
