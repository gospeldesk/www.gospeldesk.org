<p><span class="v">49</span>John answered, “Master, we saw someone casting out demons in your name, and we forbade him, because he doesn’t follow with us.” </p>
<p><span class="v">50</span>Jesus said to him, “Don’t forbid him, for he who is not against us is for us.” </p>
<p><span class="v">51</span>It came to pass, when the days were near that he should be taken up, he intently set his face to go to Jerusalem,  <span class="v">52</span>and sent messengers before his face. They went, and entered into a village of the Samaritans, so as to prepare for him.  <span class="v">53</span>They didn’t receive him, because he was traveling with his face set towards Jerusalem. </p>
<p><span class="v">54</span>When his disciples, James and John, saw this, they said, “Lord, do you want us to command fire to come down from the sky, and destroy them, just as Elijah did?”  <span class="v">55</span>But he turned and rebuked them, “You don’t know of what kind of spirit you are.  <span class="v">56</span>For the Son of Man didn’t come to destroy men’s lives, but to save them.”</p>
<p>They went to another village.  <span class="v">22</span>Turning to the disciples, he said, “All things have been delivered to me by my Father. No one knows who the Son is, except the Father, and who the Father is, except the Son, and he to whomever the Son desires to reveal him.”  <span class="v">23</span>Turning to the disciples, he said privately, “Blessed are the eyes which see the things that you see,  <span class="v">24</span>for I tell you that many prophets and kings desired to see the things which you see, and didn’t see them, and to hear the things which you hear, and didn’t hear them.” </p>
