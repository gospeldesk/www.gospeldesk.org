<p><span class="v">23</span>When he got into a boat, his disciples followed him.  <span class="v">24</span>Behold, a violent storm came up on the sea, so much that the boat was covered with the waves, but he was asleep.  <span class="v">25</span>They came to him, and woke him up, saying, “Save us, Lord! We are dying!” </p>
<p><span class="v">26</span>He said to them, “Why are you fearful, O you of little faith?” Then he got up, rebuked the wind and the sea, and there was a great calm. </p>
<p><span class="v">27</span>The men marveled, saying, “What kind of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?” </p>
