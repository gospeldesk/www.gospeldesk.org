<p><span class="v">24</span>“Everyone therefore who hears these words of mine, and does them, I will liken him to a wise man, who built his house on a rock.  <span class="v">25</span>The rain came down, the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat on that house; and it didn’t fall, for it was founded on the rock.  <span class="v">26</span>Everyone who hears these words of mine, and doesn’t do them will be like a foolish man, who built his house on the sand.  <span class="v">27</span>The rain came down, the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat on that house; and it fell—and great was its fall.” </p>
<p><span class="v">28</span>When Jesus had finished saying these things, the multitudes were astonished at his teaching,  <span class="v">29</span>for he taught them with authority, and not like the scribes. </p>
<h4>Healing a Leper</h4>
<p><span class="v">1</span>When he came down from the mountain, great multitudes followed him.  <span class="v">2</span>Behold, a leper came to him and worshiped him, saying, “Lord, if you want to, you can make me clean.” </p>
<p><span class="v">3</span>Jesus stretched out his hand, and touched him, saying, “I want to. Be made clean.” Immediately his leprosy was cleansed.  <span class="v">4</span>Jesus said to him, “See that you tell nobody, but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift that Moses commanded, as a testimony to them.” </p>
