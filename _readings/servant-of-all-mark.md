<p><span class="v">33</span>He came to Capernaum, and when he was in the house he asked them, “What were you arguing among yourselves on the way?” </p>
<p><span class="v">34</span>But they were silent, for they had disputed one with another on the way about who was the greatest. </p>
<p><span class="v">35</span>He sat down, and called the twelve; and he said to them, “If any man wants to be first, he shall be last of all, and servant of all.”  <span class="v">36</span>He took a little child, and set him in the middle of them. Taking him in his arms, he said to them,  <span class="v">37</span>“Whoever receives one such little child in my name, receives me, and whoever receives me, doesn’t receive me, but him who sent me.” </p>
<p><span class="v">38</span>John said to him, “Teacher, we saw someone who doesn’t follow us casting out demons in your name; and we forbade him, because he doesn’t follow us.” </p>
<p><span class="v">39</span>But Jesus said, “Don’t forbid him, for there is no one who will do a mighty work in my name, and be able quickly to speak evil of me.  <span class="v">40</span>For whoever is not against us is on our side.  <span class="v">41</span>For whoever will give you a cup of water to drink in my name, because you are Christ’s, most certainly I tell you, he will in no way lose his reward.”</p>
