<p><span class="v">1</span>When they came near to Jerusalem, and came to Bethsphage, to the Mount of Olives, then Jesus sent two disciples,  <span class="v">2</span>saying to them, “Go into the village that is opposite you, and immediately you will find a donkey tied, and a colt with her. Untie them, and bring them to me.  <span class="v">3</span>If anyone says anything to you, you shall say, ‘The Lord needs them,’ and immediately he will send them.”  <span class="v">4</span>All this was done, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through the prophet, saying,  <span class="v">5</span>“Tell the daughter of Zion, behold, your King comes to you, humble, and riding on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey.”  <span class="v">6</span>The disciples went, and did just as Jesus commanded them,  <span class="v">7</span>and brought the donkey and the colt, and laid their clothes on them; and he sat on them.  <span class="v">8</span>A very great multitude spread their clothes on the road. Others cut branches from the trees, and spread them on the road.  <span class="v">9</span>The multitudes who went in front of him, and those who followed, kept shouting, “Hosanna to the son of David! Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord! Hosanna in the highest!”  <span class="v">10</span>When he had come into Jerusalem, all the city was stirred up, saying, “Who is this?”  <span class="v">11</span>The multitudes said, “This is the prophet, Jesus, from Nazareth of Galilee.”  <span class="v">15</span>But when the chief priests and the scribes saw the wonderful things that he did, and the children who were crying in the temple and saying, “Hosanna to the son of David!” they were indignant,  <span class="v">16</span>and said to him, “Do you hear what these are saying?” Jesus said to them, “Yes. Did you never read, ‘Out of the mouth of babes and nursing babies you have perfected praise?’”  <span class="v">17</span>He left them, and went out of the city to Bethany, and camped there. </p>
