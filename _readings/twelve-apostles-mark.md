<p><span class="v">13</span>He went up into the mountain, and called to himself those whom he wanted, and they went to him. </p>
<p><span class="v">14</span>He appointed twelve, that they might be with him, and that he might send them out to preach,  <span class="v">15</span>and to have authority to heal sicknesses and to cast out demons:  <span class="v">16</span>Simon (to whom he gave the name Peter);  <span class="v">17</span>James the son of Zebedee; and John, the brother of James, (whom he called Boanerges, which means, Sons of Thunder);  <span class="v">18</span>Andrew; Philip; Bartholomew; Matthew; Thomas; James, the son of Alphaeus; Thaddaeus; Simon the Zealot;  <span class="v">19</span>and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him. </p>
