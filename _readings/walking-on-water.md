<p><span class="v">22</span>Immediately Jesus made the disciples get into the boat, and to go ahead of him to the other side, while he sent the multitudes away.  <span class="v">23</span>After he had sent the multitudes away, he went up into the mountain by himself to pray. When evening had come, he was there alone.  <span class="v">24</span>But the boat was now in the middle of the sea, distressed by the waves, for the wind was contrary. </p>
<p><span class="v">25</span>In the fourth watch of the night, Jesus came to them, walking on the sea.  <span class="v">26</span>When the disciples saw him walking on the sea, they were troubled, saying, “It’s a ghost!” and they cried out for fear. </p>
<p><span class="v">27</span>But immediately Jesus spoke to them, saying, “Cheer up! It is I! Don’t be afraid.” </p>
<p><span class="v">28</span>Peter answered him and said, “Lord, if it is you, command me to come to you on the waters.” </p>
<p><span class="v">29</span>He said, “Come!”</p>
<p>Peter stepped down from the boat, and walked on the waters to come to Jesus.  <span class="v">30</span>But when he saw that the wind was strong, he was afraid, and beginning to sink, he cried out, saying, “Lord, save me!” </p>
<p><span class="v">31</span>Immediately Jesus stretched out his hand, took hold of him, and said to him, “You of little faith, why did you doubt?” </p>
<p><span class="v">32</span>When they got up into the boat, the wind ceased.  <span class="v">33</span>Those who were in the boat came and worshiped him, saying, “You are truly the Son of God!”  <span class="v">34</span>When they had crossed over, they came to the land of Gennesaret. </p>
