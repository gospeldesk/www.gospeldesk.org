<p><span class="v">1</span>Now before the feast of the Passover, Jesus, knowing that his time had come that he would depart from this world to the Father, having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them to the end. </p>
<p><span class="v">2</span>During supper, the devil having already put into the heart of Judas Iscariot, Simon’s son, to betray him,  <span class="v">3</span>Jesus, knowing that the Father had given all things into his hands, and that he came from God, and was going to God,  <span class="v">4</span>arose from supper, and laid aside his outer garments. He took a towel, and wrapped a towel around his waist.  <span class="v">5</span>Then he poured water into the basin, and began to wash the disciples’ feet, and to wipe them with the towel that was wrapped around him. </p>
<p><span class="v">6</span>Then he came to Simon Peter. He said to him, “Lord, do you wash my feet?” </p>
<p><span class="v">7</span>Jesus answered him, “You don’t know what I am doing now, but you will understand later.” </p>
<p><span class="v">8</span>Peter said to him, “You will never wash my feet!”</p>
<p>Jesus answered him, “If I don’t wash you, you have no part with me.” </p>
<p><span class="v">9</span>Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, not my feet only, but also my hands and my head!” </p>
<p><span class="v">10</span>Jesus said to him, “Someone who has bathed only needs to have his feet washed, but is completely clean. You are clean, but not all of you.”  <span class="v">11</span>For he knew him who would betray him, therefore he said, “You are not all clean.” </p>
<p><span class="v">12</span>So when he had washed their feet, put his outer garment back on, and sat down again, he said to them, “Do you know what I have done to you?  <span class="v">13</span>You call me, ‘Teacher’ and ‘Lord.’ You say so correctly, for so I am.  <span class="v">14</span>If I then, the Lord and the Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another’s feet.  <span class="v">15</span>For I have given you an example, that you also should do as I have done to you.  <span class="v">16</span>Most certainly I tell you, a servant is not greater than his lord, neither one who is sent greater than he who sent him.  <span class="v">17</span>If you know these things, blessed are you if you do them.”</p>
