<p><span class="v">28</span>One of the scribes came, and heard them questioning together. Knowing that he had answered them well, asked him, “Which commandment is the greatest of all?” </p>
<p><span class="v">29</span>Jesus answered, “The greatest is, ‘Hear, Israel, the Lord our God, the Lord is one:  <span class="v">30</span>you shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your mind, and with all your strength.’ This is the first commandment.  <span class="v">31</span>The second is like this, ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’ There is no other commandment greater than these.” </p>
<p><span class="v">32</span>The scribe said to him, “Truly, teacher, you have said well that he is one, and there is none other but he,  <span class="v">33</span>and to love him with all the heart, and with all the understanding, with all the soul, and with all the strength, and to love his neighbor as himself, is more important than all whole burnt offerings and sacrifices.” </p>
<p><span class="v">34</span>When Jesus saw that he answered wisely, he said to him, “You are not far from God’s Kingdom.”</p>
<p>No one dared ask him any question after that. </p>
<h4>Whose Son Is the Christ?</h4>
<p><span class="v">35</span>Jesus responded, as he taught in the temple, “How is it that the scribes say that the Christ is the son of David?  <span class="v">36</span>For David himself said in the Holy Spirit, ‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at my right hand, until I make your enemies the footstool of your feet.”’  <span class="v">37</span>Therefore David himself calls him Lord, so how can he be his son?”</p>
<p>The common people heard him gladly. </p>
