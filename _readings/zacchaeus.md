<p><span class="v">1</span>He entered and was passing through Jericho.  <span class="v">2</span>There was a man named Zacchaeus. He was a chief tax collector, and he was rich.  <span class="v">3</span>He was trying to see who Jesus was, and couldn’t because of the crowd, because he was short.  <span class="v">4</span>He ran on ahead, and climbed up into a sycamore tree to see him, for he was going to pass that way. </p>
<p><span class="v">5</span>When Jesus came to the place, he looked up and saw him, and said to him, “Zacchaeus, hurry and come down, for today I must stay at your house.” </p>
<p><span class="v">6</span>He hurried, came down, and received him joyfully. </p>
<p><span class="v">7</span>When they saw it, they all murmured, saying, “He has gone in to lodge with a man who is a sinner.” </p>
<p><span class="v">8</span>Zacchaeus stood and said to the Lord, “Behold, Lord, half of my goods I give to the poor. If I have wrongfully exacted anything of anyone, I restore four times as much.” </p>
<p><span class="v">9</span>Jesus said to him, “Today, salvation has come to this house, because he also is a son of Abraham.  <span class="v">10</span>For the Son of Man came to seek and to save that which was lost.” </p>
