<div class="verse"><span>16</span>The soldiers led him away within the court, which is the Praetorium; and they called together the whole cohort. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>They clothed him with purple, and weaving a crown of thorns, they put it on him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>They began to salute him, “Hail, King of the Jews!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>They struck his head with a reed, and spat on him, and bowing their knees, did homage to him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>When they had mocked him, they took the purple off him, and put his own garments on him. They led him out to crucify him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>They compelled one passing by, coming from the country, Simon of Cyrene, the father of Alexander and Rufus, to go with them, that he might bear his cross. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>They brought him to the place called Golgotha, which is, being interpreted, “The place of a skull.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>They offered him wine mixed with myrrh to drink, but he didn’t take it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Crucifying him, they parted his garments among them, casting lots on them, what each should take. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>It was the third hour, and they crucified him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>The superscription of his accusation was written over him, “THE KING OF THE JEWS.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>With him they crucified two robbers; one on his right hand, and one on his left. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>The Scripture was fulfilled, which says, “He was counted with transgressors.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>Those who passed by blasphemed him, wagging their heads, and saying, “Ha! You who destroy the temple, and build it in three days, </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>save yourself, and come down from the cross!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Likewise, also the chief priests mocking among themselves with the scribes said, “He saved others. He can’t save himself. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>Let the Christ, the King of Israel, now come down from the cross, that we may see and believe him.” Those who were crucified with him also insulted him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>When the sixth hour had come, there was darkness over the whole land until the ninth hour. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>At the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, “Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani?” which is, being interpreted, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>Some of those who stood by, when they heard it, said, “Behold, he is calling Elijah.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>One ran, and filling a sponge full of vinegar, put it on a reed, and gave it to him to drink, saying, “Let him be. Let’s see whether Elijah comes to take him down.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>Jesus cried out with a loud voice, and gave up the spirit. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>The veil of the temple was torn in two from the top to the bottom. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>When the centurion, who stood by opposite him, saw that he cried out like this and breathed his last, he said, “Truly this man was the Son of God!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>There were also women watching from afar, among whom were both Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the less and of Joses, and Salome; </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>who, when he was in Galilee, followed him, and served him; and many other women who came up with him to Jerusalem. </div>
