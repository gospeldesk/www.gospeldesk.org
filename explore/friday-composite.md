<div class="verse"><span>18</span>I don’t speak concerning all of you. I know whom I have chosen. But that the Scripture may be fulfilled, ‘He who eats bread with me has lifted up his heel against me.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>From now on, I tell you before it happens, that when it happens, you may believe that I am he. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Most certainly I tell you, he who receives whomever I send, receives me; and he who receives me, receives him who sent me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>When Jesus had said this, he was troubled in spirit, and testified, “Most certainly I tell you that one of you will betray me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>The disciples looked at one another, perplexed about whom he spoke. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>One of his disciples, whom Jesus loved, was at the table, leaning against Jesus’ breast. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Simon Peter therefore beckoned to him, and said to him, “Tell us who it is of whom he speaks.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>He, leaning back, as he was, on Jesus’ breast, asked him, “Lord, who is it?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Jesus therefore answered, “It is he to whom I will give this piece of bread when I have dipped it.” So when he had dipped the piece of bread, he gave it to Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>After the piece of bread, then Satan entered into him. Then Jesus said to him, “What you do, do quickly.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>Now no man at the table knew why he said this to him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>For some thought, because Judas had the money box, that Jesus said to him, “Buy what things we need for the feast,” or that he should give something to the poor. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Therefore having received that morsel, he went out immediately. It was night. </div>

Then Jesus said to him, “What you do, do quickly.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>Now no man at the table knew why he said this to him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>For some thought, because Judas had the money box, that Jesus said to him, “Buy what things we need for the feast,” or that he should give something to the poor. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Therefore having received that morsel, he went out immediately. It was night. </div>
