<div class="verse"><span>1</span>When Jesus had spoken these words, he went out with his disciples over the brook Kidron, where there was a garden, into which he and his disciples entered. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Now Judas, who betrayed him, also knew the place, for Jesus often met there with his disciples. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>Judas then, having taken a detachment of soldiers and officers from the chief priests and the Pharisees, came there with lanterns, torches, and weapons. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Jesus therefore, knowing all the things that were happening to him, went out, and said to them, “Who are you looking for?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>They answered him, “Jesus of Nazareth.” Jesus said to them, “I am he.” Judas also, who betrayed him, was standing with them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>When therefore he said to them, “I am he,” they went backward, and fell to the ground. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Again therefore he asked them, “Who are you looking for?” They said, “Jesus of Nazareth.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>Jesus answered, “I told you that I am he. If therefore you seek me, let these go their way,” </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>that the word might be fulfilled which he spoke, “Of those whom you have given me, I have lost none.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Simon Peter therefore, having a sword, drew it, and struck the high priest’s servant, and cut off his right ear. The servant’s name was Malchus. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>Jesus therefore said to Peter, “Put the sword into its sheath. The cup which the Father has given me, shall I not surely drink it?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>So the detachment, the commanding officer, and the officers of the Jews, seized Jesus and bound him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>and led him to Annas first, for he was father-in-law to Caiaphas, who was high priest that year. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Now it was Caiaphas who advised the Jews that it was expedient that one man should perish for the people. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Simon Peter followed Jesus, as did another disciple. Now that disciple was known to the high priest, and entered in with Jesus into the court of the high priest; </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>but Peter was standing at the door outside. So the other disciple, who was known to the high priest, went out and spoke to her who kept the door, and brought in Peter. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Then the maid who kept the door said to Peter, “Are you also one of this man’s disciples?” He said, “I am not.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Now the servants and the officers were standing there, having made a fire of coals, for it was cold. They were warming themselves. Peter was with them, standing and warming himself. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>The high priest therefore asked Jesus about his disciples, and about his teaching. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Jesus answered him, “I spoke openly to the world. I always taught in synagogues, and in the temple, where the Jews always meet. I said nothing in secret. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>Why do you ask me? Ask those who have heard me what I said to them. Behold, these know the things which I said.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>When he had said this, one of the officers standing by slapped Jesus with his hand, saying, “Do you answer the high priest like that?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>Jesus answered him, “If I have spoken evil, testify of the evil; but if well, why do you beat me?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Annas sent him bound to Caiaphas, the high priest. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Now Simon Peter was standing and warming himself. They said therefore to him, “You aren’t also one of his disciples, are you?” He denied it, and said, “I am not.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>One of the servants of the high priest, being a relative of him whose ear Peter had cut off, said, “Didn’t I see you in the garden with him?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>Peter therefore denied it again, and immediately the rooster crowed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>They led Jesus therefore from Caiaphas into the Praetorium. It was early, and they themselves didn’t enter into the Praetorium, that they might not be defiled, but might eat the Passover. </div>
