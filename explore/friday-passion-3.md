<div class="verse"><span>57</span>Those who had taken Jesus led him away to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and the elders were gathered together. </div> <div class="verse"><span>58</span>But Peter followed him from a distance, to the court of the high priest, and entered in and sat with the officers, to see the end. </div> <div class="verse"><span>59</span>Now the chief priests, the elders, and the whole council sought false testimony against Jesus, that they might put him to death; </div> <div class="verse"><span>60</span>and they found none. Even though many false witnesses came forward, they found none. But at last two false witnesses came forward, </div> <div class="verse"><span>61</span>and said, “This man said, ‘I am able to destroy the temple of God, and to build it in three days.’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>62</span>The high priest stood up, and said to him, “Have you no answer? What is this that these testify against you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>63</span>But Jesus held his peace. The high priest answered him, “I adjure you by the living God, that you tell us whether you are the Christ, the Son of God.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>64</span>Jesus said to him, “You have said it. Nevertheless, I tell you, after this you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power, and coming on the clouds of the sky.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>65</span>Then the high priest tore his clothing, saying, “He has spoken blasphemy! Why do we need any more witnesses? Behold, now you have heard his blasphemy. </div> <div class="verse"><span>66</span>What do you think?” They answered, “He is worthy of death!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>67</span>Then they spat in his face and beat him with their fists, and some slapped him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>68</span>saying, “Prophesy to us, you Christ! Who hit you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>69</span>Now Peter was sitting outside in the court, and a maid came to him, saying, “You were also with Jesus, the Galilean!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>70</span>But he denied it before them all, saying, “I don’t know what you are talking about.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>71</span>When he had gone out onto the porch, someone else saw him, and said to those who were there, “This man also was with Jesus of Nazareth.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>72</span>Again he denied it with an oath, “I don’t know the man.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>73</span>After a little while those who stood by came and said to Peter, “Surely you are also one of them, for your speech makes you known.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>74</span>Then he began to curse and to swear, “I don’t know the man!” Immediately the rooster crowed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>75</span>Peter remembered the word which Jesus had said to him, “Before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times.” He went out and wept bitterly. </div>
