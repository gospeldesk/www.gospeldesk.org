<div class="verse"><span>3</span>Then Judas, who betrayed him, when he saw that Jesus was condemned, felt remorse, and brought back the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders, </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>saying, “I have sinned in that I betrayed innocent blood.” But they said, “What is that to us? You see to it.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>He threw down the pieces of silver in the sanctuary, and departed. He went away and hanged himself. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>The chief priests took the pieces of silver, and said, “It’s not lawful to put them into the treasury, since it is the price of blood.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>They took counsel, and bought the potter’s field with them, to bury strangers in. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>Therefore that field was called “The Field of Blood” to this day. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>Then that which was spoken through Jeremiah “They took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him upon whom a price had been set, whom some of the children of Israel priced, </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>and they gave them for the potter’s field, as the Lord commanded me.” ;  ;  </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>Now Jesus stood before the governor: and the governor asked him, saying, “Are you the King of the Jews?” Jesus said to him, “So you say.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>When he was accused by the chief priests and elders, he answered nothing. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Then Pilate said to him, “Don’t you hear how many things they testify against you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>He gave him no answer, not even one word, so that the governor marveled greatly. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Now at the feast the governor was accustomed to release to the multitude one prisoner, whom they desired. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>They had then a notable prisoner, called Barabbas. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>When therefore they were gathered together, Pilate said to them, “Whom do you want me to release to you? Barabbas, or Jesus, who is called Christ?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>For he knew that because of envy they had delivered him up. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>While he was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent to him, saying, “Have nothing to do with that righteous man, for I have suffered many things today in a dream because of him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Now the chief priests and the elders persuaded the multitudes to ask for Barabbas, and destroy Jesus. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>But the governor answered them, “Which of the two do you want me to release to you?” They said, “Barabbas!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Pilate said to them, “What then shall I do to Jesus, who is called Christ?” They all said to him, “Let him be crucified!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>But the governor said, “Why? What evil has he done?” But they cried out exceedingly, saying, “Let him be crucified!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>So when Pilate saw that nothing was being gained, but rather that a disturbance was starting, he took water, and washed his hands before the multitude, saying, “I am innocent of the blood of this righteous person. You see to it.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>All the people answered, “May his blood be on us, and on our children!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Then he released to them Barabbas, but Jesus he flogged and delivered to be crucified. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>Then the governor’s soldiers took Jesus into the Praetorium, and gathered the whole garrison together against him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>They stripped him, and put a scarlet robe on him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>They braided a crown of thorns and put it on his head, and a reed in his right hand; and they kneeled down before him, and mocked him, saying, “Hail, King of the Jews!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>They spat on him, and took the reed and struck him on the head. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>When they had mocked him, they took the robe off him, and put his clothes on him, and led him away to crucify him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>As they came out, they found a man of Cyrene, Simon by name, and they compelled him to go with them, that he might carry his cross. </div>
