<div class="verse"><span>32</span>There were also others, two criminals, led with him to be put to death. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>When they came to the place that is called The Skull, they crucified him there with the criminals, one on the right and the other on the left. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they don’t know what they are doing.” Dividing his garments among them, they cast lots. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>The people stood watching. The rulers with them also scoffed at him, saying, “He saved others. Let him save himself, if this is the Christ of God, his chosen one!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>The soldiers also mocked him, coming to him and offering him vinegar, </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>and saying, “If you are the King of the Jews, save yourself!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>An inscription was also written over him in letters of Greek, Latin, and Hebrew: “THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>One of the criminals who was hanged insulted him, saying, “If you are the Christ, save yourself and us!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>But the other answered, and rebuking him said, “Don’t you even fear God, seeing you are under the same condemnation? </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>And we indeed justly, for we receive the due reward for our deeds, but this man has done nothing wrong.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>He said to Jesus, “Lord, remember me when you come into your Kingdom.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>Jesus said to him, “Assuredly I tell you, today you will be with me in Paradise.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>It was now about the sixth hour, and darkness came over the whole land until the ninth hour. </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>The sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was torn in two. </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>Jesus, crying with a loud voice, said, “Father, into your hands I commit my spirit!” Having said this, he breathed his last. </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>When the centurion saw what was done, he glorified God, saying, “Certainly this was a righteous man.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>48</span>All the multitudes that came together to see this, when they saw the things that were done, returned home beating their breasts. </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>All his acquaintances, and the women who followed with him from Galilee, stood at a distance, watching these things. </div>
