<div class="verse"><span>25</span>But there were standing by the cross of Jesus his mother, and his mother’s sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Therefore when Jesus saw his mother, and the disciple whom he loved standing there, he said to his mother, “Woman, behold, your son!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>Then he said to the disciple, “Behold, your mother!” From that hour, the disciple took her to his own home. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>After this, Jesus, seeing “I am thirsty.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>Now a vessel full of vinegar was set there; so they put a sponge full of the vinegar on hyssop, and held it at his mouth. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>When Jesus therefore had received the vinegar, he said, “It is finished.” He bowed his head, and gave up his spirit. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Therefore the Jews, because it was the Preparation Day, so that the bodies wouldn’t remain on the cross on the Sabbath (for that Sabbath was a special one), asked of Pilate that their legs might be broken, and that they might be taken away. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>Therefore the soldiers came, and broke the legs of the first, and of the other who was crucified with him; </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>but when they came to Jesus, and saw that he was already dead, they didn’t break his legs. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>However one of the soldiers pierced his side with a spear, and immediately blood and water came out. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>He who has seen has testified, and his testimony is true. He knows that he tells the truth, that you may believe. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>For these things happened, that the Scripture might be fulfilled, “A bone of him will not be broken.” ;  ;  </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>Again another Scripture says, “They will look on him whom they pierced.” </div>
