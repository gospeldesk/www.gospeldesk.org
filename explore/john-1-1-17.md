<div class="passage">
<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">1</span>In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">2</span>The same was in the beginning with God. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">3</span>All things were made through him. Without him was not anything made that has been made. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">4</span>In him was life, and the life was the light of men. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">5</span>The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness hasn’t overcome it. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">6</span>There came a man, sent from God, whose name was John. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">7</span>The same came as a witness, that he might testify about the light, that all might believe through him. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">8</span>He was not the light, but was sent that he might testify about the light. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">9</span>The true light that enlightens everyone was coming into the world. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">10</span>He was in the world, and the world was made through him, and the world didn’t recognize him. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">11</span>He came to his own, and those who were his own didn’t receive him. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">12</span>But as many as received him, to them he gave the right to become God’s children, to those who believe in his name: </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">13</span>who were born not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">14</span>The Word became flesh, and lived among us. We saw his glory, such glory as of the one and only Son of the Father, full of grace and truth. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">15</span>John testified about him. He cried out, saying, “This was he of whom I said, ‘He who comes after me has surpassed me, for he was before me.’” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">16</span>From his fullness we all received grace upon grace. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">17</span>For the law was given through Moses. Grace and truth were realized through Jesus Christ. </span></p>
