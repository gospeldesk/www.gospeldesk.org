<div class="verse"><span>18</span>No one has seen God at any time. The one and only Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, he has declared him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>This is John’s testimony, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him, “Who are you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>He declared, and didn’t deny, but he declared, “I am not the Christ.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>They asked him, “What then? Are you Elijah?” He said, “I am not.” “Are you the prophet?” He answered, “No.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>They said therefore to him, “Who are you? Give us an answer to take back to those who sent us. What do you say about yourself?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>He said, “I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness, ‘Make straight the way of the Lord,’ as Isaiah the prophet said.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>The ones who had been sent were from the Pharisees. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>They asked him, “Why then do you baptize, if you are not the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the prophet?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>John answered them, “I baptize in water, but among you stands one whom you don’t know. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>He is the one who comes after me, who is preferred before me, whose sandal strap I’m not worthy to loosen.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>These things were done in Bethany beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing. </div>
