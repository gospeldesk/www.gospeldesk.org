<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">35</span>Again, the next day, John was standing with two of his disciples, </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">36</span>and he looked at Jesus as he walked, and said, “Behold, the Lamb of God!” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">37</span>The two disciples heard him speak, and they followed Jesus. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">38</span>Jesus turned, and saw them following, and said to them, “What are you looking for?” They said to him, “Rabbi” (which is to say, being interpreted, Teacher), “where are you staying?” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">39</span>He said to them, “Come, and see.” They came and saw where he was staying, and they stayed with him that day. It was about the tenth hour. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">40</span>One of the two who heard John, and followed him, was Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">41</span>He first found his own brother, Simon, and said to him, “We have found the Messiah!” (which is, being interpreted, Christ ). </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">42</span>He brought him to Jesus. Jesus looked at him, and said, “You are Simon the son of Jonah. You shall be called Cephas” (which is by interpretation, Peter). </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">43</span>On the next day, he was determined to go out into Galilee, and he found Philip. Jesus said to him, “Follow me.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">44</span>Now Philip was from Bethsaida, of the city of Andrew and Peter. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">45</span>Philip found Nathanael, and said to him, “We have found him, of whom Moses in the law, and the prophets, wrote: Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">46</span>Nathanael said to him, “Can any good thing come out of Nazareth?” Philip said to him, “Come and see.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">47</span>Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him, and said about him, “Behold, an Israelite indeed, in whom is no deceit!” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">48</span>Nathanael said to him, “How do you know me?” Jesus answered him, “Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">49</span>Nathanael answered him, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are King of Israel!” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">50</span>Jesus answered him, “Because I told you, ‘I saw you underneath the fig tree,’ do you believe? You will see greater things than these!” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">51</span>He said to him, “Most certainly, I tell you all, hereafter you will see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man.” </span></p>
