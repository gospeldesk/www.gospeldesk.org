<div class="verse"><span>17</span>Therefore the Father loves me, because I lay down my life, that I may take it again. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>No one takes it away from me, but I lay it down by myself. I have power to lay it down, and I have power to take it again. I received this commandment from my Father.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Therefore a division arose again among the Jews because of these words. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Many of them said, “He has a demon, and is insane! Why do you listen to him?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>Others said, “These are not the sayings of one possessed by a demon. It isn’t possible for a demon to open the eyes of the blind, is it?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>It was the Feast of the Dedication at Jerusalem. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>It was winter, and Jesus was walking in the temple, in Solomon’s porch. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>The Jews therefore came around him and said to him, “How long will you hold us in suspense? If you are the Christ, tell us plainly.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Jesus answered them, “I told you, and you don’t believe. The works that I do in my Father’s name, these testify about me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>But you don’t believe, because you are not of my sheep, as I told you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>I give eternal life to them. They will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>My Father, who has given them to me, is greater than all. No one is able to snatch them out of my Father’s hand. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>I and the Father are one.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Therefore Jews took up stones again to stone him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>Jesus answered them, “I have shown you many good works from my Father. For which of those works do you stone me?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>The Jews answered him, “We don’t stone you for a good work, but for blasphemy: because you, being a man, make yourself God.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Jesus answered them, “Isn’t it written in your law, ‘I said, you are gods?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>If he called them gods, to whom the word of God came (and the Scripture can’t be broken), </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>do you say of him whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world, ‘You blaspheme,’ because I said, ‘I am the Son of God?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>If I don’t do the works of my Father, don’t believe me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>But if I do them, though you don’t believe me, believe the works; that you may know and believe that the Father is in me, and I in the Father.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>They sought again to seize him, and he went out of their hand. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>He went away again beyond the Jordan into the place where John was baptizing at first, and there he stayed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>Many came to him. They said, “John indeed did no sign, but everything that John said about this man is true.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>Many believed in him there. </div>
