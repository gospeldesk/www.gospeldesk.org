<div class="verse"><span>9</span>I am the door. If anyone enters in by me, he will be saved, and will go in and go out, and will find pasture. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>The thief only comes to steal, kill, and destroy. I came that they may have life, and may have it abundantly. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>I am the good shepherd. ;  , The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>He who is a hired hand, and not a shepherd, who doesn’t own the sheep, sees the wolf coming, leaves the sheep, and flees. The wolf snatches the sheep, and scatters them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>The hired hand flees because he is a hired hand, and doesn’t care for the sheep. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>I am the good shepherd. I know my own, and I’m known by my own; </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>even as the Father knows me, and I know the Father. I lay down my life for the sheep. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>I have other sheep, which are not of this fold. I must bring them also, and they will hear my voice. They will become one flock with one shepherd. </div>
