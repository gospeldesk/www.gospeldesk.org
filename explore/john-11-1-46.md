<div class="verse"><span>1</span>Now a certain man was sick, Lazarus from Bethany, of the village of Mary and her sister, Martha. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>It was that Mary who had anointed the Lord with ointment, and wiped his feet with her hair, whose brother, Lazarus, was sick. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>The sisters therefore sent to him, saying, “Lord, behold, he for whom you have great affection is sick.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>But when Jesus heard it, he said, “This sickness is not to death, but for the glory of God, that God’s Son may be glorified by it.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>When therefore he heard that he was sick, he stayed two days in the place where he was. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Then after this he said to the disciples, “Let’s go into Judea again.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>The disciples told him, “Rabbi, the Jews were just trying to stone you, and are you going there again?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>Jesus answered, “Aren’t there twelve hours of daylight? If a man walks in the day, he doesn’t stumble, because he sees the light of this world. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>But if a man walks in the night, he stumbles, because the light isn’t in him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>He said these things, and after that, he said to them, “Our friend, Lazarus, has fallen asleep, but I am going so that I may awake him out of sleep.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>The disciples therefore said, “Lord, if he has fallen asleep, he will recover.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Now Jesus had spoken of his death, but they thought that he spoke of taking rest in sleep. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>So Jesus said to them plainly then, “Lazarus is dead. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>I am glad for your sakes that I was not there, so that you may believe. Nevertheless, let’s go to him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>Thomas therefore, who is called Didymus, said to his fellow disciples, “Let’s go also, that we may die with him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>So when Jesus came, he found that he had been in the tomb four days already. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Now Bethany was near Jerusalem, about fifteen stadia away. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Many of the Jews had joined the women around Martha and Mary, to console them concerning their brother. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Then when Martha heard that Jesus was coming, she went and met him, but Mary stayed in the house. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>Therefore Martha said to Jesus, “Lord, if you would have been here, my brother wouldn’t have died. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Even now I know that, whatever you ask of God, God will give you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>Jesus said to her, “Your brother will rise again.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Martha said to him, “I know that he will rise again in the resurrection at the last day.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Jesus said to her, “I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in me will still live, even if he dies. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Whoever lives and believes in me will never die. Do you believe this?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>She said to him, “Yes, Lord. I have come to believe that you are the Christ, God’s Son, he who comes into the world.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>When she had said this, she went away, and called Mary, her sister, secretly, saying, “The Teacher is here, and is calling you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>When she heard this, she arose quickly, and went to him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Now Jesus had not yet come into the village, but was in the place where Martha met him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Then the Jews who were with her in the house, and were consoling her, when they saw Mary, that she rose up quickly and went out, followed her, saying, “She is going to the tomb to weep there.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>Therefore when Mary came to where Jesus was, and saw him, she fell down at his feet, saying to him, “Lord, if you would have been here, my brother wouldn’t have died.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>When Jesus therefore saw her weeping, and the Jews weeping who came with her, he groaned in the spirit, and was troubled, </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>and said, “Where have you laid him?” They told him, “Lord, come and see.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>Jesus wept. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>The Jews therefore said, “See how much affection he had for him!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>Some of them said, “Couldn’t this man, who opened the eyes of him who was blind, have also kept this man from dying?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>Jesus therefore, again groaning in himself, came to the tomb. Now it was a cave, and a stone lay against it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>Jesus said, “Take away the stone.” Martha, the sister of him who was dead, said to him, “Lord, by this time there is a stench, for he has been dead four days.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>Jesus said to her, “Didn’t I tell you that if you believed, you would see God’s glory?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>So they took away the stone from the place where the dead man was lying. Jesus lifted up his eyes, and said, “Father, I thank you that you listened to me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>I know that you always listen to me, but because of the multitude standing around I said this, that they may believe that you sent me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>When he had said this, he cried with a loud voice, “Lazarus, come out!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>He who was dead came out, bound hand and foot with wrappings, and his face was wrapped around with a cloth. Jesus said to them, “Free him, and let him go.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>Therefore many of the Jews, who came to Mary and saw what Jesus did, believed in him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>But some of them went away to the Pharisees, and told them the things which Jesus had done. </div>
