<div class="verse"><span>19</span>The Pharisees therefore said among themselves, “See how you accomplish nothing. Behold, the world has gone after him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Now there were certain Greeks among those that went up to worship at the feast. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>These, therefore, came to Philip, who was from Bethsaida of Galilee, and asked him, saying, “Sir, we want to see Jesus.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Philip came and told Andrew, and in turn, Andrew came with Philip, and they told Jesus. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>Jesus answered them, “The time has come for the Son of Man to be glorified. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Most certainly I tell you, unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains by itself alone. But if it dies, it bears much fruit. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>He who loves his life will lose it. He who hates his life in this world will keep it to eternal life. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>If anyone serves me, let him follow me. Where I am, there will my servant also be. If anyone serves me, the Father will honor him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>“Now my soul is troubled. What shall I say? ‘Father, save me from this time?’ But for this cause I came to this time. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>Father, glorify your name!” Then there came a voice out of the sky, saying, “I have both glorified it, and will glorify it again.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>The multitude therefore, who stood by and heard it, said that it had thundered. Others said, “An angel has spoken to him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Jesus answered, “This voice hasn’t come for my sake, but for your sakes. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Now is the judgment of this world. Now the prince of this world will be cast out. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>And I, if I am lifted up from the earth, will draw all people to myself.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>But he said this, signifying by what kind of death he should die. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>The multitude answered him, “We have heard out of the law that the Christ remains forever. How do you say, ‘The Son of Man must be lifted up?’ Who is this Son of Man?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>Jesus therefore said to them, “Yet a little while the light is with you. Walk while you have the light, that darkness doesn’t overtake you. He who walks in the darkness doesn’t know where he is going. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>While you have the light, believe in the light, that you may become children of light.” Jesus said these things, and he departed and hid himself from them. </div>
