<div class="verse"><span>36</span>While you have the light, believe in the light, that you may become children of light.” Jesus said these things, and he departed and hid himself from them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>But though he had done so many signs before them, yet they didn’t believe in him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>that the word of Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, which he spoke, “Lord, who has believed our report? To whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>For this cause they couldn’t believe, for Isaiah said again, </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>“He has blinded their eyes and he hardened their heart, lest they should see with their eyes, and perceive with their heart, and would turn, and I would heal them.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>Isaiah said these things when he saw his glory, and spoke of him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>Nevertheless even of the rulers many believed in him, but because of the Pharisees they didn’t confess it, so that they wouldn’t be put out of the synagogue, </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>for they loved men’s praise more than God’s praise. </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>Jesus cried out and said, “Whoever believes in me, believes not in me, but in him who sent me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>He who sees me sees him who sent me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>I have come as a light into the world, that whoever believes in me may not remain in the darkness. </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>If anyone listens to my sayings, and doesn’t believe, I don’t judge him. For I came not to judge the world, but to save the world. </div> <div class="verse"><span>48</span>He who rejects me, and doesn’t receive my sayings, has one who judges him. The word that I spoke, the same will judge him in the last day. </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>For I spoke not from myself, but the Father who sent me, he gave me a commandment, what I should say, and what I should speak. </div> <div class="verse"><span>50</span>I know that his commandment is eternal life. The things therefore which I speak, even as the Father has said to me, so I speak.” </div>
