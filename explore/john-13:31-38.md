<div class="verse"><span>31</span>When he had gone out, Jesus said, “Now the Son of Man has been glorified, and God has been glorified in him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>If God has been glorified in him, God will also glorify him in himself, and he will glorify him immediately. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>Little children, I will be with you a little while longer. You will seek me, and as I said to the Jews, ‘Where I am going, you can’t come,’ so now I tell you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another. Just as I have loved you, you also love one another. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, where are you going?” Jesus answered, “Where I am going, you can’t follow now, but you will follow afterwards.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>Peter said to him, “Lord, why can’t I follow you now? I will lay down my life for you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>Jesus answered him, “Will you lay down your life for me? Most certainly I tell you, the rooster won’t crow until you have denied me three times. </div>
