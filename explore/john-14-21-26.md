<div class="verse"><span>21</span>One who has my commandments, and keeps them, that person is one who loves me. One who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him, and will reveal myself to him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Judas (not Iscariot) said to him, “Lord, what has happened that you are about to reveal yourself to us, and not to the world?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>Jesus answered him, “If a man loves me, he will keep my word. My Father will love him, and we will come to him, and make our home with him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>He who doesn’t love me doesn’t keep my words. The word which you hear isn’t mine, but the Father’s who sent me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>I have said these things to you, while still living with you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>But the Counselor, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, he will teach you all things, and will remind you of all that I said to you. </div>
