<div class="verse"><span>27</span>Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, give I to you. Don’t let your heart be troubled, neither let it be fearful. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>You heard how I told you, ‘I go away, and I come to you.’ If you loved me, you would have rejoiced, because I said ‘I am going to my Father;’ for the Father is greater than I. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>Now I have told you before it happens so that, when it happens, you may believe. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>I will no more speak much with you, for the prince of the world comes, and he has nothing in me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>But that the world may know that I love the Father, and as the Father commanded me, even so I do. Arise, let us go from here. </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>“I am the true vine, and my Father is the farmer. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Every branch in me that doesn’t bear fruit, he takes away. Every branch that bears fruit, he prunes, that it may bear more fruit. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>You are already pruned clean because of the word which I have spoken to you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Remain in me, and I in you. As the branch can’t bear fruit by itself, unless it remains in the vine, so neither can you, unless you remain in me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>I am the vine. You are the branches. He who remains in me, and I in him, the same bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>If a man doesn’t remain in me, he is thrown out as a branch, and is withered; and they gather them, throw them into the fire, and they are burned. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>If you remain in me, and my words remain in you, you will ask whatever you desire, and it will be done for you. </div>
