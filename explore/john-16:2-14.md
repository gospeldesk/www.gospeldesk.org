<div class="verse"><span>2</span>They will put you out of the synagogues. Yes, the time comes that whoever kills you will think that he offers service to God. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>They will do these things because they have not known the Father, nor me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>But I have told you these things, so that when the time comes, you may remember that I told you about them. I didn’t tell you these things from the beginning, because I was with you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>But now I am going to him who sent me, and none of you asks me, ‘Where are you going?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>But because I have told you these things, sorrow has filled your heart. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Nevertheless I tell you the truth: It is to your advantage that I go away, for if I don’t go away, the Counselor won’t come to you. But if I go, I will send him to you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>When he has come, he will convict the world about sin, about righteousness, and about judgment; </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>about sin, because they don’t believe in me; </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>about righteousness, because I am going to my Father, and you won’t see me any more; </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>about judgment, because the prince of this world has been judged. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>“I have yet many things to tell you, but you can’t bear them now. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>However when he, the Spirit of truth, has come, he will guide you into all truth, for he will not speak from himself; but whatever he hears, he will speak. He will declare to you things that are coming. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>He will glorify me, for he will take from what is mine, and will declare it to you. </div>
