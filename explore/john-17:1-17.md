<div class="verse"><span>1</span>Jesus said these things, and lifting up his eyes to heaven, he said, “Father, the time has come. Glorify your Son, that your Son may also glorify you; </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>even as you gave him authority over all flesh, so he will give eternal life to all whom you have given him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>This is eternal life, that they should know you, the only true God, and him whom you sent, Jesus Christ. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>I glorified you on the earth. I have accomplished the work which you have given me to do. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Now, Father, glorify me with your own self with the glory which I had with you before the world existed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>I revealed your name to the people whom you have given me out of the world. They were yours, and you have given them to me. They have kept your word. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Now they have known that all things whatever you have given me are from you, </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>for the words which you have given me I have given to them, and they received them, and knew for sure that I came from you, and they have believed that you sent me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>I pray for them. I don’t pray for the world, but for those whom you have given me, for they are yours. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>All things that are mine are yours, and yours are mine, and I am glorified in them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I am coming to you. Holy Father, keep them through your name which you have given me, that they may be one, even as we are. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>While I was with them in the world, I kept them in your name. Those whom you have given me I have kept. None of them is lost, except the son of destruction, that the Scripture might be fulfilled. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>But now I come to you, and I say these things in the world, that they may have my joy made full in themselves. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>I have given them your word. The world hated them, because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>I pray not that you would take them from the world, but that you would keep them from the evil one. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>They are not of the world even as I am not of the world. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Sanctify them in your truth. Your word is truth. </div>
