<div class="verse"><span>28</span>They led Jesus therefore from Caiaphas into the Praetorium. It was early, and they themselves didn’t enter into the Praetorium, that they might not be defiled, but might eat the Passover. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>Pilate therefore went out to them, and said, “What accusation do you bring against this man?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>They answered him, “If this man weren’t an evildoer, we wouldn’t have delivered him up to you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Pilate therefore said to them, “Take him yourselves, and judge him according to your law.” Therefore the Jews said to him, “It is not lawful for us to put anyone to death,” </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>that the word of Jesus might be fulfilled, which he spoke, signifying by what kind of death he should die. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>Pilate therefore entered again into the Praetorium, called Jesus, and said to him, “Are you the King of the Jews?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Jesus answered him, “Do you say this by yourself, or did others tell you about me?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>Pilate answered, “I’m not a Jew, am I? Your own nation and the chief priests delivered you to me. What have you done?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>Jesus answered, “My Kingdom is not of this world. If my Kingdom were of this world, then my servants would fight, that I wouldn’t be delivered to the Jews. But now my Kingdom is not from here.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>Pilate therefore said to him, “Are you a king then?” Jesus answered, “You say that I am a king. For this reason I have been born, and for this reason I have come into the world, that I should testify to the truth. Everyone who is of the truth listens to my voice.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>Pilate said to him, “What is truth?” When he had said this, he went out again to the Jews, and said to them, “I find no basis for a charge against him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>But you have a custom, that I should release someone to you at the Passover. Therefore, do you want me to release to you the King of the Jews?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>Then they all shouted again, saying, “Not this man, but Barabbas!” Now Barabbas was a robber. </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>So Pilate then took Jesus, and flogged him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>The soldiers twisted thorns into a crown, and put it on his head, and dressed him in a purple garment. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>They kept saying, “Hail, King of the Jews!” and they kept slapping him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Then Pilate went out again, and said to them, “Behold, I bring him out to you, that you may know that I find no basis for a charge against him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Jesus therefore came out, wearing the crown of thorns and the purple garment. Pilate said to them, “Behold, the man!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>When therefore the chief priests and the officers saw him, they shouted, saying, “Crucify! Crucify!” Pilate said to them, “Take him yourselves, and crucify him, for I find no basis for a charge against him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>The Jews answered him, “We have a law, and by our law he ought to die, because he made himself the Son of God.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>When therefore Pilate heard this saying, he was more afraid. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>He entered into the Praetorium again, and said to Jesus, “Where are you from?” But Jesus gave him no answer. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Pilate therefore said to him, “Aren’t you speaking to me? Don’t you know that I have power to release you, and have power to crucify you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>Jesus answered, “You would have no power at all against me, unless it were given to you from above. Therefore he who delivered me to you has greater sin.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>At this, Pilate was seeking to release him, but the Jews cried out, saying, “If you release this man, you aren’t Caesar’s friend! Everyone who makes himself a king speaks against Caesar!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>When Pilate therefore heard these words, he brought Jesus out, and sat down on the judgment seat at a place called “The Pavement”, but in Hebrew, “Gabbatha.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Now it was the Preparation Day of the Passover, at about the sixth hour. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>They cried out, “Away with him! Away with him! Crucify him!” Pilate said to them, “Shall I crucify your King?” The chief priests answered, “We have no king but Caesar!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>So then he delivered him to them to be crucified. So they took Jesus and led him away. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>He went out, bearing his cross, to the place called “The Place of a Skull”, which is called in Hebrew, “Golgotha”, </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>where they crucified him, and with him two others, on either side one, and Jesus in the middle. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Pilate wrote a title also, and put it on the cross. There was written, “JESUS OF NAZARETH, THE KING OF THE JEWS.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Therefore many of the Jews read this title, for the place where Jesus was crucified was near the city; and it was written in Hebrew, in Latin, and in Greek. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>The chief priests of the Jews therefore said to Pilate, “Don’t write, ‘The King of the Jews,’ but, ‘he said, “I am King of the Jews.”’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Pilate answered, “What I have written, I have written.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>Then the soldiers, when they had crucified Jesus, took his garments and made four parts, to every soldier a part; and also the coat. Now the coat was without seam, woven from the top throughout. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Then they said to one another, “Let’s not tear it, but cast lots for it to decide whose it will be,” that the Scripture might be fulfilled, which says, “They parted my garments among them. For my cloak they cast lots.” Therefore the soldiers did these things. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>But there were standing by the cross of Jesus his mother, and his mother’s sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Therefore when Jesus saw his mother, and the disciple whom he loved standing there, he said to his mother, “Woman, behold, your son!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>Then he said to the disciple, “Behold, your mother!” From that hour, the disciple took her to his own home. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>After this, Jesus, seeing “I am thirsty.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>Now a vessel full of vinegar was set there; so they put a sponge full of the vinegar on hyssop, and held it at his mouth. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>When Jesus therefore had received the vinegar, he said, “It is finished.” He bowed his head, and gave up his spirit. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Therefore the Jews, because it was the Preparation Day, so that the bodies wouldn’t remain on the cross on the Sabbath (for that Sabbath was a special one), asked of Pilate that their legs might be broken, and that they might be taken away. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>Therefore the soldiers came, and broke the legs of the first, and of the other who was crucified with him; </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>but when they came to Jesus, and saw that he was already dead, they didn’t break his legs. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>However one of the soldiers pierced his side with a spear, and immediately blood and water came out. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>He who has seen has testified, and his testimony is true. He knows that he tells the truth, that you may believe. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>For these things happened, that the Scripture might be fulfilled, “A bone of him will not be broken.” ;  ;  </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>Again another Scripture says, “They will look on him whom they pierced.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>This is the disciple who testifies about these things, and wrote these things. We know that his witness is true. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>There are also many other things which Jesus did, which if they would all be written, I suppose that even the world itself wouldn’t have room for the books that would be written. 
</div>
