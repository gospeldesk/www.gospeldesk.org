<div class="verse"><span>29</span>The next day, he saw Jesus coming to him, and said, “Behold, </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>This is he of whom I said, ‘After me comes a man who is preferred before me, for he was before me.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>I didn’t know him, but for this reason I came baptizing in water: that he would be revealed to Israel.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>John testified, saying, “I have seen the Spirit descending like a dove out of heaven, and it remained on him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>I didn’t recognize him, but he who sent me to baptize in water, he said to me, ‘On whomever you will see the Spirit descending, and remaining on him, the same is he who baptizes in the Holy Spirit.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>I have seen, and have testified that this is the Son of God.” </div>
