<div class="verse"><span>12</span>After this, he went down to Capernaum, he, and his mother, his brothers, and his disciples; and they stayed there a few days. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>The Passover of the Jews was at hand, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>He found in the temple those who sold oxen, sheep, and doves, and the changers of money sitting. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>He made a whip of cords, and threw all out of the temple, both the sheep and the oxen; and he poured out the changers’ money, and overthrew their tables. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>To those who sold the doves, he said, “Take these things out of here! Don’t make my Father’s house a marketplace!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>His disciples remembered that it was written, “Zeal for your house will eat me up.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>The Jews therefore answered him, “What sign do you show us, seeing that you do these things?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Jesus answered them, “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>The Jews therefore said, “It took forty-six years to build this temple! Will you raise it up in three days?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>But he spoke of the temple of his body. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>When therefore he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered that he said this, and they believed the Scripture, and the word which Jesus had said. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>Now when he was in Jerusalem at the Passover, during the feast, many believed in his name, observing his signs which he did. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>But Jesus didn’t entrust himself to them, because he knew everyone, </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>and because he didn’t need for anyone to testify concerning man; for he himself knew what was in man. </div>
