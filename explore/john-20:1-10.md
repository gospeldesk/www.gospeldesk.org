<div class="verse"><span>1</span>Now on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene went early, while it was still dark, to the tomb, and saw the stone taken away from the tomb. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Therefore she ran and came to Simon Peter, and to the other disciple whom Jesus loved, and said to them, “They have taken away the Lord out of the tomb, and we don’t know where they have laid him!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>Therefore Peter and the other disciple went out, and they went toward the tomb. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>They both ran together. The other disciple outran Peter, and came to the tomb first. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Stooping and looking in, he saw the linen cloths lying, yet he didn’t enter in. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>Then Simon Peter came, following him, and entered into the tomb. He saw the linen cloths lying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>and the cloth that had been on his head, not lying with the linen cloths, but rolled up in a place by itself. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>So then the other disciple who came first to the tomb also entered in, and he saw and believed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>For as yet they didn’t know the Scripture, that he must rise from the dead. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>So the disciples went away again to their own homes. </div>
