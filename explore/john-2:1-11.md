<div class="verse"><span>1</span>The third day, there was a marriage in Cana of Galilee. Jesus’ mother was there. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Jesus also was invited, with his disciples, to the marriage. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>When the wine ran out, Jesus’ mother said to him, “They have no wine.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Jesus said to her, “Woman, what does that have to do with you and me? My hour has not yet come.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>His mother said to the servants, “Whatever he says to you, do it.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>Now there were six water pots of stone set there after the Jews’ way of purifying, containing two or three metretes </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Jesus said to them, “Fill the water pots with water.” They filled them up to the brim. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>He said to them, “Now draw some out, and take it to the ruler of the feast.” So they took it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>When the ruler of the feast tasted the water now become wine, and didn’t know where it came from (but the servants who had drawn the water knew), the ruler of the feast called the bridegroom, </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>and said to him, “Everyone serves the good wine first, and when the guests have drunk freely, then that which is worse. You have kept the good wine until now!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>This beginning of his signs Jesus did in Cana of Galilee, and revealed his glory; and his disciples believed in him. </div>
