<div class="verse"><span>1</span>Therefore when the Lord knew that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus was making and baptizing more disciples than John </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>(although Jesus himself didn’t baptize, but his disciples), </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>he left Judea, and departed into Galilee. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>He needed to pass through Samaria. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>So he came to a city of Samaria, called Sychar, near the parcel of ground that Jacob gave to his son, Joseph. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>Jacob’s well was there. Jesus therefore, being tired from his journey, sat down by the well. It was about the sixth hour. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>A woman of Samaria came to draw water. Jesus said to her, “Give me a drink.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>For his disciples had gone away into the city to buy food. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>The Samaritan woman therefore said to him, “How is it that you, being a Jew, ask for a drink from me, a Samaritan woman?” (For Jews have no dealings with Samaritans.) </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Jesus answered her, “If you knew the gift of God, and who it is who says to you, ‘Give me a drink,’ you would have asked him, and he would have given you living water.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>The woman said to him, “Sir, you have nothing to draw with, and the well is deep. So where do you get that living water? </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>Are you greater than our father, Jacob, who gave us the well, and drank of it himself, as did his children, and his livestock?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Jesus answered her, “Everyone who drinks of this water will thirst again, </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>but whoever drinks of the water that I will give him will never thirst again; but the water that I will give him will become in him a well of water springing up to eternal life.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>The woman said to him, “Sir, give me this water, so that I don’t get thirsty, neither come all the way here to draw.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>Jesus said to her, “Go, call your husband, and come here.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>The woman answered, “I have no husband.” Jesus said to her, “You said well, ‘I have no husband,’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>for you have had five husbands; and he whom you now have is not your husband. This you have said truly.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>The woman said to him, “Sir, I perceive that you are a prophet. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Our fathers worshiped in this mountain, and you Jews say that in Jerusalem is the place where people ought to worship.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>Jesus said to her, “Woman, believe me, the hour comes, when neither in this mountain, nor in Jerusalem, will you worship the Father. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>You worship that which you don’t know. We worship that which we know; for salvation is from the Jews. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>But the hour comes, and now is, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth, for the Father seeks such to be his worshipers. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>God is spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>The woman said to him, “I know that Messiah comes, he who is called Christ. When he has come, he will declare to us all things.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Jesus said to her, “I am he, the one who speaks to you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>At this, his disciples came. They marveled that he was speaking with a woman; yet no one said, “What are you looking for?” or, “Why do you speak with her?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>So the woman left her water pot, and went away into the city, and said to the people, </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>“Come, see a man who told me everything that I did. Can this be the Christ?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>They went out of the city, and were coming to him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>In the meanwhile, the disciples urged him, saying, “Rabbi, eat.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>But he said to them, “I have food to eat that you don’t know about.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>The disciples therefore said to one another, “Has anyone brought him something to eat?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Jesus said to them, “My food is to do the will of him who sent me, and to accomplish his work. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>Don’t you say, ‘There are yet four months until the harvest?’ Behold, I tell you, lift up your eyes, and look at the fields, that they are white for harvest already. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>He who reaps receives wages, and gathers fruit to eternal life; that both he who sows and he who reaps may rejoice together. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>For in this the saying is true, ‘One sows, and another reaps.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>I sent you to reap that for which you haven’t labored. Others have labored, and you have entered into their labor.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>From that city many of the Samaritans believed in him because of the word of the woman, who testified, “He told me everything that I did.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>So when the Samaritans came to him, they begged him to stay with them. He stayed there two days. </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>Many more believed because of his word. </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>They said to the woman, “Now we believe, not because of your speaking; for we have heard for ourselves, and know that this is indeed the Christ, the Savior of the world.” </div>
