<div class="verse"><span>30</span>I can of myself do nothing. As I hear, I judge, and my judgment is righteous; because I don’t seek my own will, but the will of my Father who sent me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>“If I testify about myself, my witness is not valid. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>It is another who testifies about me. I know that the testimony which he testifies about me is true. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>You have sent to John, and he has testified to the truth. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>But the testimony which I receive is not from man. However, I say these things that you may be saved. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>He was the burning and shining lamp, and you were willing to rejoice for a while in his light. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>But the testimony which I have is greater than that of John, for the works which the Father gave me to accomplish, the very works that I do, testify about me, that the Father has sent me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>The Father himself, who sent me, has testified about me. You have neither heard his voice at any time, nor seen his form. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>You don’t have his word living in you; because you don’t believe him whom he sent. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>“You search the Scriptures, because you think that in them you have eternal life; and these are they which testify about me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>Yet you will not come to me, that you may have life. </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>I don’t receive glory from men. </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>But I know you, that you don’t have God’s love in yourselves. </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>I have come in my Father’s name, and you don’t receive me. If another comes in his own name, you will receive him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>How can you believe, who receive glory from one another, and you don’t seek the glory that comes from the only God? </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>“Don’t think that I will accuse you to the Father. There is one who accuses you, even Moses, on whom you have set your hope. </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>For if you believed Moses, you would believe me; for he wrote about me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>But if you don’t believe his writings, how will you believe my words?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>After these things, Jesus went away to the other side of the sea of Galilee, which is also called the Sea of Tiberias. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>A great multitude followed him, because they saw his signs which he did on those who were sick. </div>
