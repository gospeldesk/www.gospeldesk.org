<div class="verse"><span>3</span>Jesus went up into the mountain, and he sat there with his disciples. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Now the Passover, the feast of the Jews, was at hand. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Jesus therefore lifting up his eyes, and seeing that a great multitude was coming to him, said to Philip, “Where are we to buy bread, that these may eat?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>This he said to test him, for he himself knew what he would do. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Philip answered him, “Two hundred denarii worth of bread is not sufficient for them, that everyone of them may receive a little.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>One of his disciples, Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, said to him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>“There is a boy here who has five barley loaves and two fish, but what are these among so many?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Jesus said, “Have the people sit down.” Now there was much grass in that place. So the men sat down, in number about five thousand. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>Jesus took the loaves; and having given thanks, he distributed to the disciples, and the disciples to those who were sitting down; likewise also of the fish as much as they desired. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>When they were filled, he said to his disciples, “Gather up the broken pieces which are left over, that nothing be lost.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>So they gathered them up, and filled twelve baskets with broken pieces from the five barley loaves, which were left over by those who had eaten. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>When therefore the people saw the sign which Jesus did, they said, “This is truly the prophet who comes into the world.” </div>
