<div class="verse"><span>48</span>I am the bread of life. </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, and they died. </div> <div class="verse"><span>50</span>This is the bread which comes down out of heaven, that anyone may eat of it and not die. </div> <div class="verse"><span>51</span>I am the living bread which came down out of heaven. If anyone eats of this bread, he will live forever. Yes, the bread which I will give for the life of the world is my flesh.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>52</span>The Jews therefore contended with one another, saying, “How can this man give us his flesh to eat?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>53</span>Jesus therefore said to them, “Most certainly I tell you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you don’t have life in yourselves. </div> <div class="verse"><span>54</span>He who eats my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day. </div> <div class="verse"><span>55</span>For my flesh is food indeed, and my blood is drink indeed. </div>
