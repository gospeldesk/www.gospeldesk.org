<div class="verse"><span>56</span>He who eats my flesh and drinks my blood lives in me, and I in him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>57</span>As the living Father sent me, and I live because of the Father; so he who feeds on me, he will also live because of me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>58</span>This is the bread which came down out of heaven—not as our fathers ate the manna, and died. He who eats this bread will live forever.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>59</span>He said these things in the synagogue, as he taught in Capernaum. </div> <div class="verse"><span>60</span>Therefore many of his disciples, when they heard this, said, “This is a hard saying! Who can listen to it?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>61</span>But Jesus knowing in himself that his disciples murmured at this, said to them, “Does this cause you to stumble? </div> <div class="verse"><span>62</span>Then what if you would see the Son of Man ascending to where he was before? </div> <div class="verse"><span>63</span>It is the spirit who gives life. The flesh profits nothing. The words that I speak to you are spirit, and are life. </div> <div class="verse"><span>64</span>But there are some of you who don’t believe.” For Jesus knew from the beginning who they were who didn’t believe, and who it was who would betray him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>65</span>He said, “For this cause I have said to you that no one can come to me, unless it is given to him by my Father.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>66</span>At this, many of his disciples went back, and walked no more with him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>67</span>Jesus said therefore to the twelve, “You don’t also want to go away, do you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>68</span>Simon Peter answered him, “Lord, to whom would we go? You have the words of eternal life. </div> <div class="verse"><span>69</span>We have come to believe and know that you are the Christ, the Son of the living God.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>70</span>Jesus answered them, “Didn’t I choose you, the twelve, and one of you is a devil?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>71</span>Now he spoke of Judas, the son of Simon Iscariot, for it was he who would betray him, being one of the twelve. </div>
