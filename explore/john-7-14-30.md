<div class="verse"><span>14</span>But when it was now the middle of the feast, Jesus went up into the temple and taught. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>The Jews therefore marveled, saying, “How does this man know letters, having never been educated?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>Jesus therefore answered them, “My teaching is not mine, but his who sent me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>If anyone desires to do his will, he will know about the teaching, whether it is from God, or if I am speaking from myself. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>He who speaks from himself seeks his own glory, but he who seeks the glory of him who sent him is true, and no unrighteousness is in him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Didn’t Moses give you the law, and yet none of you keeps the law? Why do you seek to kill me?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>The multitude answered, “You have a demon! Who seeks to kill you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>Jesus answered them, “I did one work, and you all marvel because of it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Moses has given you circumcision (not that it is of Moses, but of the fathers), and on the Sabbath you circumcise a boy. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>If a boy receives circumcision on the Sabbath, that the law of Moses may not be broken, are you angry with me, because I made a man completely healthy on the Sabbath? </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Don’t judge according to appearance, but judge righteous judgment.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Therefore some of them of Jerusalem said, “Isn’t this he whom they seek to kill? </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Behold, he speaks openly, and they say nothing to him. Can it be that the rulers indeed know that this is truly the Christ? </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>However we know where this man comes from, but when the Christ comes, no one will know where he comes from.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>Jesus therefore cried out in the temple, teaching and saying, “You both know me, and know where I am from. I have not come of myself, but he who sent me is true, whom you don’t know. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>I know him, because I am from him, and he sent me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>They sought therefore to take him; but no one laid a hand on him, because his hour had not yet come. </div>
