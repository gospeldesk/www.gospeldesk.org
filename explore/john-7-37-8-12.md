<div class="verse"><span>37</span>Now on the last and greatest day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried out, “If anyone is thirsty, let him come to me and drink! </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>He who believes in me, as the Scripture has said, from within him will flow rivers of living water.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>But he said this about the Spirit, which those believing in him were to receive. For the Holy Spirit was not yet given, because Jesus wasn’t yet glorified. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>Many of the multitude therefore, when they heard these words, said, “This is truly the prophet.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>Others said, “This is the Christ.” But some said, “What, does the Christ come out of Galilee? </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>Hasn’t the Scripture said that the Christ comes of the offspring of David, and from Bethlehem, the village where David was?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>So there arose a division in the multitude because of him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>Some of them would have arrested him, but no one laid hands on him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>The officers therefore came to the chief priests and Pharisees, and they said to them, “Why didn’t you bring him?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>The officers answered, “No man ever spoke like this man!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>The Pharisees therefore answered them, “You aren’t also led astray, are you? </div> <div class="verse"><span>48</span>Have any of the rulers believed in him, or of the Pharisees? </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>But this multitude that doesn’t know the law is accursed.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>50</span>Nicodemus (he who came to him by night, being one of them) said to them, </div> <div class="verse"><span>51</span>“Does our law judge a man, unless it first hears from him personally and knows what he does?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>52</span>They answered him, “Are you also from Galilee? Search, and see that no prophet has arisen out of Galilee.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>53</span>Everyone went to his own house, </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>Again, therefore, Jesus spoke to them, saying, “I am the light of the world. He who follows me will not walk in the darkness, but will have the light of life.” </div>
