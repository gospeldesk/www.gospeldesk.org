<div class="verse"><span>31</span>Jesus therefore said to those Jews who had believed him, “If you remain in my word, then you are truly my disciples. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>You will know the truth, and the truth will make you free.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>They answered him, “We are Abraham’s offspring, and have never been in bondage to anyone. How do you say, ‘You will be made free’ ?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Jesus answered them, “Most certainly I tell you, everyone who commits sin is the bondservant of sin. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>A bondservant doesn’t live in the house forever. A son remains forever. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>If therefore the Son makes you free, you will be free indeed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>I know that you are Abraham’s offspring, yet you seek to kill me, because my word finds no place in you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>I say the things which I have seen with my Father; and you also do the things which you have seen with your father.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>They answered him, “Our father is Abraham.” Jesus said to them, “If you were Abraham’s children, you would do the works of Abraham. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>But now you seek to kill me, a man who has told you the truth, which I heard from God. Abraham didn’t do this. </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>You do the works of your father.” They said to him, “We were not born of sexual immorality. We have one Father, God.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>Therefore Jesus said to them, “If God were your father, you would love me, for I came out and have come from God. For I haven’t come of myself, but he sent me. </div>
