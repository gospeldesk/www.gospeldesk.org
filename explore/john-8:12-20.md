<div class="verse"><span>12</span>Again, therefore, Jesus spoke to them, saying, “I am the light of the world. Your testimony is not valid.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Jesus answered them, “Even if I testify about myself, my testimony is true, for I know where I came from, and where I am going; but you don’t know where I came from, or where I am going. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>You judge according to the flesh. If you knew me, you would know my Father also.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Jesus spoke these words in the treasury, as he taught in the temple.
