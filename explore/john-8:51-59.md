<div class="verse"><span>51</span>Most certainly, I tell you, if a person keeps my word, he will never see death.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>52</span>Then the Jews said to him, “Now we know that you have a demon. Abraham died, and the prophets; and you say, ‘If a man keeps my word, he will never taste of death.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>53</span>Are you greater than our father, Abraham, who died? The prophets died. Who do you make yourself out to be?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>54</span>Jesus answered, “If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing. It is my Father who glorifies me, of whom you say that he is our God. </div> <div class="verse"><span>55</span>You have not known him, but I know him. If I said, ‘I don’t know him,’ I would be like you, a liar. But I know him, and keep his word. </div> <div class="verse"><span>56</span>Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day. He saw it, and was glad.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>57</span>The Jews therefore said to him, “You are not yet fifty years old, and have you seen Abraham?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>58</span>Jesus said to them, “Most certainly, I tell you, before Abraham came into existence, I AM. ” </div> <div class="verse"><span>59</span>Therefore they took up stones to throw at him, but Jesus was hidden, and went out of the temple, having gone through the middle of them, and so passed by. </div>
