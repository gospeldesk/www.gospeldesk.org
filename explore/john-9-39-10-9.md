<div class="passage">
<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">39</span>Jesus said, “I came into this world for judgment, that those who don’t see may see; and that those who see may become blind.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">40</span>Those of the Pharisees who were with him heard these things, and said to him, “Are we also blind?” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">41</span>Jesus said to them, “If you were blind, you would have no sin; but now you say, ‘We see.’ Therefore your sin remains. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">1</span>“Most certainly, I tell you, one who doesn’t enter by the door into the sheep fold, but climbs up some other way, the same is a thief and a robber. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">2</span>But one who enters in by the door is the shepherd of the sheep. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">3</span>The gatekeeper opens the gate for him, and the sheep listen to his voice. He calls his own sheep by name, and leads them out. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">4</span>Whenever he brings out his own sheep, he goes before them, and the sheep follow him, for they know his voice. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">5</span>They will by no means follow a stranger, but will flee from him; for they don’t know the voice of strangers.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">6</span>Jesus spoke this parable to them, but they didn’t understand what he was telling them. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">7</span>Jesus therefore said to them again, “Most certainly, I tell you, I am the sheep’s door. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">8</span>All who came before me are thieves and robbers, but the sheep didn’t listen to them. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">9</span>I am the door. If anyone enters in by me, he will be saved, and will go in and go out, and will find pasture. </span></p>
