<div class="verse"><span>1</span>Since many have undertaken to set in order a narrative concerning those matters which have been fulfilled among us, </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>even as those who from the beginning were eyewitnesses and servants of the word delivered them to us, </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>it seemed good to me also, having traced the course of all things accurately from the first, to write to you in order, most excellent Theophilus; </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>that you might know the certainty concerning the things in which you were instructed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>There was in the days of Herod, the king of Judea, a certain priest named Zacharias, of the priestly division of Abijah. He had a wife of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elizabeth. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>They were both righteous before God, walking blamelessly in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>But they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and they both were well advanced in years. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>Now while he executed the priest’s office before God in the order of his division, </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>according to the custom of the priest’s office, his lot was to enter into the temple of the Lord and burn incense. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>The whole multitude of the people were praying outside at the hour of incense. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>An angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing on the right side of the altar of incense. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>Zacharias was troubled when he saw him, and fear fell upon him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>But the angel said to him, “Don’t be afraid, Zacharias, because your request has been heard, and your wife, Elizabeth, will bear you a son, and you shall call his name John. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>You will have joy and gladness; and many will rejoice at his birth. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>For he will be great in the sight of the Lord, and he will drink no wine nor strong drink. He will be filled with the Holy Spirit, even from his mother’s womb. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>He will turn many of the children of Israel to the Lord, their God. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>He will go before him in the spirit and power of Elijah, ‘to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children,’ and the disobedient to the wisdom of the just; to prepare a people prepared for the Lord.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Zacharias said to the angel, “How can I be sure of this? For I am an old man, and my wife is well advanced in years.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>The angel answered him, “I am Gabriel, who stands in the presence of God. I was sent to speak to you, and to bring you this good news. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Behold, you will be silent and not able to speak, until the day that these things will happen, because you didn’t believe my words, which will be fulfilled in their proper time.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>The people were waiting for Zacharias, and they marveled that he delayed in the temple. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>When he came out, he could not speak to them, and they perceived that he had seen a vision in the temple. He continued making signs to them, and remained mute. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>When the days of his service were fulfilled, he departed to his house. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>After these days Elizabeth, his wife, conceived, and she hid herself five months, saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>“Thus has the Lord done to me in the days in which he looked at me, to take away my reproach among men.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Now in the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city of Galilee, named Nazareth, </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>to a virgin pledged to be married to a man whose name was Joseph, of David’s house. The virgin’s name was Mary. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>Having come in, the angel said to her, “Rejoice, you highly favored one! The Lord is with you. Blessed are you among women!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>But when she saw him, she was greatly troubled at the saying, and considered what kind of salutation this might be. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>The angel said to her, “Don’t be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Behold, you will conceive in your womb, and give birth to a son, and will call his name ‘Jesus.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>He will be great, and will be called the Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of his father, David, </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>and he will reign over the house of Jacob forever. There will be no end to his Kingdom.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Mary said to the angel, “How can this be, seeing I am a virgin?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>The angel answered her, “The Holy Spirit will come on you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. Therefore also the holy one who is born from you will be called the Son of God. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>Behold, Elizabeth, your relative, also has conceived a son in her old age; and this is the sixth month with her who was called barren. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>For nothing spoken by God is impossible.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>Mary said, “Behold, the servant of the Lord; let it be done to me according to your word.” The angel departed from her. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>Mary arose in those days and went into the hill country with haste, into a city of Judah, </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>and entered into the house of Zacharias and greeted Elizabeth. </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>When Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, the baby leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit. </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>She called out with a loud voice, and said, “Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb! </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>Why am I so favored, that the mother of my Lord should come to me? </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>For behold, when the voice of your greeting came into my ears, the baby leaped in my womb for joy! </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>Blessed is she who believed, for there will be a fulfillment of the things which have been spoken to her from the Lord!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>Mary said, “My soul magnifies the Lord. </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>My spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior, </div> <div class="verse"><span>48</span>for he has looked at the humble state of his servant. For behold, from now on, all generations will call me blessed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>For he who is mighty has done great things for me. Holy is his name. </div> <div class="verse"><span>50</span>His mercy is for generations of generations on those who fear him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>51</span>He has shown strength with his arm. He has scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts. </div> <div class="verse"><span>52</span>He has put down princes from their thrones. And has exalted the lowly. </div> <div class="verse"><span>53</span>He has filled the hungry with good things. He has sent the rich away empty. </div> <div class="verse"><span>54</span>He has given help to Israel, his servant, that he might remember mercy, </div> <div class="verse"><span>55</span>As he spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and his offspring forever.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>56</span>Mary stayed with her about three months, and then returned to her house. </div> <div class="verse"><span>57</span>Now the time that Elizabeth should give birth was fulfilled, and she gave birth to a son. </div> <div class="verse"><span>58</span>Her neighbors and her relatives heard that the Lord had magnified his mercy towards her, and they rejoiced with her. </div> <div class="verse"><span>59</span>On the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child; and they would have called him Zacharias, after the name of his father. </div> <div class="verse"><span>60</span>His mother answered, “Not so; but he will be called John.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>61</span>They said to her, “There is no one among your relatives who is called by this name.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>62</span>They made signs to his father, what he would have him called. </div> <div class="verse"><span>63</span>He asked for a writing tablet, and wrote, “His name is John.” They all marveled. </div> <div class="verse"><span>64</span>His mouth was opened immediately, and his tongue freed, and he spoke, blessing God. </div> <div class="verse"><span>65</span>Fear came on all who lived around them, and all these sayings were talked about throughout all the hill country of Judea. </div> <div class="verse"><span>66</span>All who heard them laid them up in their heart, saying, “What then will this child be?” The hand of the Lord was with him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>67</span>His father, Zacharias, was filled with the Holy Spirit, and prophesied, saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>68</span>“Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, for he has visited and redeemed his people; </div> <div class="verse"><span>69</span>and has raised up a horn of salvation for us in the house of his servant David </div> <div class="verse"><span>70</span>(as he spoke by the mouth of his holy prophets who have been from of old), </div> <div class="verse"><span>71</span>salvation from our enemies, and from the hand of all who hate us; </div> <div class="verse"><span>72</span>to show mercy towards our fathers, to remember his holy covenant, </div> <div class="verse"><span>73</span>the oath which he swore to Abraham, our father, </div> <div class="verse"><span>74</span>to grant to us that we, being delivered out of the hand of our enemies, should serve him without fear, </div> <div class="verse"><span>75</span>In holiness and righteousness before him all the days of our life. </div> <div class="verse"><span>76</span>And you, child, will be called a prophet of the Most High, for you will go before the face of the Lord to prepare his ways, </div> <div class="verse"><span>77</span>to give knowledge of salvation to his people by the remission of their sins, </div> <div class="verse"><span>78</span>because of the tender mercy of our God, whereby the dawn from on high will visit us, </div> <div class="verse"><span>79</span>to shine on those who sit in darkness and the shadow of death; to guide our feet into the way of peace.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>80</span>The child was growing, and becoming strong in spirit, and was in the desert until the day of his public appearance to Israel. </div>
