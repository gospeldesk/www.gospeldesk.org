<div class="verse"><span>1</span>Now after these things, the Lord also appointed seventy others, and sent them two by two ahead of him </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Then he said to them, “The harvest is indeed plentiful, but the laborers are few. Pray therefore to the Lord of the harvest, that he may send out laborers into his harvest. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>Go your ways. Behold, I send you out as lambs among wolves. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Carry no purse, nor wallet, nor sandals. Greet no one on the way. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Into whatever house you enter, first say, ‘Peace be to this house.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>If a son of peace is there, your peace will rest on him; but if not, it will return to you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Remain in that same house, eating and drinking the things they give, for the laborer is worthy of his wages. Don’t go from house to house. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>Into whatever city you enter, and they receive you, eat the things that are set before you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>Heal the sick who are therein, and tell them, ‘God’s Kingdom has come near to you.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>But into whatever city you enter, and they don’t receive you, go out into its streets and say, </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>‘Even the dust from your city that clings to us, we wipe off against you. Nevertheless know this, that God’s Kingdom has come near to you.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>I tell you, it will be more tolerable in that day for Sodom than for that city. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>“Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! For if the mighty works had been done in Tyre and Sidon which were done in you, they would have repented long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>But it will be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon in the judgment than for you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>You, Capernaum, who are exalted to heaven, will be brought down to Hades. </div>
