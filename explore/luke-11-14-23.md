<div class="verse"><span>14</span>He was casting out a demon, and it was mute. When the demon had gone out, the mute man spoke; and the multitudes marveled. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>But some of them said, “He casts out demons by Beelzebul, the prince of the demons.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>Others, testing him, sought from him a sign from heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>But he, knowing their thoughts, said to them, “Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation. A house divided against itself falls. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>If Satan also is divided against himself, how will his kingdom stand? For you say that I cast out demons by Beelzebul. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>But if I cast out demons by Beelzebul, by whom do your children cast them out? Therefore will they be your judges. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>But if I by God’s finger cast out demons, then God’s Kingdom has come to you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>“When the strong man, fully armed, guards his own dwelling, his goods are safe. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>But when someone stronger attacks him and overcomes him, he takes from him his whole armor in which he trusted, and divides his plunder. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>“He that is not with me is against me. He who doesn’t gather with me scatters. </div>
