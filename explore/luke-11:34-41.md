<div class="verse"><span>34</span>The lamp of the body is the eye. He went in, and sat at the table. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>When the Pharisee saw it, he marveled that he had not first washed himself before dinner. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>The Lord said to him, “Now you Pharisees cleanse the outside of the cup and of the platter, but your inward part is full of extortion and wickedness. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>You foolish ones, didn’t he who made the outside make the inside also? </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>But give for gifts to the needy those things which are within, and behold, all things will be clean to you. </div>
