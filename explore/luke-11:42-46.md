For you are like hidden graves, and the men who walk over them don’t know it.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>One of the lawyers answered him, “Teacher, in saying this you insult us also.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>He said, “Woe to you lawyers also!
