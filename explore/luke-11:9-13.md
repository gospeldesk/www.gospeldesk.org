<div class="verse"><span>9</span>“I tell you, keep asking, and it will be given you. Keep seeking, and you will find. To him who knocks it will be opened. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>“Which of you fathers, if your son asks for bread, will give him a stone? Or if he asks for a fish, he won’t give him a snake instead of a fish, will he? </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>Or if he asks for an egg, he won’t give him a scorpion, will he? </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>If you then, being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him?” </div>
