<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">13</span>One of the multitude said to him, “Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">14</span>But he said to him, “Man, who made me a judge or an arbitrator over you?” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">15</span>He said to them, “Beware! Keep yourselves from covetousness, for a man’s life doesn’t consist of the abundance of the things which he possesses.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">22</span>He said to his disciples, “Therefore I tell you, don’t be anxious for your life, what you will eat, nor yet for your body, what you will wear. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">23</span>Life is more than food, and the body is more than clothing. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">24</span>Consider the ravens: they don’t sow, they don’t reap, they have no warehouse or barn, and God feeds them. How much more valuable are you than birds! </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">25</span>Which of you by being anxious can add a cubit to his height? </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">26</span>If then you aren’t able to do even the least things, why are you anxious about the rest? </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">27</span>Consider the lilies, how they grow. They don’t toil, neither do they spin; yet I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">28</span>But if this is how God clothes the grass in the field, which today exists, and tomorrow is cast into the oven, how much more will he clothe you, O you of little faith? </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">29</span>Don’t seek what you will eat or what you will drink; neither be anxious. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">30</span>For the nations of the world seek after all of these things, but your Father knows that you need these things. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">31</span>But seek God’s Kingdom, and all these things will be added to you. </span></p>
