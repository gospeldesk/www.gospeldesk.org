<div class="verse"><span>16</span>He spoke a parable to them, saying, “The ground of a certain rich man produced abundantly. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>He reasoned within himself, saying, ‘What will I do, because I don’t have room to store my crops?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>He said, ‘This is what I will do. I will pull down my barns, and build bigger ones, and there I will store all my grain and my goods. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>I will tell my soul, “Soul, you have many goods laid up for many years. Take your ease, eat, drink, be merry.”’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>“But God said to him, ‘You foolish one, tonight your soul is required of you. The things which you have prepared—whose will they be?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>So is he who lays up treasure for himself, and is not rich toward God.” </div>
