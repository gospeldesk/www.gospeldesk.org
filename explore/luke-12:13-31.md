<div class="verse"><span>13</span>One of the multitude said to him, “Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>But he said to him, “Man, who made me a judge or an arbitrator over you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>He said to them, “Beware! Keep yourselves from covetousness, for a man’s life doesn’t consist of the abundance of the things which he possesses.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>He said to his disciples, “Therefore I tell you, don’t be anxious for your life, what you will eat, nor yet for your body, what you will wear. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>Life is more than food, and the body is more than clothing. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Consider the ravens: they don’t sow, they don’t reap, they have no warehouse or barn, and God feeds them. How much more valuable are you than birds! </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Which of you by being anxious can add a cubit </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>If then you aren’t able to do even the least things, why are you anxious about the rest? </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>Consider the lilies, how they grow. They don’t toil, neither do they spin; yet I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>But if this is how God clothes the grass in the field, which today exists, and tomorrow is cast into the oven, how much more will he clothe you, O you of little faith? </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>Don’t seek what you will eat or what you will drink; neither be anxious. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>For the nations of the world seek after all of these things, but your Father knows that you need these things. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>But seek God’s Kingdom, and all these things will be added to you. </div>
