<div class="verse"><span>32</span>Don’t be afraid, little flock, for it is your Father’s good pleasure to give you the Kingdom. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>Sell that which you have, and give gifts to the needy. Make for yourselves purses which don’t grow old, a treasure in the heavens that doesn’t fail, where no thief approaches, neither moth destroys. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>“Let your waist be dressed and your lamps burning. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>Be like men watching for their lord, when he returns from the marriage feast; that, when he comes and knocks, they may immediately open to him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>Blessed are those servants, whom the lord will find watching when he comes. Most certainly I tell you, that he will dress himself, and make them recline, and will come and serve them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>They will be blessed if he comes in the second or third watch, and finds them so. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what hour the thief was coming, he would have watched, and not allowed his house to be broken into. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>Therefore be ready also, for the Son of Man is coming in an hour that you don’t expect him.” </div>
