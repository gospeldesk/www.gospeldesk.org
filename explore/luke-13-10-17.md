<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">10</span>He was teaching in one of the synagogues on the Sabbath day. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">11</span>Behold, there was a woman who had a spirit of infirmity eighteen years, and she was bent over, and could in no way straighten herself up. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">12</span>When Jesus saw her, he called her, and said to her, “Woman, you are freed from your infirmity.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">13</span>He laid his hands on her, and immediately she stood up straight, and glorified God. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">14</span>The ruler of the synagogue, being indignant because Jesus had healed on the Sabbath, said to the multitude, “There are six days in which men ought to work. Therefore come on those days and be healed, and not on the Sabbath day!” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">15</span>Therefore the Lord answered him, “You hypocrites! Doesn’t each one of you free his ox or his donkey from the stall on the Sabbath, and lead him away to water? </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">16</span>Ought not this woman, being a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan had bound eighteen long years, be freed from this bondage on the Sabbath day?” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">17</span>As he said these things, all his adversaries were disappointed, and all the multitude rejoiced for all the glorious things that were done by him. </span></p>
