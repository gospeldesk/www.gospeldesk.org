<div class="verse"><span>1</span>Now there were some present at the same time who told him about the Galileans, whose blood Pilate had mixed with their sacrifices. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Jesus answered them, “Do you think that these Galileans were worse sinners than all the other Galileans, because they suffered such things? </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>I tell you, no, but unless you repent, you will all perish in the same way. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Or those eighteen, on whom the tower in Siloam fell, and killed them; do you think that they were worse offenders than all the men who dwell in Jerusalem? </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>I tell you, no, but, unless you repent, you will all perish in the same way.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>He spoke this parable. “A certain man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard, and he came seeking fruit on it, and found none. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>He said to the vine dresser, ‘Behold, these three years I have come looking for fruit on this fig tree, and found none. Cut it down. Why does it waste the soil?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>He answered, ‘Lord, leave it alone this year also, until I dig around it, and fertilize it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>If it bears fruit, fine; but if not, after that, you can cut it down.’” </div>
