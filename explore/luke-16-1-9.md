<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">1</span>He also said to his disciples, “There was a certain rich man who had a manager. An accusation was made to him that this man was wasting his possessions. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">2</span>He called him, and said to him, ‘What is this that I hear about you? Give an accounting of your management, for you can no longer be manager.’ </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">3</span>“The manager said within himself, ‘What will I do, seeing that my lord is taking away the management position from me? I don’t have strength to dig. I am ashamed to beg. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">4</span>I know what I will do, so that when I am removed from management, they may receive me into their houses.’ </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">5</span>Calling each one of his lord’s debtors to him, he said to the first, ‘How much do you owe to my lord?’ </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">6</span>He said, ‘A hundred batos of oil.’ He said to him, ‘Take your bill, and sit down quickly and write fifty.’ </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">7</span>Then he said to another, ‘How much do you owe?’ He said, ‘A hundred cors of wheat.’ He said to him, ‘Take your bill, and write eighty.’ </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">8</span>“His lord commended the dishonest manager because he had done wisely, for the children of this world are, in their own generation, wiser than the children of the light. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">9</span>I tell you, make for yourselves friends by means of unrighteous mammon, so that when you fail, they may receive you into the eternal tents. </span></p>
