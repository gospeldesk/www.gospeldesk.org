<div class="verse"><span>1</span>He also said to his disciples, “There was a certain rich man who had a manager. An accusation was made to him that this man was wasting his possessions. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>He called him, and said to him, ‘What is this that I hear about you? Give an accounting of your management, for you can no longer be manager.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>“The manager said within himself, ‘What will I do, seeing that my lord is taking away the management position from me?
