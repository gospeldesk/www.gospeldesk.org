<div class="verse"><span>11</span>As he was on his way to Jerusalem, he was passing along the borders of Samaria and Galilee. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>As he entered into a certain village, ten men who were lepers met him, who stood at a distance. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>They lifted up their voices, saying, “Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>When he saw them, he said to them, “Go and show yourselves to the priests.” As they went, they were cleansed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>One of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back, glorifying God with a loud voice. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>He fell on his face at Jesus’ feet, giving him thanks; and he was a Samaritan. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Jesus answered, “Weren’t the ten cleansed? But where are the nine? </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Were there none found who returned to give glory to God, except this stranger?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Then he said to him, “Get up, and go your way. Your faith has healed you.” </div>
