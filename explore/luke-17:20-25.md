<div class="verse"><span>20</span>Being asked by the Pharisees when God’s Kingdom would come, he answered them, “God’s Kingdom doesn’t come with observation; </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>neither will they say, ‘Look, here!’ or, ‘Look, there!’ for behold, God’s Kingdom is within you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>He said to the disciples, “The days will come, when you will desire to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and you will not see it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>They will tell you, ‘Look, here!’ or ‘Look, there!’ Don’t go away, nor follow after them, </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>for as the lightning, when it flashes out of the one part under the sky, shines to the other part under the sky; so will the Son of Man be in his day. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>But first, he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation. </div>
