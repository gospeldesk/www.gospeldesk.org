<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">35</span>As he came near Jericho, a certain blind man sat by the road, begging. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">36</span>Hearing a multitude going by, he asked what this meant. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">37</span>They told him that Jesus of Nazareth was passing by. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">38</span>He cried out, “Jesus, you son of David, have mercy on me!” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">39</span>Those who led the way rebuked him, that he should be quiet; but he cried out all the more, “You son of David, have mercy on me!” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">40</span>Standing still, Jesus commanded him to be brought to him. When he had come near, he asked him, </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">41</span>“What do you want me to do?” He said, “Lord, that I may see again.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">42</span>Jesus said to him, “Receive your sight. Your faith has healed you.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">43</span>Immediately he received his sight, and followed him, glorifying God. All the people, when they saw it, praised God. </span></p>
