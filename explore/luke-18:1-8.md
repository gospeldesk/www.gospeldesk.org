<div class="verse"><span>1</span>He also spoke a parable to them that they must always pray, and not give up, </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>saying, “There was a judge in a certain city who didn’t fear God, and didn’t respect man. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>A widow was in that city, and she often came to him, saying, ‘Defend me from my adversary!’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>He wouldn’t for a while, but afterward he said to himself, ‘Though I neither fear God, nor respect man, </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>yet because this widow bothers me, I will defend her, or else she will wear me out by her continual coming.’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>The Lord said, “Listen to what the unrighteous judge says. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Won’t God avenge his chosen ones, who are crying out to him day and night, and yet he exercises patience with them? </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>I tell you that he will avenge them quickly. Nevertheless, when the Son of Man comes, will he find faith on the earth?” </div>
