<div class="verse"><span>15</span>They were also bringing their babies to him, that he might touch them. But when the disciples saw it, they rebuked them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>Jesus summoned them, saying, “Allow the little children to come to me, and don’t hinder them, for God’s Kingdom belongs to such as these. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Most certainly, I tell you, whoever doesn’t receive God’s Kingdom like a little child, he will in no way enter into it.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Those who heard it said, “Then who can be saved?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>But he said, “The things which are impossible with men are possible with God.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>Peter said, “Look, we have left everything, and followed you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>He said to them, “Most certainly I tell you, there is no one who has left house, or wife, or brothers, or parents, or children, for God’s Kingdom’s sake, </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>who will not receive many times more in this time, and in the world to come, eternal life.” </div>
