<div class="verse"><span>35</span>As he came near Jericho, a certain blind man sat by the road, begging. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>Hearing a multitude going by, he asked what this meant. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>They told him that Jesus of Nazareth was passing by. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>He cried out, “Jesus, you son of David, have mercy on me!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>Those who led the way rebuked him, that he should be quiet; but he cried out all the more, “You son of David, have mercy on me!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>Standing still, Jesus commanded him to be brought to him. When he had come near, he asked him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>“What do you want me to do?” He said, “Lord, that I may see again.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>Jesus said to him, “Receive your sight. Your faith has healed you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>Immediately he received his sight, and followed him, glorifying God. All the people, when they saw it, praised God. </div>
