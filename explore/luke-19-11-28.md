<div class="verse"><span>11</span>As they heard these things, he went on and told a parable, because he was near Jerusalem, and they supposed that God’s Kingdom would be revealed immediately. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>He said therefore, “A certain nobleman went into a far country to receive for himself a kingdom, and to return. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>He called ten servants of his, and gave them ten mina coins, and told them, ‘Conduct business until I come.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>But his citizens hated him, and sent an envoy after him, saying, ‘We don’t want this man to reign over us.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>“When he had come back again, having received the kingdom, he commanded these servants, to whom he had given the money, to be called to him, that he might know what they had gained by conducting business. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>The first came before him, saying, ‘Lord, your mina has made ten more minas.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>“He said to him, ‘Well done, you good servant! Because you were found faithful with very little, you shall have authority over ten cities.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>“The second came, saying, ‘Your mina, Lord, has made five minas.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>“So he said to him, ‘And you are to be over five cities.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Another came, saying, ‘Lord, behold, your mina, which I kept laid away in a handkerchief, </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>for I feared you, because you are an exacting man. You take up that which you didn’t lay down, and reap that which you didn’t sow.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>“He said to him, ‘Out of your own mouth will I judge you, you wicked servant! You knew that I am an exacting man, taking up that which I didn’t lay down, and reaping that which I didn’t sow. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>Then why didn’t you deposit my money in the bank, and at my coming, I might have earned interest on it?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>He said to those who stood by, ‘Take the mina away from him, and give it to him who has the ten minas.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>“They said to him, ‘Lord, he has ten minas!’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>‘For I tell you that to everyone who has, will more be given; but from him who doesn’t have, even that which he has will be taken away from him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>But bring those enemies of mine who didn’t want me to reign over them here, and kill them before me.’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>Having said these things, he went on ahead, going up to Jerusalem. </div>
