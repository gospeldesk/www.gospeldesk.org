<div class="verse"><span>29</span>When he came near to Bethsphage </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>saying, “Go your way into the village on the other side, in which, as you enter, you will find a colt tied, which no man had ever sat upon. Untie it, and bring it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>If anyone asks you, ‘Why are you untying it?’ say to him: ‘The Lord needs it.’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>Those who were sent went away, and found things just as he had told them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>As they were untying the colt, its owners said to them, “Why are you untying the colt?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>They said, “The Lord needs it.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>They brought it to Jesus. They threw their cloaks on the colt, and set Jesus on them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>As he went, they spread their cloaks on the road. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>As he was now getting near, at the descent of the Mount of Olives, the whole multitude of the disciples began to rejoice and praise God with a loud voice for all the mighty works which they had seen, </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>saying, “Blessed is the King who comes in the name of the Lord! Peace in heaven, and glory in the highest!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>Some of the Pharisees from the multitude said to him, “Teacher, rebuke your disciples!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>He answered them, “I tell you that if these were silent, the stones would cry out.” </div>
