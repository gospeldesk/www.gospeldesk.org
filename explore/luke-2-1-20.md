<div class="verse"><span>1</span>Now in those days, a decree went out from Caesar Augustus that all the world should be enrolled. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>This was the first enrollment made when Quirinius was governor of Syria. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>All went to enroll themselves, everyone to his own city. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth, into Judea, to David’s city, which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family of David; </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>to enroll himself with Mary, who was pledged to be married to him as wife, being pregnant. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>While they were there, the day had come for her to give birth. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>She gave birth to her firstborn son. She wrapped him in bands of cloth, and laid him in a feeding trough, because there was no room for them in the inn. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>There were shepherds in the same country staying in the field, and keeping watch by night over their flock. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>Behold, an angel of the Lord stood by them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were terrified. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>The angel said to them, “Don’t be afraid, for behold, I bring you good news of great joy which will be to all the people. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>For there is born to you today, in David’s city, a Savior, who is Christ the Lord. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>This is the sign to you: you will find a baby wrapped in strips of cloth, lying in a feeding trough.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Suddenly, there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army praising God, and saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>“Glory to God in the highest, on earth peace, good will toward men.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>When the angels went away from them into the sky, the shepherds said to one another, “Let’s go to Bethlehem, now, and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has made known to us.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>They came with haste, and found both Mary and Joseph, and the baby was lying in the feeding trough. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>When they saw it, they publicized widely the saying which was spoken to them about this child. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>All who heard it wondered at the things which were spoken to them by the shepherds. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>But Mary kept all these sayings, pondering them in her heart. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>The shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things that they had heard and seen, just as it was told them. </div>
