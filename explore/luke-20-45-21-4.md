<div class="verse"><span>45</span>In the hearing of all the people, he said to his disciples, </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>“Beware of those scribes who like to walk in long robes, and love greetings in the marketplaces, the best seats in the synagogues, and the best places at feasts; </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>who devour widows’ houses, and for a pretense make long prayers: these will receive greater condemnation.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>He looked up, and saw the rich people who were putting their gifts into the treasury. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>He saw a certain poor widow casting in two small brass coins. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>He said, “Truly I tell you, this poor widow put in more than all of them, </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>for all these put in gifts for God from their abundance, but she, out of her poverty, put in all that she had to live on.” </div>
