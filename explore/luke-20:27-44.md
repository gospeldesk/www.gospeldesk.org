<div class="verse"><span>27</span>Some of the Sadducees came to him, those who deny that there is a resurrection. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>They asked him, “Teacher, Moses wrote to us that if a man’s brother dies having a wife, and he is childless, his brother should take the wife, and raise up children for his brother. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>There were therefore seven brothers. The first took a wife, and died childless. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>The second took her as wife, and he died childless. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>The third took her, and likewise the seven all left no children, and died. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>Afterward the woman also died. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>Therefore in the resurrection whose wife of them will she be? For the seven had her as a wife.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Jesus said to them, “The children of this age marry, and are given in marriage. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>But those who are considered worthy to attain to that age and the resurrection from the dead, neither marry, nor are given in marriage. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>For they can’t die any more, for they are like the angels, and are children of God, being children of the resurrection. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>But that the dead are raised, even Moses showed at the bush, when he called the Lord ‘The God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>Now he is not the God of the dead, but of the living, for all are alive to him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>Some of the scribes answered, “Teacher, you speak well.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>They didn’t dare to ask him any more questions. </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>He said to them, “Why do they say that the Christ is David’s son? </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>David himself says in the book of Psalms, ‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at my right hand, </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>until I make your enemies the footstool of your feet.”’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>“David therefore calls him Lord, so how is he his son?” </div>
