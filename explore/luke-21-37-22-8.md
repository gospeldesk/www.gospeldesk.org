<div class="verse"><span>37</span>Every day Jesus was teaching in the temple, and every night he would go out and spend the night on the mountain that is called Olivet. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>All the people came early in the morning to him in the temple to hear him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>Now the feast of unleavened bread, which is called the Passover, was approaching. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>The chief priests and the scribes sought how they might put him to death, for they feared the people. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>Satan entered into Judas, who was also called Iscariot, who was counted with the twelve. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>He went away, and talked with the chief priests and captains about how he might deliver him to them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>They were glad, and agreed to give him money. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>He consented, and sought an opportunity to deliver him to them in the absence of the multitude. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>The day of unleavened bread came, on which the Passover must be sacrificed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>Jesus sent Peter and John, saying, “Go and prepare the Passover for us, that we may eat.” </div>
