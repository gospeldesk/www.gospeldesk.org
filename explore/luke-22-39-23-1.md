<div class="verse"><span>39</span>He came out, and went, as his custom was, to the Mount of Olives. His disciples also followed him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>When he was at the place, he said to them, “Pray that you don’t enter into temptation.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>He was withdrawn from them about a stone’s throw, and he knelt down and prayed, </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>saying, “Father, if you are willing, remove this cup from me. Nevertheless, not my will, but yours, be done.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>An angel from heaven appeared to him, strengthening him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>Being in agony he prayed more earnestly. His sweat became like great drops of blood falling down on the ground. </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>When he rose up from his prayer, he came to the disciples, and found them sleeping because of grief, </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>and said to them, “Why do you sleep? Rise and pray that you may not enter into temptation.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>While he was still speaking, behold, a multitude, and he who was called Judas, one of the twelve, was leading them. He came near to Jesus to kiss him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>48</span>But Jesus said to him, “Judas, do you betray the Son of Man with a kiss?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>When those who were around him saw what was about to happen, they said to him, “Lord, shall we strike with the sword?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>50</span>A certain one of them struck the servant of the high priest, and cut off his right ear. </div> <div class="verse"><span>51</span>But Jesus answered, “Let me at least do this” —and he touched his ear, and healed him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>52</span>Jesus said to the chief priests, captains of the temple, and elders, who had come against him, “Have you come out as against a robber, with swords and clubs? </div> <div class="verse"><span>53</span>When I was with you in the temple daily, you didn’t stretch out your hands against me. But this is your hour, and the power of darkness.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>54</span>They seized him, and led him away, and brought him into the high priest’s house. But Peter followed from a distance. </div> <div class="verse"><span>55</span>When they had kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard, and had sat down together, Peter sat among them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>56</span>A certain servant girl saw him as he sat in the light, and looking intently at him, said, “This man also was with him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>57</span>He denied Jesus, saying, “Woman, I don’t know him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>58</span>After a little while someone else saw him, and said, “You also are one of them!” But Peter answered, “Man, I am not!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>59</span>After about one hour passed, another confidently affirmed, saying, “Truly this man also was with him, for he is a Galilean!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>60</span>But Peter said, “Man, I don’t know what you are talking about!” Immediately, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>61</span>The Lord turned, and looked at Peter. Then Peter remembered the Lord’s word, how he said to him, “Before the rooster crows you will deny me three times.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>62</span>He went out, and wept bitterly. </div> <div class="verse"><span>63</span>The men who held Jesus mocked him and beat him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>64</span>Having blindfolded him, they struck him on the face and asked him, “Prophesy! Who is the one who struck you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>65</span>They spoke many other things against him, insulting him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>66</span>As soon as it was day, the assembly of the elders of the people were gathered together, both chief priests and scribes, and they led him away into their council, saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>67</span>“If you are the Christ, tell us.” But he said to them, “If I tell you, you won’t believe, </div> <div class="verse"><span>68</span>and if I ask, you will in no way answer me or let me go. </div> <div class="verse"><span>69</span>From now on, the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the power of God.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>70</span>They all said, “Are you then the Son of God?” He said to them, “You say it, because I am.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>71</span>They said, “Why do we need any more witness? For we ourselves have heard from his own mouth!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>The whole company of them rose up and brought him before Pilate. </div>
