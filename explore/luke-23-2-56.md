<div class="verse"><span>2</span>They began to accuse him, saying, “We found this man perverting the nation, forbidding paying taxes to Caesar, and saying that he himself is Christ, a king.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>Pilate asked him, “Are you the King of the Jews?” He answered him, “So you say.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Pilate said to the chief priests and the multitudes, “I find no basis for a charge against this man.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>But they insisted, saying, “He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee even to this place.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>But when Pilate heard Galilee mentioned, he asked if the man was a Galilean. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>When he found out that he was in Herod’s jurisdiction, he sent him to Herod, who was also in Jerusalem during those days. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>Now when Herod saw Jesus, he was exceedingly glad, for he had wanted to see him for a long time, because he had heard many things about him. He hoped to see some miracle done by him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>He questioned him with many words, but he gave no answers. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>The chief priests and the scribes stood, vehemently accusing him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>Herod with his soldiers humiliated him and mocked him. Dressing him in luxurious clothing, they sent him back to Pilate. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>Herod and Pilate became friends with each other that very day, for before that they were enemies with each other. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Pilate called together the chief priests, the rulers, and the people, </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>and said to them, “You brought this man to me as one that perverts the people, and behold, having examined him before you, I found no basis for a charge against this man concerning those things of which you accuse him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Neither has Herod, for I sent you to him, and see, nothing worthy of death has been done by him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>I will therefore chastise him and release him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Now he had to release one prisoner to them at the feast. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>But they all cried out together, saying, “Away with this man! Release to us Barabbas!”— </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>one who was thrown into prison for a certain revolt in the city, and for murder. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Then Pilate spoke to them again, wanting to release Jesus, </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>but they shouted, saying, “Crucify! Crucify him!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>He said to them the third time, “Why? What evil has this man done? I have found no capital crime in him. I will therefore chastise him and release him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>But they were urgent with loud voices, asking that he might be crucified. Their voices and the voices of the chief priests prevailed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Pilate decreed that what they asked for should be done. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>He released him who had been thrown into prison for insurrection and murder, for whom they asked, but he delivered Jesus up to their will. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>When they led him away, they grabbed one Simon of Cyrene, coming from the country, and laid on him the cross, to carry it after Jesus. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>A great multitude of the people followed him, including women who also mourned and lamented him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>But Jesus, turning to them, said, “Daughters of Jerusalem, don’t weep for me, but weep for yourselves and for your children. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>For behold, the days are coming in which they will say, ‘Blessed are the barren, the wombs that never bore, and the breasts that never nursed.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Then they will begin to tell the mountains, ‘Fall on us!’ and tell the hills, ‘Cover us.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>For if they do these things in the green tree, what will be done in the dry?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>There were also others, two criminals, led with him to be put to death. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>When they came to the place that is called The Skull, they crucified him there with the criminals, one on the right and the other on the left. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they don’t know what they are doing.” Dividing his garments among them, they cast lots. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>The people stood watching. The rulers with them also scoffed at him, saying, “He saved others. Let him save himself, if this is the Christ of God, his chosen one!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>The soldiers also mocked him, coming to him and offering him vinegar, </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>and saying, “If you are the King of the Jews, save yourself!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>An inscription was also written over him in letters of Greek, Latin, and Hebrew: “THIS IS THE KING OF THE JEWS.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>One of the criminals who was hanged insulted him, saying, “If you are the Christ, save yourself and us!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>But the other answered, and rebuking him said, “Don’t you even fear God, seeing you are under the same condemnation? </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>And we indeed justly, for we receive the due reward for our deeds, but this man has done nothing wrong.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>He said to Jesus, “Lord, remember me when you come into your Kingdom.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>Jesus said to him, “Assuredly I tell you, today you will be with me in Paradise.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>It was now about the sixth hour, and darkness came over the whole land until the ninth hour. </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>The sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was torn in two. </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>Jesus, crying with a loud voice, said, “Father, into your hands I commit my spirit!” Having said this, he breathed his last. </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>When the centurion saw what was done, he glorified God, saying, “Certainly this was a righteous man.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>48</span>All the multitudes that came together to see this, when they saw the things that were done, returned home beating their breasts. </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>All his acquaintances, and the women who followed with him from Galilee, stood at a distance, watching these things. </div> <div class="verse"><span>50</span>Behold, a man named Joseph, who was a member of the council, a good and righteous man </div> <div class="verse"><span>51</span>(he had not consented to their counsel and deed), from Arimathaea, a city of the Jews, who was also waiting for God’s Kingdom: </div> <div class="verse"><span>52</span>this man went to Pilate, and asked for Jesus’ body. </div> <div class="verse"><span>53</span>He took it down, and wrapped it in a linen cloth, and laid him in a tomb that was cut in stone, where no one had ever been laid. </div> <div class="verse"><span>54</span>It was the day of the Preparation, and the Sabbath was drawing near. </div> <div class="verse"><span>55</span>The women, who had come with him out of Galilee, followed after, and saw the tomb, and how his body was laid. </div> <div class="verse"><span>56</span>They returned, and prepared spices and ointments. On the Sabbath they rested according to the commandment. </div>
