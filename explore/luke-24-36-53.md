<div class="verse"><span>36</span>As they said these things, Jesus himself stood among them, and said to them, “Peace be to you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>But they were terrified and filled with fear, and supposed that they had seen a spirit. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>He said to them, “Why are you troubled? Why do doubts arise in your hearts? </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>See my hands and my feet, that it is truly me. Touch me and see, for a spirit doesn’t have flesh and bones, as you see that I have.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>When he had said this, he showed them his hands and his feet. </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>While they still didn’t believe for joy, and wondered, he said to them, “Do you have anything here to eat?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>They gave him a piece of a broiled fish and some honeycomb. </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>He took them, and ate in front of them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>He said to them, “This is what I told you, while I was still with you, that all things which are written in the law of Moses, the prophets, and the psalms, concerning me must be fulfilled.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>Then he opened their minds, that they might understand the Scriptures. </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>He said to them, “Thus it is written, and thus it was necessary for the Christ to suffer and to rise from the dead the third day, </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>and that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name to all the nations, beginning at Jerusalem. </div> <div class="verse"><span>48</span>You are witnesses of these things. </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>Behold, I send out the promise of my Father on you. But wait in the city of Jerusalem until you are clothed with power from on high.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>50</span>He led them out as far as Bethany, and he lifted up his hands, and blessed them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>51</span>While he blessed them, he withdrew from them, and was carried up into heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>52</span>They worshiped him, and returned to Jerusalem with great joy, </div> <div class="verse"><span>53</span>and were continually in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen. </div>
