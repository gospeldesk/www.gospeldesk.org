<div class="verse"><span>1</span>But on the first day of the week, at early dawn, they and some others came to the tomb, bringing the spices which they had prepared. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>They found the stone rolled away from the tomb. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>They entered in, and didn’t find the Lord Jesus’ body. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>While they were greatly perplexed about this, behold, two men stood by them in dazzling clothing. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Becoming terrified, they bowed their faces down to the earth. They said to them, “Why do you seek the living among the dead? </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>He isn’t here, but is risen. Remember what he told you when he was still in Galilee, </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>saying that the Son of Man must be delivered up into the hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and the third day rise again?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>They remembered his words, </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>returned from the tomb, and told all these things to the eleven, and to all the rest. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Now they were Mary Magdalene, Joanna, and Mary the mother of James. The other women with them told these things to the apostles. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>These words seemed to them to be nonsense, and they didn’t believe them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>But Peter got up and ran to the tomb. Stooping and looking in, he saw the strips of linen lying by themselves, and he departed to his home, wondering what had happened. </div>
