<div class="verse"><span>12</span>But Peter got up and ran to the tomb. Stooping and looking in, he saw the strips of linen lying by themselves, and he departed to his home, wondering what had happened. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Behold, two of them were going that very day to a village named Emmaus, which was sixty stadia </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>They talked with each other about all of these things which had happened. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>While they talked and questioned together, Jesus himself came near, and went with them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>But their eyes were kept from recognizing him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>He said to them, “What are you talking about as you walk, and are sad?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>One of them, named Cleopas, answered him, “Are you the only stranger in Jerusalem who doesn’t know the things which have happened there in these days?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>He said to them, “What things?” They said to him, “The things concerning Jesus, the Nazarene, who was a prophet mighty in deed and word before God and all the people; </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>and how the chief priests and our rulers delivered him up to be condemned to death, and crucified him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>But we were hoping that it was he who would redeem Israel. Yes, and besides all this, it is now the third day since these things happened. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Also, certain women of our company amazed us, having arrived early at the tomb; </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>and when they didn’t find his body, they came saying that they had also seen a vision of angels, who said that he was alive. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Some of us went to the tomb, and found it just like the women had said, but they didn’t see him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>He said to them, “Foolish men, and slow of heart to believe in all that the prophets have spoken! </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Didn’t the Christ have to suffer these things and to enter into his glory?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>Beginning from Moses and from all the prophets, he explained to them in all the Scriptures the things concerning himself. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>They came near to the village, where they were going, and he acted like he would go further. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>They urged him, saying, “Stay with us, for it is almost evening, and the day is almost over.” He went in to stay with them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>When he had sat down at the table with them, he took the bread and gave thanks. Breaking it, he gave it to them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Their eyes were opened, and they recognized him, and he vanished out of their sight. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>They said to one another, “Weren’t our hearts burning within us, while he spoke to us along the way, and while he opened the Scriptures to us?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>They rose up that very hour, returned to Jerusalem, and found the eleven gathered together, and those who were with them, </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>saying, “The Lord is risen indeed, and has appeared to Simon!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>They related the things that happened along the way, and how he was recognized by them in the breaking of the bread. </div>
