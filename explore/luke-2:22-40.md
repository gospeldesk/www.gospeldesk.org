<div class="verse"><span>22</span>When the days of their purification according to the law of Moses were fulfilled, they brought him up to Jerusalem, to present him to the Lord </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>(as it is written in the law of the Lord, “Every male who opens the womb shall be called holy to the Lord”), , </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>and to offer a sacrifice according to that which is said in the law of the Lord, “A pair of turtledoves, or two young pigeons.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Behold, there was a man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon. This man was righteous and devout, looking for the consolation of Israel, and the Holy Spirit was on him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>It had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he should not see death before he had seen the Lord’s Christ. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>He came in the Spirit into the temple. When the parents brought in the child, Jesus, that they might do concerning him according to the custom of the law, </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>then he received him into his arms, and blessed God, and said, </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>“Now you are releasing your servant, Master, according to your word, in peace; </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>for my eyes have seen your salvation, </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>which you have prepared before the face of all peoples; </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>a light for revelation to the nations, and the glory of your people Israel.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>Joseph and his mother were marveling at the things which were spoken concerning him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>and Simeon blessed them, and said to Mary, his mother, “Behold, this child is set for the falling and the rising of many in Israel, and for a sign which is spoken against. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>Yes, a sword will pierce through your own soul, that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>There was one Anna, a prophetess, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher (she was of a great age, having lived with a husband seven years from her virginity, </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>and she had been a widow for about eighty-four years), who didn’t depart from the temple, worshiping with fastings and petitions night and day. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>Coming up at that very hour, she gave thanks to the Lord, and spoke of him to all those who were looking for redemption in Jerusalem. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>When they had accomplished all things that were according to the law of the Lord, they returned into Galilee, to their own city, Nazareth. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>The child was growing, and was becoming strong in spirit, being filled with wisdom, and the grace of God was upon him. </div>
