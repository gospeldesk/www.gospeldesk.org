<div class="verse"><span>1</span>Jesus, full of the Holy Spirit, returned from the Jordan, and was led by the Spirit into the wilderness </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>for forty days, being tempted by the devil. He ate nothing in those days. Afterward, when they were completed, he was hungry. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>The devil said to him, “If you are the Son of God, command this stone to become bread.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Jesus answered him, saying, “It is written, ‘Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of God.’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>The devil, leading him up on a high mountain, showed him all the kingdoms of the world in a moment of time. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>The devil said to him, “I will give you all this authority, and their glory, for it has been delivered to me; and I give it to whomever I want. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>If you therefore will worship before me, it will all be yours.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>Jesus answered him, “Get behind me Satan! For it is written, ‘You shall worship the Lord your God, and you shall serve him only.’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>He led him to Jerusalem, and set him on the pinnacle of the temple, and said to him, “If you are the Son of God, cast yourself down from here, </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>for it is written, ‘He will put his angels in charge of you, to guard you;’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>and, ‘On their hands they will bear you up, lest perhaps you dash your foot against a stone.’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>Jesus answering, said to him, “It has been said, ‘You shall not tempt the Lord your God.’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>When the devil had completed every temptation, he departed from him until another time. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit into Galilee, and news about him spread through all the surrounding area. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>He taught in their synagogues, being glorified by all. </div>
