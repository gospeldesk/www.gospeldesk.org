<div class="verse"><span>27</span>After these things he went out, and saw a tax collector named Levi sitting at the tax office, and said to him, “Follow me!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>He left everything, and rose up and followed him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>Levi made a great feast for him in his house. There was a great crowd of tax collectors and others who were reclining with them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Their scribes and the Pharisees murmured against his disciples, saying, “Why do you eat and drink with the tax collectors and sinners?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Jesus answered them, “Those who are healthy have no need for a physician, but those who are sick do. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.” </div>
