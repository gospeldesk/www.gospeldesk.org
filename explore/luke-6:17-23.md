<div class="verse"><span>17</span>He came down with them, and stood on a level place, with a crowd of his disciples, and a great number of the people from all Judea and Jerusalem, and the sea coast of Tyre and Sidon, who came to hear him and to be healed of their diseases; </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>as well as those who were troubled by unclean spirits, and they were being healed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>All the multitude sought to touch him, for power came out of him and healed them all. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>He lifted up his eyes to his disciples, and said, “Blessed are you who are poor, God’s Kingdom is yours. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>Blessed are you who hunger now, for you will be filled. Blessed are you who weep now, for you will laugh. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Blessed are you when men shall hate you, and when they shall exclude and mock you, and throw out your name as evil, for the Son of Man’s sake. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>Rejoice in that day, and leap for joy, for behold, your reward is great in heaven, for their fathers did the same thing to the prophets. </div>
