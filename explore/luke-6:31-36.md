<div class="verse"><span>31</span>“As you would like people to do to you, do exactly so to them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>If you love those who love you, what credit is that to you? For even sinners love those who love them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>If you do good to those who do good to you, what credit is that to you? For even sinners do the same. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>If you lend to those from whom you hope to receive, what credit is that to you? Even sinners lend to sinners, to receive back as much. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>But love your enemies, and do good, and lend, expecting nothing back; and your reward will be great, and you will be children of the Most High; for he is kind toward the unthankful and evil. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>“Therefore be merciful, even as your Father is also merciful. </div>
