<div class="verse"><span>46</span>“Why do you call me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ and don’t do the things which I say? </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>Everyone who comes to me, and hears my words, and does them, I will show you who he is like. </div> <div class="verse"><span>48</span>He is like a man building a house, who dug and went deep, and laid a foundation on the rock. When a flood arose, the stream broke against that house, and could not shake it, because it was founded on the rock. </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>But he who hears, and doesn’t do, is like a man who built a house on the earth without a foundation, against which the stream broke, and immediately it fell, and the ruin of that house was great.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>After he had finished speaking in the hearing of the people, he entered into Capernaum. </div>
