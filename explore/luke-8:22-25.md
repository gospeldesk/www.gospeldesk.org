<div class="verse"><span>22</span>Now on one of those days, he entered into a boat, himself and his disciples, and he said to them, “Let’s go over to the other side of the lake.” So they launched out. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>But as they sailed, he fell asleep. A wind storm came down on the lake, and they were taking on dangerous amounts of water. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>They came to him, and awoke him, saying, “Master, master, we are dying!” He awoke, and rebuked the wind and the raging of the water, and they ceased, and it was calm. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>He said to them, “Where is your faith?” Being afraid they marveled, saying to one another, “Who is this then, that he commands even the winds and the water, and they obey him?” </div>
