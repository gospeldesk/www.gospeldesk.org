<div class="verse"><span>26</span>They arrived at the country of the Gadarenes, which is opposite Galilee. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>When Jesus stepped ashore, a certain man out of the city who had demons for a long time met him. He wore no clothes, and didn’t live in a house, but in the tombs. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>When he saw Jesus, he cried out, and fell down before him, and with a loud voice said, “What do I have to do with you, Jesus, you Son of the Most High God? I beg you, don’t torment me!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>For Jesus was commanding the unclean spirit to come out of the man. For the unclean spirit had often seized the man. He was kept under guard, and bound with chains and fetters. Breaking the bonds apart, he was driven by the demon into the desert. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Jesus asked him, “What is your name?” He said, “Legion,” for many demons had entered into him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>They begged him that he would not command them to go into the abyss. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>Now there was there a herd of many pigs feeding on the mountain, and they begged him that he would allow them to enter into those. He allowed them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>The demons came out of the man, and entered into the pigs, and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake, and were drowned. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>When those who fed them saw what had happened, they fled, and told it in the city and in the country. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>People went out to see what had happened. They came to Jesus, and found the man from whom the demons had gone out, sitting at Jesus’ feet, clothed and in his right mind; and they were afraid. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>Those who saw it told them how he who had been possessed by demons was healed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>All the people of the surrounding country of the Gadarenes asked him to depart from them, for they were very much afraid. He entered into the boat, and returned. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>But the man from whom the demons had gone out begged him that he might go with him, but Jesus sent him away, saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>“Return to your house, and declare what great things God has done for you.” He went his way, proclaiming throughout the whole city what great things Jesus had done for him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>When Jesus returned, the multitude welcomed him, for they were all waiting for him. </div>
