<div class="verse"><span>1</span>He called the twelve </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>He sent them out to preach God’s Kingdom and to heal the sick. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>He said to them, “Take nothing for your journey—neither staffs, nor wallet, nor bread, nor money; neither have two coats each. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Into whatever house you enter, stay there, and depart from there. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>As many as don’t receive you, when you depart from that city, shake off even the dust from your feet for a testimony against them.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>They departed, and went throughout the villages, preaching the Good News, and healing everywhere. </div>
