<div class="verse"><span>28</span>About eight days after these sayings, he took with him Peter, John, and James, and went up onto the mountain to pray. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>As he was praying, the appearance of his face was altered, and his clothing became white and dazzling. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Behold, two men were talking with him, who were Moses and Elijah, </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>who appeared in glory, and spoke of his departure, </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>Now Peter and those who were with him were heavy with sleep, but when they were fully awake, they saw his glory, and the two men who stood with him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>As they were parting from him, Peter said to Jesus, “Master, it is good for us to be here. Let’s make three tents: one for you, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah,” not knowing what he said. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>While he said these things, a cloud came and overshadowed them, and they were afraid as they entered into the cloud. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>A voice came out of the cloud, saying, “This is my beloved Son. Listen to him!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>When the voice came, Jesus was found alone. They were silent, and told no one in those days any of the things which they had seen. </div>
