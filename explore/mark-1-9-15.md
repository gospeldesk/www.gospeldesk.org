<div class="verse"><span>9</span>In those days, Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee, and was baptized by John in the Jordan. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Immediately coming up from the water, he saw the heavens parting, and the Spirit descending on him like a dove. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>A voice came out of the sky, “You are my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>Immediately the Spirit drove him out into the wilderness. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>He was there in the wilderness forty days tempted by Satan. He was with the wild animals; and the angels were serving him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Now after John was taken into custody, Jesus came into Galilee, preaching the Good News of God’s Kingdom, </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>and saying, “The time is fulfilled, and God’s Kingdom is at hand! Repent, and believe in the Good News.” </div>
