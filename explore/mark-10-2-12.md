<div class="verse"><span>2</span>Pharisees came to him testing him, and asked him, “Is it lawful for a man to divorce his wife?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>He answered, “What did Moses command you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>They said, “Moses allowed a certificate of divorce to be written, and to divorce her.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>But Jesus said to them, “For your hardness of heart, he wrote you this commandment. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>But from the beginning of the creation, God made them male and female. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>For this cause a man will leave his father and mother, and will join to his wife, </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>and the two will become one flesh, so that they are no longer two, but one flesh. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>What therefore God has joined together, let no man separate.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>In the house, his disciples asked him again about the same matter. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>He said to them, “Whoever divorces his wife, and marries another, commits adultery against her. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>If a woman herself divorces her husband, and marries another, she commits adultery.” </div>
