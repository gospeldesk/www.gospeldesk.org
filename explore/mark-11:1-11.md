<div class="verse"><span>1</span>When they came near to Jerusalem, to Bethsphage </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>and said to them, “Go your way into the village that is opposite you. Immediately as you enter into it, you will find a young donkey tied, on which no one has sat. Untie him, and bring him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>If anyone asks you, ‘Why are you doing this?’ say, ‘The Lord needs him;’ and immediately he will send him back here.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>They went away, and found a young donkey tied at the door outside in the open street, and they untied him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Some of those who stood there asked them, “What are you doing, untying the young donkey?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>They said to them just as Jesus had said, and they let them go. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>They brought the young donkey to Jesus, and threw their garments on it, and Jesus sat on it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>Many spread their garments on the way, and others were cutting down branches from the trees, and spreading them on the road. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>Those who went in front, and those who followed, cried out, “Hosanna! </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Blessed is the kingdom of our father David that is coming in the name of the Lord! Hosanna in the highest!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>Jesus entered into the temple in Jerusalem. When he had looked around at everything, it being now evening, he went out to Bethany with the twelve. </div>
