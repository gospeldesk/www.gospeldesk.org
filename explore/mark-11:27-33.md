<div class="verse"><span>27</span>They came again to Jerusalem, and as he was walking in the temple, the chief priests, and the scribes, and the elders came to him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>and they began saying to him, “By what authority do you do these things? Or who gave you this authority to do these things?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>Jesus said to them, “I will ask you one question. Answer me, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>The baptism of John—was it from heaven, or from men? Answer me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>They reasoned with themselves, saying, “If we should say, ‘From heaven;’ he will say, ‘Why then did you not believe him?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>If we should say, ‘From men’”—they feared the people, for all held John to really be a prophet. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>They answered Jesus, “We don’t know.” Jesus said to them, “Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things.” </div>
