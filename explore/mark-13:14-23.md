<div class="verse"><span>14</span>But when you see the abomination of desolation, ;  ;  spoken of by Daniel the prophet, standing where it ought not (let the reader understand), then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains, </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>and let him who is on the housetop not go down, nor enter in, to take anything out of his house. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>Let him who is in the field not return back to take his cloak. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>But woe to those who are with child and to those who nurse babies in those days! </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Pray that your flight won’t be in the winter. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>For in those days there will be oppression, such as there has not been the like from the beginning of the creation which God created until now, and never will be. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Unless the Lord had shortened the days, no flesh would have been saved; but for the sake of the chosen ones, whom he picked out, he shortened the days. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>Then if anyone tells you, ‘Look, here is the Christ!’ or, ‘Look, there!’ don’t believe it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>For there will arise false christs and false prophets, and will show signs and wonders, that they may lead astray, if possible, even the chosen ones. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>But you watch. “Behold, I have told you all things beforehand. </div>
