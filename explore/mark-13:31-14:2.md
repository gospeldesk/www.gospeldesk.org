<div class="verse"><span>31</span>Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>But of that day or that hour no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but only the Father. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>Watch, keep alert, and pray; for you don’t know when the time is. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>“It is like a man, traveling to another country, having left his house, and given authority to his servants, and to each one his work, and also commanded the doorkeeper to keep watch. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>Watch therefore, for you don’t know when the lord of the house is coming, whether at evening, or at midnight, or when the rooster crows, or in the morning; </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>lest coming suddenly he might find you sleeping. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>What I tell you, I tell all: Watch.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>It was now two days before the feast of the Passover and the unleavened bread, and the chief priests and the scribes sought how they might seize him by deception, and kill him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>For they said, “Not during the feast, because there might be a riot among the people.” </div>
