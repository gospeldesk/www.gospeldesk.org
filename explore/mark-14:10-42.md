<div class="verse"><span>10</span>Judas Iscariot, who was one of the twelve, went away to the chief priests, that he might deliver him to them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>They, when they heard it, were glad, and promised to give him money. He sought how he might conveniently deliver him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>On the first day of unleavened bread, when they sacrificed the Passover, his disciples asked him, “Where do you want us to go and prepare that you may eat the Passover?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>He sent two of his disciples, and said to them, “Go into the city, and there you will meet a man carrying a pitcher of water. Follow him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>and wherever he enters in, tell the master of the house, ‘The Teacher says, “Where is the guest room, where I may eat the Passover with my disciples?”’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>He will himself show you a large upper room furnished and ready. Get ready for us there.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>His disciples went out, and came into the city, and found things as he had said to them, and they prepared the Passover. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>When it was evening he came with the twelve. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>As they sat and were eating, Jesus said, “Most certainly I tell you, one of you will betray me—he who eats with me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>They began to be sorrowful, and to ask him one by one, “Surely not I?” And another said, “Surely not I?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>He answered them, “It is one of the twelve, he who dips with me in the dish. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>For the Son of Man goes, even as it is written about him, but woe to that man by whom the Son of Man is betrayed! It would be better for that man if he had not been born.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>As they were eating, Jesus took bread, and when he had blessed, he broke it, and gave to them, and said, “Take, eat. This is my body.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>He took the cup, and when he had given thanks, he gave to them. They all drank of it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>He said to them, “This is my blood of the new covenant, which is poured out for many. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Most certainly I tell you, I will no more drink of the fruit of the vine, until that day when I drink it anew in God’s Kingdom.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>When they had sung a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>Jesus said to them, “All of you will be made to stumble because of me tonight, for it is written, ‘I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep will be scattered.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>However, after I am raised up, I will go before you into Galilee.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>But Peter said to him, “Although all will be offended, yet I will not.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Jesus said to him, “Most certainly I tell you, that you today, even this night, before the rooster crows twice, you will deny me three times.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>But he spoke all the more, “If I must die with you, I will not deny you.” They all said the same thing. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>They came to a place which was named Gethsemane. He said to his disciples, “Sit here, while I pray.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>He took with him Peter, James, and John, and began to be greatly troubled and distressed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>He said to them, “My soul is exceedingly sorrowful, even to death. Stay here, and watch.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>He went forward a little, and fell on the ground, and prayed that, if it were possible, the hour might pass away from him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>He said, “Abba, </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>He came and found them sleeping, and said to Peter, “Simon, are you sleeping? Couldn’t you watch one hour? </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>Watch and pray, that you may not enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>Again he went away, and prayed, saying the same words. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>Again he returned, and found them sleeping, for their eyes were very heavy, and they didn’t know what to answer him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>He came the third time, and said to them, “Sleep on now, and take your rest. It is enough. The hour has come. Behold, the Son of Man is betrayed into the hands of sinners. </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>Arise, let us be going. Behold, he who betrays me is at hand.” </div>
