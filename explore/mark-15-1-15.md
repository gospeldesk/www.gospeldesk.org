<div class="verse"><span>1</span>Immediately in the morning the chief priests, with the elders and scribes, and the whole council, held a consultation, and bound Jesus, and carried him away, and delivered him up to Pilate. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Pilate asked him, “Are you the King of the Jews?” He answered, “So you say.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>The chief priests accused him of many things. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Pilate again asked him, “Have you no answer? See how many things they testify against you!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>But Jesus made no further answer, so that Pilate marveled. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>Now at the feast he used to release to them one prisoner, whom they asked of him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>There was one called Barabbas, bound with his fellow insurgents, men who in the insurrection had committed murder. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>The multitude, crying aloud, began to ask him to do as he always did for them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>Pilate answered them, saying, “Do you want me to release to you the King of the Jews?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>For he perceived that for envy the chief priests had delivered him up. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>But the chief priests stirred up the multitude, that he should release Barabbas to them instead. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>Pilate again asked them, “What then should I do to him whom you call the King of the Jews?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>They cried out again, “Crucify him!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Pilate said to them, “Why, what evil has he done?” But they cried out exceedingly, “Crucify him!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Pilate, wishing to please the multitude, released Barabbas to them, and handed over Jesus, when he had flogged him, to be crucified. </div>
