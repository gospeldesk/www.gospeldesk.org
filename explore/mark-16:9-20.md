<div class="verse"><span>9</span></div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>She went and told those who had been with him, as they mourned and wept. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>When they heard that he was alive, and had been seen by her, they disbelieved. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>After these things he was revealed in another form to two of them, as they walked, on their way into the country. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>They went away and told it to the rest. They didn’t believe them, either. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Afterward he was revealed to the eleven themselves as they sat at the table, and he rebuked them for their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they didn’t believe those who had seen him after he had risen. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>He said to them, “Go into all the world, and preach the Good News to the whole creation. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>He who believes and is baptized will be saved; but he who disbelieves will be condemned. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>These signs will accompany those who believe: in my name they will cast out demons; they will speak with new languages; </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>they will take up serpents; and if they drink any deadly thing, it will in no way hurt them; they will lay hands on the sick, and they will recover.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>So then the Lord, </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>They went out, and preached everywhere, the Lord working with them, and confirming the word by the signs that followed. Amen. </div>
