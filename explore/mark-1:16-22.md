<div class="verse"><span>16</span>Passing along by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew the brother of Simon casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Jesus said to them, “Come after me, and I will make you into fishers for men.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Immediately they left their nets, and followed him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Going on a little further from there, he saw James the son of Zebedee, and John, his brother, who were also in the boat mending the nets. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Immediately he called them, and they left their father, Zebedee, in the boat with the hired servants, and went after him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>They went into Capernaum, and immediately on the Sabbath day he entered into the synagogue and taught. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>They were astonished at his teaching, for he taught them as having authority, and not as the scribes. </div>
