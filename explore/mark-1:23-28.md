<div class="verse"><span>23</span>Immediately there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit, and he cried out, </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>saying, “Ha! I know you who you are: the Holy One of God!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Jesus rebuked him, saying, “Be quiet, and come out of him!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>The unclean spirit, convulsing him and crying with a loud voice, came out of him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>They were all amazed, so that they questioned among themselves, saying, “What is this? A new teaching? For with authority he commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>The report of him went out immediately everywhere into all the region of Galilee and its surrounding area. </div>
