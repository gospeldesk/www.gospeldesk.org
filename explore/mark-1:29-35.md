<div class="verse"><span>29</span>Immediately, when they had come out of the synagogue, they came into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Now Simon’s wife’s mother lay sick with a fever, and immediately they told him about her. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>He came and took her by the hand, and raised her up. The fever left her, and she served them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>At evening, when the sun had set, they brought to him all who were sick, and those who were possessed by demons. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>All the city was gathered together at the door. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>He healed many who were sick with various diseases, and cast out many demons. He didn’t allow the demons to speak, because they knew him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>Early in the morning, while it was still dark, he rose up and went out, and departed into a deserted place, and prayed there. </div>
