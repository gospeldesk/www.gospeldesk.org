<div class="verse"><span>19</span>and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him. Then he came into a house. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>The multitude came together again, so that they could not so much as eat bread. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>When his friends heard it, they went out to seize him: for they said, “He is insane.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>The scribes who came down from Jerusalem said, “He has Beelzebul,” and, “By the prince of the demons he casts out the demons.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>He summoned them, and said to them in parables, “How can Satan cast out Satan? </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>If a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>If a house is divided against itself, that house cannot stand. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>If Satan has risen up against himself, and is divided, he can’t stand, but has an end. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>But no one can enter into the house of the strong man to plunder, unless he first binds the strong man; and then he will plunder his house. </div>
