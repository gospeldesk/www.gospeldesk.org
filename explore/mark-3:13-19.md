<div class="verse"><span>13</span>He went up into the mountain, and called to himself those whom he wanted, and they went to him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>He appointed twelve, that they might be with him, and that he might send them out to preach, </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>and to have authority to heal sicknesses and to cast out demons: </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>Simon (to whom he gave the name Peter); </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>James the son of Zebedee; and John, the brother of James, (whom he called Boanerges, which means, Sons of Thunder); </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Andrew; Philip; Bartholomew; Matthew; Thomas; James, the son of Alphaeus; Thaddaeus; Simon the Zealot; </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him. Then he came into a house. </div>
