<div class="verse"><span>6</span>The Pharisees went out, and immediately conspired with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Jesus withdrew to the sea with his disciples, and a great multitude followed him from Galilee, from Judea, </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>from Jerusalem, from Idumaea, beyond the Jordan, and those from around Tyre and Sidon. A great multitude, hearing what great things he did, came to him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>He spoke to his disciples that a little boat should stay near him because of the crowd, so that they wouldn’t press on him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>For he had healed many, so that as many as had diseases pressed on him that they might touch him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>The unclean spirits, whenever they saw him, fell down before him, and cried, “You are the Son of God!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>He sternly warned them that they should not make him known. </div>
