<div class="verse"><span>10</span>When he was alone, those who were around him with the twelve asked him about the parables. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>He said to them, “To you is given the mystery of God’s Kingdom, but to those who are outside, all things are done in parables, </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>that ‘seeing they may see, and not perceive; and hearing they may hear, and not understand; lest perhaps they should turn again, and their sins should be forgiven them.’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>He said to them, “Don’t you understand this parable? How will you understand all of the parables? </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>The farmer sows the word. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>The ones by the road are the ones where the word is sown; and when they have heard, immediately Satan comes, and takes away the word which has been sown in them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>These in the same way are those who are sown on the rocky places, who, when they have heard the word, immediately receive it with joy. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>They have no root in themselves, but are short-lived. When oppression or persecution arises because of the word, immediately they stumble. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Others are those who are sown among the thorns. These are those who have heard the word, </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>and the cares of this age, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts of other things entering in choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Those which were sown on the good ground are those who hear the word, and accept it, and bear fruit, some thirty times, some sixty times, and some one hundred times.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>He said to them, “Is the lamp brought to be put under a basket or under a bed? Isn’t it put on a stand? </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>For there is nothing hidden, except that it should be made known; neither was anything made secret, but that it should come to light. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>If any man has ears to hear, let him hear.” </div>
