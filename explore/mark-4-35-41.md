<div class="verse"><span>35</span>On that day, when evening had come, he said to them, “Let’s go over to the other side.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>Leaving the multitude, they took him with them, even as he was, in the boat. Other small boats were also with him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>A big wind storm arose, and the waves beat into the boat, so much that the boat was already filled. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>He himself was in the stern, asleep on the cushion, and they woke him up, and told him, “Teacher, don’t you care that we are dying?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>He awoke, and rebuked the wind, and said to the sea, “Peace! Be still!” The wind ceased, and there was a great calm. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>He said to them, “Why are you so afraid? How is it that you have no faith?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>They were greatly afraid, and said to one another, “Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?” </div>
