<div class="verse"><span>24</span>He went with him, and a great multitude followed him, and they pressed upon him on all sides. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>A certain woman, who had an issue of blood for twelve years, </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>and had suffered many things by many physicians, and had spent all that she had, and was no better, but rather grew worse, </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>having heard the things concerning Jesus, came up behind him in the crowd, and touched his clothes. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>For she said, “If I just touch his clothes, I will be made well.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>Immediately the flow of her blood was dried up, and she felt in her body that she was healed of her affliction. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Immediately Jesus, perceiving in himself that the power had gone out from him, turned around in the crowd, and asked, “Who touched my clothes?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>His disciples said to him, “You see the multitude pressing against you, and you say, ‘Who touched me?’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>He looked around to see her who had done this thing. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>But the woman, fearing and trembling, knowing what had been done to her, came and fell down before him, and told him all the truth. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>He said to her, “Daughter, your faith has made you well. Go in peace, and be cured of your disease.” </div>
