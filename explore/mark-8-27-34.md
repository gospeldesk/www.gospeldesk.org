<div class="verse"><span>27</span>Jesus went out, with his disciples, into the villages of Caesarea Philippi. On the way he asked his disciples, “Who do men say that I am?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>They told him, “John the Baptizer, and others say Elijah, but others: one of the prophets.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>He said to them, “But who do you say that I am?” Peter answered, “You are the Christ.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>He commanded them that they should tell no one about him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>He began to teach them that the Son of Man must suffer many things, and be rejected by the elders, the chief priests, and the scribes, and be killed, and after three days rise again. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>He spoke to them openly. Peter took him, and began to rebuke him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>But he, turning around, and seeing his disciples, rebuked Peter, and said, “Get behind me, Satan! For you have in mind not the things of God, but the things of men.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>He called the multitude to himself with his disciples, and said to them, “Whoever wants to come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me. </div>
