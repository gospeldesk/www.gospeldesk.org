<div class="verse"><span>1</span>In those days, when there was a very great multitude, and they had nothing to eat, Jesus called his disciples to himself, and said to them, </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>“I have compassion on the multitude, because they have stayed with me now three days, and have nothing to eat. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>If I send them away fasting to their home, they will faint on the way, for some of them have come a long way.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>His disciples answered him, “From where could one satisfy these people with bread here in a deserted place?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>He asked them, “How many loaves do you have?” They said, “Seven.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>He commanded the multitude to sit down on the ground, and he took the seven loaves. Having given thanks, he broke them, and gave them to his disciples to serve, and they served the multitude. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>They had a few small fish. Having blessed them, he said to serve these also. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>They ate, and were filled. They took up seven baskets of broken pieces that were left over. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>Those who had eaten were about four thousand. Then he sent them away. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Immediately he entered into the boat with his disciples, and came into the region of Dalmanutha. </div>
