<div class="verse"><span>11</span>The Pharisees came out and began to question him, seeking from him a sign from heaven, and testing him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>He sighed deeply in his spirit, and said, “Why does this generation seek a sign? Most certainly I tell you, no sign will be given to this generation.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>He left them, and again entering into the boat, departed to the other side. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>They forgot to take bread; and they didn’t have more than one loaf in the boat with them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>He warned them, saying, “Take heed: beware of the yeast of the Pharisees and the yeast of Herod.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>They reasoned with one another, saying, “It’s because we have no bread.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Jesus, perceiving it, said to them, “Why do you reason that it’s because you have no bread? Don’t you perceive yet, neither understand? Is your heart still hardened? </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Having eyes, don’t you see? Having ears, don’t you hear? Don’t you remember? </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>When I broke the five loaves among the five thousand, how many baskets full of broken pieces did you take up?” They told him, “Twelve.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>“When the seven loaves fed the four thousand, how many baskets full of broken pieces did you take up?” They told him, “Seven.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>He asked them, “Don’t you understand yet?” </div>
