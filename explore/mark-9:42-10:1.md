<div class="verse"><span>42</span>Whoever will cause one of these little ones who believe in me to stumble, it would be better for him if he were thrown into the sea with a millstone hung around his neck. </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>If your hand causes you to stumble, cut it off. It is better for you to enter into life maimed, rather than having your two hands to go into Gehenna, into the unquenchable fire, </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>‘where their worm doesn’t die, and the fire is not quenched.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>If your foot causes you to stumble, cut it off. It is better for you to enter into life lame, rather than having your two feet to be cast into Gehenna, into the fire that will never be quenched— </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>‘where their worm doesn’t die, and the fire is not quenched.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>If your eye causes you to stumble, cast it out. It is better for you to enter into God’s Kingdom with one eye, rather than having two eyes to be cast into the Gehenna of fire, </div> <div class="verse"><span>48</span>‘where their worm doesn’t die, and the fire is not quenched.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>For everyone will be salted with fire, and every sacrifice will be seasoned with salt. </div> <div class="verse"><span>50</span>Salt is good, but if the salt has lost its saltiness, with what will you season it? Have salt in yourselves, and be at peace with one another.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>He arose from there and came into the borders of Judea and beyond the Jordan. Multitudes came together to him again. As he usually did, he was again teaching them. </div>
