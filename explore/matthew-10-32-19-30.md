I didn’t come to send peace, but a sword. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">35</span>For I came to set a man at odds against his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">36</span>A man’s foes will be those of his own household. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">37</span>He who loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me; and he who loves son or daughter more than me isn’t worthy of me. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">38</span>He who doesn’t take his cross and follow after me, isn’t worthy of me. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">39</span>He who seeks his life will lose it; and he who loses his life for my sake will find it. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">40</span>He who receives you receives me, and he who receives me receives him who sent me. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">41</span>He who receives a prophet in the name of a prophet will receive a prophet’s reward. He who receives a righteous man in the name of a righteous man will receive a righteous man’s reward. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">42</span>Whoever gives one of these little ones just a cup of cold water to drink in the name of a disciple, most certainly I tell you he will in no way lose his reward.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">1</span>When Jesus had finished directing his twelve disciples, he departed from there to teach and preach in their cities. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">27</span>Then Peter answered, “Behold, we have left everything, and followed you.
