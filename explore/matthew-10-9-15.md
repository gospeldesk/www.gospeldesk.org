<div class="verse"><span>9</span>Don’t take any gold, silver, or brass in your money belts. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Take no bag for your journey, neither two coats, nor shoes, nor staff: for the laborer is worthy of his food. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>Into whatever city or village you enter, find out who in it is worthy; and stay there until you go on. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>As you enter into the household, greet it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>If the household is worthy, let your peace come on it, but if it isn’t worthy, let your peace return to you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Whoever doesn’t receive you, nor hear your words, as you go out of that house or that city, shake the dust off your feet. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Most certainly I tell you, it will be more tolerable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah in the day of judgment than for that city. </div>
