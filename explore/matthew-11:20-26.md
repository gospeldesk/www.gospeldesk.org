For if the mighty works had been done in Tyre and Sidon which were done in you, they would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>But I tell you, it will be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon on the day of judgment than for you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>You, Capernaum, who are exalted to heaven, you will go down to Hades. For if the mighty works had been done in Sodom which were done in you, it would have remained until today. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>But I tell you that it will be more tolerable for the land of Sodom, on the day of judgment, than for you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>At that time, Jesus answered, “I thank you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, that you hid these things from the wise and understanding, and revealed them to infants. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Yes, Father, for so it was well-pleasing in your sight. </div>
