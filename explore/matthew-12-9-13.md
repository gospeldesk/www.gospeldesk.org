<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">9</span>He departed there, and went into their synagogue. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">10</span>And behold there was a man with a withered hand. They asked him, “Is it lawful to heal on the Sabbath day?” that they might accuse him. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">11</span>He said to them, “What man is there among you, who has one sheep, and if this one falls into a pit on the Sabbath day, won’t he grab on to it, and lift it out? </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">12</span>Of how much more value then is a man than a sheep! Therefore it is lawful to do good on the Sabbath day.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">13</span>Then he told the man, “Stretch out your hand.” He stretched it out; and it was restored whole, just like the other. </span></p>
