<div class="verse"><span>14</span>But the Pharisees went out, and conspired against him, how they might destroy him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Jesus, perceiving that, withdrew from there. Great multitudes followed him; and he healed them all, </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>and commanded them that they should not make him known: </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>“Behold, my servant whom I have chosen; my beloved in whom my soul is well pleased: I will put my Spirit on him. He will proclaim justice to the nations. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>He will not strive, nor shout; neither will anyone hear his voice in the streets. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>He won’t break a bruised reed. He won’t quench a smoking flax, until he leads justice to victory. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>In his name, the nations will hope.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Then one possessed by a demon, blind and mute, was brought to him and he healed him, so that the blind and mute man both spoke and saw. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>All the multitudes were amazed, and said, “Can this be the son of David?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>But when the Pharisees heard it, they said, “This man does not cast out demons, except by Beelzebul, the prince of the demons.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Knowing their thoughts, Jesus said to them, “Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation, and every city or house divided against itself will not stand. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>If Satan casts out Satan, he is divided against himself. How then will his kingdom stand? </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>If I by Beelzebul cast out demons, by whom do your children cast them out? Therefore they will be your judges. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>But if I by the Spirit of God cast out demons, then God’s Kingdom has come upon you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>Or how can one enter into the house of the strong man, and plunder his goods, unless he first bind the strong man? Then he will plunder his house. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>“He who is not with me is against me, and he who doesn’t gather with me, scatters. </div>
