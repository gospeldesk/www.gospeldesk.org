<div class="verse"><span>54</span>Coming into his own country, he taught them in their synagogue, so that they were astonished, and said, “Where did this man get this wisdom, and these mighty works? </div> <div class="verse"><span>55</span>Isn’t this the carpenter’s son? Isn’t his mother called Mary, and his brothers, James, Joses, Simon, and Judas? </div> <div class="verse"><span>56</span>Aren’t all of his sisters with us? Where then did this man get all of these things?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>57</span>They were offended by him. But Jesus said to them, “A prophet is not without honor, except in his own country, and in his own house.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>58</span>He didn’t do many mighty works there because of their unbelief. </div>
