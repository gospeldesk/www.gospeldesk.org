<div class="verse"><span>12</span>Then the disciples came, and said to him, “Do you know that the Pharisees were offended, when they heard this saying?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>But he answered, “Every plant which my heavenly Father didn’t plant will be uprooted. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Leave them alone. They are blind guides of the blind. If the blind guide the blind, both will fall into a pit.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Peter answered him, “Explain the parable to us.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>So Jesus said, “Do you also still not understand? </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Don’t you understand that whatever goes into the mouth passes into the belly, and then out of the body? </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>But the things which proceed out of the mouth come out of the heart, and they defile the man. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>For out of the heart come evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, sexual sins, thefts, false testimony, and blasphemies. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>These are the things which defile the man; but to eat with unwashed hands doesn’t defile the man.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>Jesus went out from there, and withdrew into the region of Tyre and Sidon. </div>
