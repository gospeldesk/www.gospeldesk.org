<div class="verse"><span>21</span>Jesus went out from there, and withdrew into the region of Tyre and Sidon. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Behold, a Canaanite woman came out from those borders, and cried, saying, “Have mercy on me, Lord, you son of David! My daughter is severely possessed by a demon!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>But he answered her not a word. His disciples came and begged him, saying, “Send her away; for she cries after us.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>But he answered, “I wasn’t sent to anyone but the lost sheep of the house of Israel.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>But she came and worshiped him, saying, “Lord, help me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>But he answered, “It is not appropriate to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>But she said, “Yes, Lord, but even the dogs eat the crumbs which fall from their masters’ table.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>Then Jesus answered her, “Woman, great is your faith! Be it done to you even as you desire.” And her daughter was healed from that hour. </div>
