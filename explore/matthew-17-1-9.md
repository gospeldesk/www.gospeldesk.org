<div class="verse"><span>1</span>After six days, Jesus took with him Peter, James, and John his brother, and brought them up into a high mountain by themselves. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>He was transfigured before them. His face shone like the sun, and his garments became as white as the light. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>Behold, Moses and Elijah appeared to them talking with him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Peter answered, and said to Jesus, “Lord, it is good for us to be here. If you want, let’s make three tents here: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>While he was still speaking, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them. Behold, a voice came out of the cloud, saying, “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. Listen to him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>When the disciples heard it, they fell on their faces, and were very afraid. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Jesus came and touched them and said, “Get up, and don’t be afraid.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>Lifting up their eyes, they saw no one, except Jesus alone. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>As they were coming down from the mountain, Jesus commanded them, saying, “Don’t tell anyone what you saw, until the Son of Man has risen from the dead.” </div>
