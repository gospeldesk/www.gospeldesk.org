<div class="verse"><span>10</span>His disciples asked him, saying, “Then why do the scribes say that Elijah must come first?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>Jesus answered them, “Elijah indeed comes first, and will restore all things, </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>but I tell you that Elijah has come already, and they didn’t recognize him, but did to him whatever they wanted to. Even so the Son of Man will also suffer by them.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Then the disciples understood that he spoke to them of John the Baptizer. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>When they came to the multitude, a man came to him, kneeling down to him, and saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>“Lord, have mercy on my son, for he is epileptic, and suffers grievously; for he often falls into the fire, and often into the water. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>So I brought him to your disciples, and they could not cure him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Jesus answered, “Faithless and perverse generation! How long will I be with you? How long will I bear with you? Bring him here to me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Jesus rebuked him, the demon went out of him, and the boy was cured from that hour. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Then the disciples came to Jesus privately, and said, “Why weren’t we able to cast it out?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>He said to them, “Because of your unbelief. For most certainly I tell you, if you have faith as a grain of mustard seed, you will tell this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it will move; and nothing will be impossible for you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>But this kind doesn’t go out except by prayer and fasting.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>While they were staying in Galilee, Jesus said to them, “The Son of Man is about to be delivered up into the hands of men, </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>and they will kill him, and the third day he will be raised up.” They were exceedingly sorry. </div>
