<div class="verse"><span>24</span>When they had come to Capernaum, those who collected the didrachma coins </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>He said, “Yes.” When he came into the house, Jesus anticipated him, saying, “What do you think, Simon? From whom do the kings of the earth receive toll or tribute? From their children, or from strangers?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>Peter said to him, “From strangers.” Jesus said to him, “Therefore the children are exempt. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>But, lest we cause them to stumble, go to the sea, cast a hook, and take up the first fish that comes up. When you have opened its mouth, you will find a stater coin. Take that, and give it to them for me and you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>In that hour the disciples came to Jesus, saying, “Who then is greatest in the Kingdom of Heaven?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Jesus called a little child to himself, and set him in the middle of them, </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>and said, “Most certainly I tell you, unless you turn, and become as little children, you will in no way enter into the Kingdom of Heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Whoever therefore humbles himself as this little child, the same is the greatest in the Kingdom of Heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Whoever receives one such little child in my name receives me, </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>but whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in me to stumble, it would be better for him that a huge millstone should be hung around his neck, and that he should be sunk in the depths of the sea. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>“Woe to the world because of occasions of stumbling! For it must be that the occasions come, but woe to that person through whom the occasion comes! </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>If your hand or your foot causes you to stumble, cut it off, and cast it from you. It is better for you to enter into life maimed or crippled, rather than having two hands or two feet to be cast into the eternal fire. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>If your eye causes you to stumble, pluck it out, and cast it from you. It is better for you to enter into life with one eye, rather than having two eyes to be cast into the Gehenna of fire. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>See that you don’t despise one of these little ones, for I tell you that in heaven their angels always see the face of my Father who is in heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>For the Son of Man came to save that which was lost. </div>
