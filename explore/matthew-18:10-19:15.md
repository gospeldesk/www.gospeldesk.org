<div class="verse"><span>10</span>See that you don’t despise one of these little ones, for I tell you that in heaven their angels always see the face of my Father who is in heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>For the Son of Man came to save that which was lost. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>“What do you think? If a man has one hundred sheep, and one of them goes astray, doesn’t he leave the ninety-nine, go to the mountains, and seek that which has gone astray? </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>If he finds it, most certainly I tell you, he rejoices over it more than over the ninety-nine which have not gone astray. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Even so it is not the will of your Father who is in heaven that one of these little ones should perish. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>“If your brother sins against you, go, show him his fault between you and him alone. If he listens to you, you have gained back your brother. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>But if he doesn’t listen, take one or two more with you, that at the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>If he refuses to listen to them, tell it to the assembly. If he refuses to hear the assembly also, let him be to you as a Gentile or a tax collector. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Most certainly I tell you, whatever things you bind on earth will have been bound in heaven, and whatever things you release on earth will have been released in heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Again, assuredly I tell you, that if two of you will agree on earth concerning anything that they will ask, it will be done for them by my Father who is in heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there I am in the middle of them.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>Then Peter came and said to him, “Lord, how often shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? Until seven times?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Jesus said to him, “I don’t tell you until seven times, but, until seventy times seven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>When Jesus had finished these words, he departed from Galilee, and came into the borders of Judea beyond the Jordan. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Great multitudes followed him, and he healed them there. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Then little children were brought to him, that he should lay his hands on them and pray; and the disciples rebuked them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>But Jesus said, “Allow the little children, and don’t forbid them to come to me; for the Kingdom of Heaven belongs to ones like these.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>He laid his hands on them, and departed from there. </div>
