<div class="verse"><span>1</span>“For the Kingdom of Heaven is like a man who was the master of a household, who went out early in the morning to hire laborers for his vineyard. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>When he had agreed with the laborers for a denarius a day, he sent them into his vineyard. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>He went out about the third hour, and saw others standing idle in the marketplace. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>He said to them, ‘You also go into the vineyard, and whatever is right I will give you.’ So they went their way. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Again he went out about the sixth and the ninth hour, and did likewise. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>About the eleventh hour he went out, and found others standing idle. He said to them, ‘Why do you stand here all day idle?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>“They said to him, ‘Because no one has hired us.’ “He said to them, ‘You also go into the vineyard, and you will receive whatever is right.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>When evening had come, the lord of the vineyard said to his manager, ‘Call the laborers and pay them their wages, beginning from the last to the first.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>“When those who were hired at about the eleventh hour came, they each received a denarius. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>When the first came, they supposed that they would receive more; and they likewise each received a denarius. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>When they received it, they murmured against the master of the household, </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>saying, ‘These last have spent one hour, and you have made them equal to us, who have borne the burden of the day and the scorching heat!’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>“But he answered one of them, ‘Friend, I am doing you no wrong. Didn’t you agree with me for a denarius? </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Take that which is yours, and go your way. It is my desire to give to this last just as much as to you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Isn’t it lawful for me to do what I want to with what I own? Or is your eye evil, because I am good?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>So the last will be first, and the first last. For many are called, but few are chosen.” </div>
