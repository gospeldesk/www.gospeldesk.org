<div class="verse"><span>29</span>As they went out from Jericho, a great multitude followed him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Behold, two blind men sitting by the road, when they heard that Jesus was passing by, cried out, “Lord, have mercy on us, you son of David!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>The multitude rebuked them, telling them that they should be quiet, but they cried out even more, “Lord, have mercy on us, you son of David!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>Jesus stood still, and called them, and asked, “What do you want me to do for you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>They told him, “Lord, that our eyes may be opened.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Jesus, being moved with compassion, touched their eyes; and immediately their eyes received their sight, and they followed him. </div>
