Others cut branches from the trees, and spread them on the road. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>The multitudes who went in front of him, and those who followed, kept shouting, “Hosanna </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>When he had come into Jerusalem, all the city was stirred up, saying, “Who is this?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>The multitudes said, “This is the prophet, Jesus, from Nazareth of Galilee.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>But when the chief priests and the scribes saw the wonderful things that he did, and the children who were crying in the temple and saying, “Hosanna to the son of David!” they were indignant, </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>and said to him, “Do you hear what these are saying?” Jesus said to them, “Yes.
