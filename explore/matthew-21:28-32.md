<div class="verse"><span>28</span>But what do you think? He answered, ‘I’ m going, sir,’ but he didn’t go. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Which of the two did the will of his father?” They said to him, “The first.” Jesus said to them, “Most certainly I tell you that the tax collectors and the prostitutes are entering into God’s Kingdom before you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you didn’t believe him, but the tax collectors and the prostitutes believed him. When you saw it, you didn’t even repent afterward, that you might believe him. </div>
