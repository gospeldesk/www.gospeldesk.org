<p><div class="verse"><span>23</span>On that day Sadducees (those who say that there is no resurrection) came to him. They asked him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>saying, “Teacher, Moses said, ‘If a man dies, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up offspring for his brother.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Now there were with us seven brothers. The first married and died, and having no offspring left his wife to his brother. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>In the same way, the second also, and the third, to the seventh. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>After them all, the woman died. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>In the resurrection therefore, whose wife will she be of the seven? For they all had her.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>But Jesus answered them, “You are mistaken, not knowing the Scriptures, nor the power of God. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are like God’s angels in heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>But concerning the resurrection of the dead, haven’t you read that which was spoken to you by God, saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>‘I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob?’ God is not the God of the dead, but of the living.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>When the multitudes heard it, they were astonished at his teaching. </div></p>
