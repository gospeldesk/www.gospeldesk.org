<div class="verse"><span>35</span>One of them, a lawyer, asked him a question, testing him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>“Teacher, which is the greatest commandment in the law?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>Jesus said to him, “‘You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>This is the first and great commandment. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>A second likewise is this, ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>The whole law and the prophets depend on these two commandments.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>Now while the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them a question, </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>saying, “What do you think of the Christ? Whose son is he?” They said to him, “Of David.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>He said to them, “How then does David in the Spirit call him Lord, saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>‘The Lord said to my Lord, sit on my right hand, until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>“If then David calls him Lord, how is he his son?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>No one was able to answer him a word, neither did any man dare ask him any more questions from that day forward. </div>
