<div class="verse"><span>13</span>“Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you devour widows’ houses, and as a pretense you make long prayers. Therefore you will receive greater condemnation. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>“But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you shut up the Kingdom of Heaven against men; for you don’t enter in yourselves, neither do you allow those who are entering in to enter. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you travel around by sea and land to make one proselyte; and when he becomes one, you make him twice as much a son of Gehenna as yourselves. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>“Woe to you, you blind guides, who say, ‘Whoever swears by the temple, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gold of the temple, he is obligated.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>You blind fools! For which is greater, the gold, or the temple that sanctifies the gold? </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>‘Whoever swears by the altar, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gift that is on it, he is obligated?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>You blind fools! For which is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifies the gift? </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>He therefore who swears by the altar, swears by it, and by everything on it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>He who swears by the temple, swears by it, and by him who has been living in it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>He who swears by heaven, swears by the throne of God, and by him who sits on it. </div>
