<div class="verse"><span>14</span>“For it is like a man, going into another country, who called his own servants, and entrusted his goods to them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>To one he gave five talents, to another two, to another one; to each according to his own ability. Then he went on his journey. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>Immediately he who received the five talents went and traded with them, and made another five talents. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>In the same way, he also who got the two gained another two. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>But he who received the one talent went away and dug in the earth, and hid his lord’s money. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>“Now after a long time the lord of those servants came, and reconciled accounts with them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>He who received the five talents came and brought another five talents, saying, ‘Lord, you delivered to me five talents. Behold, I have gained another five talents besides them.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>“His lord said to him, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant. You have been faithful over a few things, I will set you over many things. Enter into the joy of your lord.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>“He also who got the two talents came and said, ‘Lord, you delivered to me two talents. Behold, I have gained another two talents besides them.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>“His lord said to him, ‘Well done, good and faithful servant. You have been faithful over a few things, I will set you over many things. Enter into the joy of your lord.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>“He also who had received the one talent came and said, ‘Lord, I knew you that you are a hard man, reaping where you did not sow, and gathering where you did not scatter. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>I was afraid, and went away and hid your talent in the earth. Behold, you have what is yours.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>“But his lord answered him, ‘You wicked and slothful servant. You knew that I reap where I didn’t sow, and gather where I didn’t scatter. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>You ought therefore to have deposited my money with the bankers, and at my coming I should have received back my own with interest. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>Take away therefore the talent from him, and give it to him who has the ten talents. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>For to everyone who has will be given, and he will have abundance, but from him who doesn’t have, even that which he has will be taken away. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Throw out the unprofitable servant into the outer darkness, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’ </div>
