<div class="passage">
<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">3</span>Then the chief priests, the scribes, and the elders of the people were gathered together in the court of the high priest, who was called Caiaphas. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">4</span>They took counsel together that they might take Jesus by deceit, and kill him. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">5</span>But they said, “Not during the feast, lest a riot occur among the people.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">6</span>Now when Jesus was in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper, </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">7</span>a woman came to him having an alabaster jar of very expensive ointment, and she poured it on his head as he sat at the table. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">8</span>But when his disciples saw this, they were indignant, saying, “Why this waste? </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">9</span>For this ointment might have been sold for much, and given to the poor.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">10</span>However, knowing this, Jesus said to them, “Why do you trouble the woman? She has done a good work for me. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">11</span>For you always have the poor with you; but you don’t always have me. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">12</span>For in pouring this ointment on my body, she did it to prepare me for burial. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">13</span>Most certainly I tell you, wherever this Good News is preached in the whole world, what this woman has done will also be spoken of as a memorial of her.” </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">14</span>Then one of the twelve, who was called Judas Iscariot, went to the chief priests, </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">15</span>and said, “What are you willing to give me, that I should deliver him to you?” They weighed out for him thirty pieces of silver. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">16</span>From that time he sought opportunity to betray him. </span></p>
