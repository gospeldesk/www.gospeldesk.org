<div class="verse"><span>33</span>When they came to a place called “Golgotha”, that is to say, “The place of a skull,” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>they gave him sour wine to drink mixed with gall. When he had tasted it, he would not drink. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>When they had crucified him, they divided his clothing among them, casting lots, </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>and they sat and watched him there. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>They set up over his head the accusation against him written, “THIS IS JESUS, THE KING OF THE JEWS.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>Then there were two robbers crucified with him, one on his right hand and one on the left. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>Those who passed by blasphemed him, wagging their heads, </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>and saying, “You who destroy the temple, and build it in three days, save yourself! If you are the Son of God, come down from the cross!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>Likewise the chief priests also mocking, with the scribes, the Pharisees, and the elders, said, </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>“He saved others, but he can’t save himself. If he is the King of Israel, let him come down from the cross now, and we will believe in him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>He trusts in God. Let God deliver him now, if he wants him; for he said, ‘I am the Son of God.’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>The robbers also who were crucified with him cast on him the same reproach. </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>Now from the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land until the ninth hour. </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>About the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, “Eli, Eli, lima sabachthani?” That is, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>Some of them who stood there, when they heard it, said, “This man is calling Elijah.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>48</span>Immediately one of them ran, and took a sponge, and filled it with vinegar, and put it on a reed, and gave him a drink. </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>The rest said, “Let him be. Let’s see whether Elijah comes to save him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>50</span>Jesus cried again with a loud voice, and yielded up his spirit. </div> <div class="verse"><span>51</span>Behold, the veil of the temple was torn in two from the top to the bottom. The earth quaked and the rocks were split. </div> <div class="verse"><span>52</span>The tombs were opened, and many bodies of the saints who had fallen asleep were raised; </div> <div class="verse"><span>53</span>and coming out of the tombs after his resurrection, they entered into the holy city and appeared to many. </div> <div class="verse"><span>54</span>Now the centurion, and those who were with him watching Jesus, when they saw the earthquake, and the things that were done, feared exceedingly, saying, “Truly this was the Son of God.” </div>
