<div class="verse"><span>55</span>Many women were there watching from afar, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, serving him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>56</span>Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joses, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee. </div> <div class="verse"><span>57</span>When evening had come, a rich man from Arimathaea, named Joseph, who himself was also Jesus’ disciple came. </div> <div class="verse"><span>58</span>This man went to Pilate, and asked for Jesus’ body. Then Pilate commanded the body to be given up. </div> <div class="verse"><span>59</span>Joseph took the body, and wrapped it in a clean linen cloth, </div> <div class="verse"><span>60</span>and laid it in his own new tomb, which he had cut out in the rock, and he rolled a great stone against the door of the tomb, and departed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>61</span>Mary Magdalene was there, and the other Mary, sitting opposite the tomb. </div>
