<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">55</span>Many women were there watching from afar, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, serving him. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">56</span>Among them were Mary Magdalene, Mary the mother of James and Joses, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">57</span>When evening had come, a rich man from Arimathaea, named Joseph, who himself was also Jesus’ disciple came. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">58</span>This man went to Pilate, and asked for Jesus’ body. Then Pilate commanded the body to be given up. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">59</span>Joseph took the body, and wrapped it in a clean linen cloth, </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">60</span>and laid it in his own new tomb, which he had cut out in the rock, and he rolled a great stone against the door of the tomb, and departed. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">61</span>Mary Magdalene was there, and the other Mary, sitting opposite the tomb. </span></p>
