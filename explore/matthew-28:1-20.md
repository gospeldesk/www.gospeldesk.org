<div class="verse"><span>1</span>Now after the Sabbath, as it began to dawn on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to see the tomb. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Behold, there was a great earthquake, for an angel of the Lord descended from the sky, and came and rolled away the stone from the door, and sat on it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>His appearance was like lightning, and his clothing white as snow. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>For fear of him, the guards shook, and became like dead men. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>The angel answered the women, “Don’t be afraid, for I know that you seek Jesus, who has been crucified. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>He is not here, for he has risen, just like he said. Come, see the place where the Lord was lying. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Go quickly and tell his disciples, ‘He has risen from the dead, and behold, he goes before you into Galilee; there you will see him.’ Behold, I have told you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>They departed quickly from the tomb with fear and great joy, and ran to bring his disciples word. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>As they went to tell his disciples, behold, Jesus met them, saying, “Rejoice!” They came and took hold of his feet, and worshiped him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Then Jesus said to them, “Don’t be afraid. Go tell my brothers that they should go into Galilee, and there they will see me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>Now while they were going, behold, some of the guards came into the city, and told the chief priests all the things that had happened. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>When they were assembled with the elders, and had taken counsel, they gave a large amount of silver to the soldiers, </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>saying, “Say that his disciples came by night, and stole him away while we slept. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>If this comes to the governor’s ears, we will persuade him and make you free of worry.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>So they took the money and did as they were told. This saying was spread abroad among the Jews, and continues until today. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>But the eleven disciples went into Galilee, to the mountain where Jesus had sent them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>When they saw him, they bowed down to him, but some doubted. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Jesus came to them and spoke to them, saying, “All authority has been given to me in heaven and on earth. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>teaching them to observe all things that I commanded you. Behold, I am with you always, even to the end of the age.” Amen. </div>
