<div class="verse"><span>12</span>Now when Jesus heard that John was delivered up, he withdrew into Galilee. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Leaving Nazareth, he came and lived in Capernaum, which is by the sea, in the region of Zebulun and Naphtali, </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>“The land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, toward the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles, </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>the people who sat in darkness saw a great light, to those who sat in the region and shadow of death, to them light has dawned.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>From that time, Jesus began to preach, and to say, “Repent! For the Kingdom of Heaven is at hand.” </div>
