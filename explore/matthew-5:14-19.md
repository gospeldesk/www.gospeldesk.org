<div class="verse"><span>14</span>You are the light of the world. A city located on a hill can’t be hidden. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Neither do you light a lamp, and put it under a measuring basket, but on a stand; and it shines to all who are in the house. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>Even so, let your light shine before men; that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father who is in heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>“Don’t think that I came to destroy the law or the prophets. I didn’t come to destroy, but to fulfill. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>For most certainly, I tell you, until heaven and earth pass away, not even one smallest letter or one tiny pen stroke shall in any way pass away from the law, until all things are accomplished. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Whoever, therefore, shall break one of these least commandments, and teach others to do so, shall be called least in the Kingdom of Heaven; but whoever shall do and teach them shall be called great in the Kingdom of Heaven. </div>
