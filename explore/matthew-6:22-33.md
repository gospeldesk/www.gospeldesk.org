<div class="verse"><span>22</span>“The lamp of the body is the eye. If therefore your eye is sound, your whole body will be full of light. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>But if your eye is evil, your whole body will be full of darkness. If therefore the light that is in you is darkness, how great is the darkness! </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>“No one can serve two masters, for either he will hate the one and love the other; or else he will be devoted to one and despise the other. You can’t serve both God and Mammon. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Therefore I tell you, don’t be anxious for your life: what you will eat, or what you will drink; nor yet for your body, what you will wear. Isn’t life more than food, and the body more than clothing? </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>See the birds of the sky, that they don’t sow, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns. Your heavenly Father feeds them. Aren’t you of much more value than they? </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>“Which of you, by being anxious, can add one moment to his lifespan? </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>Why are you anxious about clothing? Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow. They don’t toil, neither do they spin, </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>yet I tell you that even Solomon in all his glory was not dressed like one of these. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>But if God so clothes the grass of the field, which today exists, and tomorrow is thrown into the oven, won’t he much more clothe you, you of little faith? </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>“Therefore don’t be anxious, saying, ‘What will we eat?’, ‘What will we drink?’ or, ‘With what will we be clothed?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>For the Gentiles seek after all these things; for your heavenly Father knows that you need all these things. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>But seek first God’s Kingdom, and his righteousness; and all these things will be given to you as well. </div>
