<div class="verse"><span>14</span>When Jesus came into Peter’s house, he saw his wife’s mother lying sick with a fever. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>He touched her hand, and the fever left her. She got up and served him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>When evening came, they brought to him many possessed with demons. He cast out the spirits with a word, and healed all who were sick; </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through Isaiah the prophet, saying, “He took our infirmities, and bore our diseases.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Now when Jesus saw great multitudes around him, he gave the order to depart to the other side. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>A scribe came, and said to him, “Teacher, I will follow you wherever you go.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Jesus said to him, “The foxes have holes, and the birds of the sky have nests, but the Son of Man has nowhere to lay his head.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>Another of his disciples said to him, “Lord, allow me first to go and bury my father.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>But Jesus said to him, “Follow me, and leave the dead to bury their own dead.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>When he got into a boat, his disciples followed him. </div>
