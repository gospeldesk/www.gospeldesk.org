<div class="verse"><span>23</span>When he got into a boat, his disciples followed him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Behold, a violent storm came up on the sea, so much that the boat was covered with the waves, but he was asleep. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>They came to him, and woke him up, saying, “Save us, Lord! We are dying!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>He said to them, “Why are you fearful, O you of little faith?” Then he got up, rebuked the wind and the sea, and there was a great calm. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>The men marveled, saying, “What kind of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?” </div>
