<div class="verse"><span>5</span>When he came into Capernaum, a centurion came to him, asking him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>and saying, “Lord, my servant lies in the house paralyzed, grievously tormented.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Jesus said to him, “I will come and heal him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>The centurion answered, “Lord, I’m not worthy for you to come under my roof. Just say the word, and my servant will be healed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>For I am also a man under authority, having under myself soldiers. I tell this one, ‘Go,’ and he goes; and tell another, ‘Come,’ and he comes; and tell my servant, ‘Do this,’ and he does it.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>When Jesus heard it, he marveled, and said to those who followed, “Most certainly I tell you, I haven’t found so great a faith, not even in Israel. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>I tell you that many will come from the east and the west, and will sit down with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the Kingdom of Heaven, </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>but the children of the Kingdom will be thrown out into the outer darkness. There will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Jesus said to the centurion, “Go your way. Let it be done for you as you have believed.” His servant was healed in that hour. </div>
