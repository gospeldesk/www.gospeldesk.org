<div class="verse"><span>1</span>He entered into a boat, and crossed over, and came into his own city. </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Behold, they brought to him a man who was paralyzed, lying on a bed. Jesus, seeing their faith, said to the paralytic, “Son, cheer up! Your sins are forgiven you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>Behold, some of the scribes said to themselves, “This man blasphemes.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Jesus, knowing their thoughts, said, “Why do you think evil in your hearts? </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>For which is easier, to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven;’ or to say, ‘Get up, and walk?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins-” (then he said to the paralytic), “Get up, and take up your mat, and go to your house.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>He arose and departed to his house. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>But when the multitudes saw it, they marveled and glorified God, who had given such authority to men. </div>
