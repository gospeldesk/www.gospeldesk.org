<div class="verse"><span>18</span>While he told these things to them, behold, a ruler came and worshiped him, saying, “My daughter has just died, but come and lay your hand on her, and she will live.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Jesus got up and followed him, as did his disciples. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Behold, a woman who had an issue of blood for twelve years came behind him, and touched the fringe of his garment; </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>for she said within herself, “If I just touch his garment, I will be made well.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>But Jesus, turning around and seeing her, said, “Daughter, cheer up! Your faith has made you well.” And the woman was made well from that hour. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>When Jesus came into the ruler’s house, and saw the flute players, and the crowd in noisy disorder, </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>he said to them, “Make room, because the girl isn’t dead, but sleeping.” They were ridiculing him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>But when the crowd was put out, he entered in, took her by the hand, and the girl arose. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>The report of this went out into all that land. </div>
