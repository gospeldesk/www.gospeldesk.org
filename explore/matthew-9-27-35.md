<div class="verse"><span>27</span>As Jesus passed by from there, two blind men followed him, calling out and saying, “Have mercy on us, son of David!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>When he had come into the house, the blind men came to him. Jesus said to them, “Do you believe that I am able to do this?” They told him, “Yes, Lord.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>Then he touched their eyes, saying, “According to your faith be it done to you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>Their eyes were opened. Jesus strictly commanded them, saying, “See that no one knows about this.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>But they went out and spread abroad his fame in all that land. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>As they went out, behold, a mute man who was demon possessed was brought to him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>When the demon was cast out, the mute man spoke. The multitudes marveled, saying, “Nothing like this has ever been seen in Israel!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>But the Pharisees said, “By the prince of the demons, he casts out demons.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>Jesus went about all the cities and the villages, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the Good News of the Kingdom, and healing every disease and every sickness among the people. </div>
