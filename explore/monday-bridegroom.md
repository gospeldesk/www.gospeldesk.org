<div class="verse"><span>18</span>Now in the morning, as he returned to the city, he was hungry. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Seeing a fig tree by the road, he came to it, and found nothing on it but leaves. He said to it, “Let there be no fruit from you forever!” Immediately the fig tree withered away. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>When the disciples saw it, they marveled, saying, “How did the fig tree immediately wither away?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>Jesus answered them, “Most certainly I tell you, if you have faith, and don’t doubt, you will not only do what was done to the fig tree, but even if you told this mountain, ‘Be taken up and cast into the sea,’ it would be done. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>All things, whatever you ask in prayer, believing, you will receive.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>When he had come into the temple, the chief priests and the elders of the people came to him as he was teaching, and said, “By what authority do you do these things? Who gave you this authority?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Jesus answered them, “I also will ask you one question, which if you tell me, I likewise will tell you by what authority I do these things. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>The baptism of John, where was it from? From heaven or from men?” They reasoned with themselves, saying, “If we say, ‘From heaven,’ he will ask us, ‘Why then did you not believe him?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>But if we say, ‘From men,’ we fear the multitude, for all hold John as a prophet.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>They answered Jesus, and said, “We don’t know.” He also said to them, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>But what do you think? A man had two sons, and he came to the first, and said, ‘Son, go work today in my vineyard.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>He answered, ‘I will not,’ but afterward he changed his mind, and went. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>He came to the second, and said the same thing. He answered, ‘I’ m going, sir,’ but he didn’t go. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Which of the two did the will of his father?” They said to him, “The first.” Jesus said to them, “Most certainly I tell you that the tax collectors and the prostitutes are entering into God’s Kingdom before you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you didn’t believe him, but the tax collectors and the prostitutes believed him. When you saw it, you didn’t even repent afterward, that you might believe him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>“Hear another parable. There was a man who was a master of a household, who planted a vineyard, set a hedge about it, dug a wine press in it, built a tower, leased it out to farmers, and went into another country. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>When the season for the fruit came near, he sent his servants to the farmers, to receive his fruit. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>The farmers took his servants, beat one, killed another, and stoned another. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>Again, he sent other servants more than the first: and they treated them the same way. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>But afterward he sent to them his son, saying, ‘They will respect my son.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>But the farmers, when they saw the son, said among themselves, ‘This is the heir. Come, let’s kill him, and seize his inheritance.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>So they took him, and threw him out of the vineyard, and killed him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>When therefore the lord of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those farmers?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>They told him, “He will miserably destroy those miserable men, and will lease out the vineyard to other farmers, who will give him the fruit in its season.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>Jesus said to them, “Did you never read in the Scriptures, ‘The stone which the builders rejected, the same was made the head of the corner. This was from the Lord. It is marvelous in our eyes?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>“Therefore I tell you, God’s Kingdom will be taken away from you, and will be given to a nation producing its fruit. </div>
