<div class="verse"><span>3</span>As he sat on the Mount of Olives, the disciples came to him privately, saying, “Tell us, when will these things be? What is the sign of your coming, and of the end of the age?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Jesus answered them, “Be careful that no one leads you astray. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>For many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am the Christ,’ and will lead many astray. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>You will hear of wars and rumors of wars. See that you aren’t troubled, for all this must happen, but the end is not yet. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; and there will be famines, plagues, and earthquakes in various places. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>But all these things are the beginning of birth pains. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>Then they will deliver you up to oppression, and will kill you. You will be hated by all of the nations for my name’s sake. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Then many will stumble, and will deliver up one another, and will hate one another. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>Many false prophets will arise, and will lead many astray. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>Because iniquity will be multiplied, the love of many will grow cold. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>But he who endures to the end, the same will be saved. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>This Good News of the Kingdom will be preached in the whole world for a testimony to all the nations, and then the end will come. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>“When, therefore, you see the abomination of desolation, which was spoken of through Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place (let the reader understand), </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Let him who is on the housetop not go down to take out the things that are in his house. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Let him who is in the field not return back to get his clothes. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>But woe to those who are with child and to nursing mothers in those days! </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Pray that your flight will not be in the winter, nor on a Sabbath, </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>for then there will be great oppression, such as has not been from the beginning of the world until now, no, nor ever will be. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Unless those days had been shortened, no flesh would have been saved. But for the sake of the chosen ones, those days will be shortened. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>“Then if any man tells you, ‘Behold, here is the Christ,’ or, ‘There,’ don’t believe it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>For there will arise false christs, and false prophets, and they will show great signs and wonders, so as to lead astray, if possible, even the chosen ones. </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>“Behold, I have told you beforehand. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>If therefore they tell you, ‘Behold, he is in the wilderness,’ don’t go out; ‘Behold, he is in the inner rooms,’ don’t believe it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>For as the lightning flashes from the east, and is seen even to the west, so will be the coming of the Son of Man. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>For wherever the carcass is, that is where the vultures gather together. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>But immediately after the oppression of those days, the sun will be darkened, the moon will not give its light, the stars will fall from the sky, and the powers of the heavens will be shaken; </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>and then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in the sky. Then all the tribes of the earth will mourn, and they will see the Son of Man coming on the clouds of the sky with power and great glory. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>He will send out his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they will gather together his chosen ones from the four winds, from one end of the sky to the other. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>“Now from the fig tree learn this parable. When its branch has now become tender, and produces its leaves, you know that the summer is near. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>Even so you also, when you see all these things, know that it is near, even at the doors. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Most certainly I tell you, this generation will not pass away, until all these things are accomplished. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away. </div>
