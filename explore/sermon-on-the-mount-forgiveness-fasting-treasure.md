<div class="verse"><span>14</span>“For if you forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>But if you don’t forgive men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>“Moreover when you fast, don’t be like the hypocrites, with sad faces. For they disfigure their faces, that they may be seen by men to be fasting. Most certainly I tell you, they have received their reward. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>But you, when you fast, anoint your head, and wash your face; </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>so that you are not seen by men to be fasting, but by your Father who is in secret, and your Father, who sees in secret, will reward you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>“Don’t lay up treasures for yourselves on the earth, where moth and rust consume, and where thieves break through and steal; </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>but lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust consume, and where thieves don’t break through and steal; </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>for where your treasure is, there your heart will be also. </div>
