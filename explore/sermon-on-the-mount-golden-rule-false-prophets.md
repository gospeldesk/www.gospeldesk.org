<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">12</span>Therefore whatever you desire for men to do to you, you shall also do to them; for this is the law and the prophets. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">13</span>“Enter in by the narrow gate; for wide is the gate and broad is the way that leads to destruction, and many are those who enter in by it. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">14</span>How narrow is the gate, and restricted is the way that leads to life! Few are those who find it. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">15</span>“Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly are ravening wolves. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">16</span>By their fruits you will know them. Do you gather grapes from thorns, or figs from thistles? </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">17</span>Even so, every good tree produces good fruit; but the corrupt tree produces evil fruit. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">18</span>A good tree can’t produce evil fruit, neither can a corrupt tree produce good fruit. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">19</span>Every tree that doesn’t grow good fruit is cut down, and thrown into the fire. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">20</span>Therefore by their fruits you will know them. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">21</span>Not everyone who says to me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ will enter into the Kingdom of Heaven; but he who does the will of my Father who is in heaven. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">22</span>Many will tell me in that day, ‘Lord, Lord, didn’t we prophesy in your name, in your name cast out demons, and in your name do many mighty works?’ </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">23</span>Then I will tell them, ‘I never knew you. Depart from me, you who work iniquity.’ </span></p>
