<div class="verse"><span>33</span>“Again you have heard that it was said to them of old time, ‘You shall not make false vows, but shall perform to the Lord your vows,’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>but I tell you, don’t swear at all: neither by heaven, for it is the throne of God; </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>nor by the earth, for it is the footstool of his feet; nor by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the great King. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>Neither shall you swear by your head, for you can’t make one hair white or black. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>But let your ‘Yes’ be ‘Yes’ and your ‘No’ be ‘No.’ Whatever is more than these is of the evil one. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>“You have heard that it was said, ‘An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>But I tell you, don’t resist him who is evil; but whoever strikes you on your right cheek, turn to him the other also. </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>If anyone sues you to take away your coat, let him have your cloak also. </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>Whoever compels you to go one mile, go with him two. </div>
