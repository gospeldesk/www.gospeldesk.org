<div class="verse"><span>1</span>The book of the genealogy of Jesus Christ, </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>Abraham became the father of Isaac. Isaac became the father of Jacob. Jacob became the father of Judah and his brothers. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>Judah became the father of Perez and Zerah by Tamar. Perez became the father of Hezron. Hezron became the father of Ram. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>Ram became the father of Amminadab. Amminadab became the father of Nahshon. Nahshon became the father of Salmon. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Salmon became the father of Boaz by Rahab. Boaz became the father of Obed by Ruth. Obed became the father of Jesse. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>Jesse became the father of King David. David became the father of Solomon by her who had been Uriah’s wife. </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Solomon became the father of Rehoboam. Rehoboam became the father of Abijah. Abijah became the father of Asa. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>Asa became the father of Jehoshaphat. Jehoshaphat became the father of Joram. Joram became the father of Uzziah. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>Uzziah became the father of Jotham. Jotham became the father of Ahaz. Ahaz became the father of Hezekiah. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Hezekiah became the father of Manasseh. Manasseh became the father of Amon. Amon became the father of Josiah. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>Josiah became the father of Jechoniah and his brothers, at the time of the exile to Babylon. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>After the exile to Babylon, Jechoniah became the father of Shealtiel. Shealtiel became the father of Zerubbabel. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Zerubbabel became the father of Abiud. Abiud became the father of Eliakim. Eliakim became the father of Azor. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Azor became the father of Zadok. Zadok became the father of Achim. Achim became the father of Eliud. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Eliud became the father of Eleazar. Eleazar became the father of Matthan. Matthan became the father of Jacob. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>Jacob became the father of Joseph, the husband of Mary, from whom was born Jesus, </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>So all the generations from Abraham to David are fourteen generations; from David to the exile to Babylon fourteen generations; and from the carrying away to Babylon to the Christ, fourteen generations. </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>Now the birth of Jesus Christ was like this: After his mother, Mary, was engaged to Joseph, before they came together, she was found pregnant by the Holy Spirit. </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Joseph, her husband, being a righteous man, and not willing to make her a public example, intended to put her away secretly. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>But when he thought about these things, behold, </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>She shall give birth to a son. You shall call his name Jesus, </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>Now all this has happened, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the Lord through the prophet, saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>“Behold, the virgin shall be with child, and shall give birth to a son. They shall call his name Immanuel”; which is, being interpreted, “God with us.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>Joseph arose from his sleep, and did as the angel of the Lord commanded him, and took his wife to himself; </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>and didn’t know her sexually until she had given birth to her firstborn son. He named him Jesus. </div>
