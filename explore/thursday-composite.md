<div class="verse"><span>2</span>“You know that after two days the Passover is coming, and the Son of Man will be delivered up to be crucified.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>Then the chief priests, the scribes, and the elders of the people were gathered together in the court of the high priest, who was called Caiaphas. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>They took counsel together that they might take Jesus by deceit, and kill him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>But they said, “Not during the feast, lest a riot occur among the people.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>Now when Jesus was in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper, </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>a woman came to him having an alabaster jar of very expensive ointment, and she poured it on his head as he sat at the table. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>But when his disciples saw this, they were indignant, saying, “Why this waste? </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>For this ointment might have been sold for much, and given to the poor.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>However, knowing this, Jesus said to them, “Why do you trouble the woman? She has done a good work for me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>For you always have the poor with you; but you don’t always have me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>For in pouring this ointment on my body, she did it to prepare me for burial. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Most certainly I tell you, wherever this Good News is preached in the whole world, what this woman has done will also be spoken of as a memorial of her.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Then one of the twelve, who was called Judas Iscariot, went to the chief priests, </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>and said, “What are you willing to give me, that I should deliver him to you?” They weighed out for him thirty pieces of silver. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>From that time he sought opportunity to betray him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Now on the first day of unleavened bread, the disciples came to Jesus, saying to him, “Where do you want us to prepare for you to eat the Passover?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>He said, “Go into the city to a certain person, and tell him, ‘The Teacher says, “My time is at hand. I will keep the Passover at your house with my disciples.”’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>The disciples did as Jesus commanded them, and they prepared the Passover. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>Now when evening had come, he was reclining at the table with the twelve disciples. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>Jesus, knowing that the Father had given all things into his hands, and that he came from God, and was going to God, </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>arose from supper, and laid aside his outer garments. He took a towel, and wrapped a towel around his waist. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>Then he poured water into the basin, and began to wash the disciples’ feet, and to wipe them with the towel that was wrapped around him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>Then he came to Simon Peter. He said to him, “Lord, do you wash my feet?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>Jesus answered him, “You don’t know what I am doing now, but you will understand later.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>Peter said to him, “You will never wash my feet!” Jesus answered him, “If I don’t wash you, you have no part with me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, not my feet only, but also my hands and my head!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Jesus said to him, “Someone who has bathed only needs to have his feet washed, but is completely clean. You are clean, but not all of you.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>For he knew him who would betray him, therefore he said, “You are not all clean.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>So when he had washed their feet, put his outer garment back on, and sat down again, he said to them, “Do you know what I have done to you? </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>You call me, ‘Teacher’ and ‘Lord.’ You say so correctly, for so I am. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>If I then, the Lord and the Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another’s feet. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>For I have given you an example, that you also should do as I have done to you. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>Most certainly I tell you, a servant is not greater than his lord, neither one who is sent greater than he who sent him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>If you know these things, blessed are you if you do them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>As they were eating, he said, “Most certainly I tell you that one of you will betray me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>They were exceedingly sorrowful, and each began to ask him, “It isn’t me, is it, Lord?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>He answered, “He who dipped his hand with me in the dish, the same will betray me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>The Son of Man goes, even as it is written of him, but woe to that man through whom the Son of Man is betrayed! It would be better for that man if he had not been born.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Judas, who betrayed him, answered, “It isn’t me, is it, Rabbi?” He said to him, “You said it.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>As they were eating, Jesus took bread, gave thanks for it, and broke it. He gave to the disciples, and said, “Take, eat; this is my body.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>He took the cup, gave thanks, and gave to them, saying, “All of you drink it, </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>for this is my blood of the new covenant, which is poured out for many for the remission of sins. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>But I tell you that I will not drink of this fruit of the vine from now on, until that day when I drink it anew with you in my Father’s Kingdom.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>When they had sung a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Then Jesus said to them, “All of you will be made to stumble because of me tonight, for it is written, ‘I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>But after I am raised up, I will go before you into Galilee.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>But Peter answered him, “Even if all will be made to stumble because of you, I will never be made to stumble.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Jesus said to him, “Most certainly I tell you that tonight, before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>Peter said to him, “Even if I must die with you, I will not deny you.” All of the disciples also said likewise. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>Then Jesus came with them to a place called Gethsemane, and said to his disciples, “Sit here, while I go there and pray.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>He took with him Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, and began to be sorrowful and severely troubled. </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>Then he said to them, “My soul is exceedingly sorrowful, even to death. Stay here, and watch with me.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>He went forward a little, fell on his face, and prayed, saying, “My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass away from me; nevertheless, not what I desire, but what you desire.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>An angel from heaven appeared to him, strengthening him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>Being in agony he prayed more earnestly. His sweat became like great drops of blood falling down on the ground. </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>When he rose up from his prayer, he came to the disciples, and found them sleeping because of grief, </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>He came to the disciples, and found them sleeping, and said to Peter, “What, couldn’t you watch with me for one hour? </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>Watch and pray, that you don’t enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>Again, a second time he went away, and prayed, saying, “My Father, if this cup can’t pass away from me unless I drink it, your desire be done.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>He came again and found them sleeping, for their eyes were heavy. </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>He left them again, went away, and prayed a third time, saying the same words. </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>Then he came to his disciples, and said to them, “Sleep on now, and take your rest. Behold, the hour is at hand, and the Son of Man is betrayed into the hands of sinners. </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>Arise, let’s be going. Behold, he who betrays me is at hand.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>47</span>While he was still speaking, behold, Judas, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great multitude with swords and clubs, from the chief priests and elders of the people. </div> <div class="verse"><span>48</span>Now he who betrayed him gave them a sign, saying, “Whoever I kiss, he is the one. Seize him.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>49</span>Immediately he came to Jesus, and said, “Hail, Rabbi!” and kissed him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>50</span>Jesus said to him, “Friend, why are you here?” Then they came and laid hands on Jesus, and took him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>51</span>Behold, one of those who were with Jesus stretched out his hand, and drew his sword, and struck the servant of the high priest, and struck off his ear. </div> <div class="verse"><span>52</span>Then Jesus said to him, “Put your sword back into its place, for all those who take the sword will die by the sword. </div> <div class="verse"><span>53</span>Or do you think that I couldn’t ask my Father, and he would even now send me more than twelve legions of angels? </div> <div class="verse"><span>54</span>How then would the Scriptures be fulfilled that it must be so?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>55</span>In that hour Jesus said to the multitudes, “Have you come out as against a robber with swords and clubs to seize me? I sat daily in the temple teaching, and you didn’t arrest me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>56</span>But all this has happened, that the Scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled.” Then all the disciples left him, and fled. </div> <div class="verse"><span>57</span>Those who had taken Jesus led him away to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and the elders were gathered together. </div> <div class="verse"><span>58</span>But Peter followed him from a distance, to the court of the high priest, and entered in and sat with the officers, to see the end. </div> <div class="verse"><span>59</span>Now the chief priests, the elders, and the whole council sought false testimony against Jesus, that they might put him to death; </div> <div class="verse"><span>60</span>and they found none. Even though many false witnesses came forward, they found none. But at last two false witnesses came forward, </div> <div class="verse"><span>61</span>and said, “This man said, ‘I am able to destroy the temple of God, and to build it in three days.’” </div> <div class="verse"><span>62</span>The high priest stood up, and said to him, “Have you no answer? What is this that these testify against you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>63</span>But Jesus held his peace. The high priest answered him, “I adjure you by the living God, that you tell us whether you are the Christ, the Son of God.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>64</span>Jesus said to him, “You have said it. Nevertheless, I tell you, after this you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power, and coming on the clouds of the sky.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>65</span>Then the high priest tore his clothing, saying, “He has spoken blasphemy! Why do we need any more witnesses? Behold, now you have heard his blasphemy. </div> <div class="verse"><span>66</span>What do you think?” They answered, “He is worthy of death!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>67</span>Then they spat in his face and beat him with their fists, and some slapped him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>68</span>saying, “Prophesy to us, you Christ! Who hit you?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>69</span>Now Peter was sitting outside in the court, and a maid came to him, saying, “You were also with Jesus, the Galilean!” </div> <div class="verse"><span>70</span>But he denied it before them all, saying, “I don’t know what you are talking about.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>71</span>When he had gone out onto the porch, someone else saw him, and said to those who were there, “This man also was with Jesus of Nazareth.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>72</span>Again he denied it with an oath, “I don’t know the man.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>73</span>After a little while those who stood by came and said to Peter, “Surely you are also one of them, for your speech makes you known.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>74</span>Then he began to curse and to swear, “I don’t know the man!” Immediately the rooster crowed. </div> <div class="verse"><span>75</span>Peter remembered the word which Jesus had said to him, “Before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times.” He went out and wept bitterly. </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>Now when morning had come, all the chief priests and the elders of the people took counsel against Jesus to put him to death: </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>and they bound him, and led him away, and delivered him up to Pontius Pilate, the governor. </div>
