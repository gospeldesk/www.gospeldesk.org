<div class="verse"><span>15</span>Then the Pharisees went and took counsel how they might entrap him in his talk. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>They sent their disciples to him, along with the Herodians, saying, “Teacher, we know that you are honest, and teach the way of God in truth, no matter whom you teach, for you aren’t partial to anyone. </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>Tell us therefore, what do you think? Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar, or not?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>But Jesus perceived their wickedness, and said, “Why do you test me, you hypocrites? </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>Show me the tax money.” They brought to him a denarius. </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>He asked them, “Whose is this image and inscription?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>They said to him, “Caesar’s.” Then he said to them, “Give therefore to Caesar the things that are Caesar’s, and to God the things that are God’s.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>When they heard it, they marveled, and left him, and went away. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>On that day Sadducees (those who say that there is no resurrection) came to him. They asked him, </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>saying, “Teacher, Moses said, ‘If a man dies, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up offspring </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>Now there were with us seven brothers. The first married and died, and having no offspring </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>In the same way, the second also, and the third, to the seventh. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>After them all, the woman died. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>In the resurrection therefore, whose wife will she be of the seven? For they all had her.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>But Jesus answered them, “You are mistaken, not knowing the Scriptures, nor the power of God. </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are like God’s angels in heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>But concerning the resurrection of the dead, haven’t you read that which was spoken to you by God, saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>‘I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob?’ God is not the God of the dead, but of the living.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>When the multitudes heard it, they were astonished at his teaching. </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>But the Pharisees, when they heard that he had silenced the Sadducees, gathered themselves together. </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>One of them, a lawyer, asked him a question, testing him. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>“Teacher, which is the greatest commandment in the law?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>Jesus said to him, “‘You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>This is the first and great commandment. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>A second likewise is this, ‘You shall love your neighbor as yourself.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>40</span>The whole law and the prophets depend on these two commandments.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>41</span>Now while the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them a question, </div> <div class="verse"><span>42</span>saying, “What do you think of the Christ? Whose son is he?” They said to him, “Of David.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>43</span>He said to them, “How then does David in the Spirit call him Lord, saying, </div> <div class="verse"><span>44</span>‘The Lord said to my Lord, sit on my right hand, until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>45</span>“If then David calls him Lord, how is he his son?” </div> <div class="verse"><span>46</span>No one was able to answer him a word, neither did any man dare ask him any more questions from that day forward. </div> <div class="verse"><span>1</span>Then Jesus spoke to the multitudes and to his disciples, </div> <div class="verse"><span>2</span>saying, “The scribes and the Pharisees sat on Moses’ seat. </div> <div class="verse"><span>3</span>All things therefore whatever they tell you to observe, observe and do, but don’t do their works; for they say, and don’t do. </div> <div class="verse"><span>4</span>For they bind heavy burdens that are grievous to be borne, and lay them on men’s shoulders; but they themselves will not lift a finger to help them. </div> <div class="verse"><span>5</span>But all their works they do to be seen by men. They make their phylacteries broad, enlarge the fringes of their garments, </div> <div class="verse"><span>6</span>and love the place of honor at feasts, the best seats in the synagogues, </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>the salutations in the marketplaces, and to be called ‘Rabbi, Rabbi’ by men. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>But don’t you be called ‘Rabbi,’ for one is your teacher, the Christ, and all of you are brothers. </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>Call no man on the earth your father, for one is your Father, he who is in heaven. </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>Neither be called masters, for one is your master, the Christ. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>But he who is greatest among you will be your servant. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>Whoever exalts himself will be humbled, and whoever humbles himself will be exalted. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>“Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you devour widows’ houses, and as a pretense you make long prayers. Therefore you will receive greater condemnation. </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>“But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! Because you shut up the Kingdom of Heaven against men; for you don’t enter in yourselves, neither do you allow those who are entering in to enter. </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you travel around by sea and land to make one proselyte; and when he becomes one, you make him twice as much a son of Gehenna as yourselves. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>“Woe to you, you blind guides, who say, ‘Whoever swears by the temple, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gold of the temple, he is obligated.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>17</span>You blind fools! For which is greater, the gold, or the temple that sanctifies the gold? </div> <div class="verse"><span>18</span>‘Whoever swears by the altar, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gift that is on it, he is obligated?’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>19</span>You blind fools! For which is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifies the gift? </div> <div class="verse"><span>20</span>He therefore who swears by the altar, swears by it, and by everything on it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>21</span>He who swears by the temple, swears by it, and by him who has been living in it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>22</span>He who swears by heaven, swears by the throne of God, and by him who sits on it. </div> <div class="verse"><span>23</span>“Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you tithe mint, dill, and cumin, and have left undone the weightier matters of the law: justice, mercy, and faith. But you ought to have done these, and not to have left the other undone. </div> <div class="verse"><span>24</span>You blind guides, who strain out a gnat, and swallow a camel! </div> <div class="verse"><span>25</span>“Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you clean the outside of the cup and of the platter, but within they are full of extortion and unrighteousness. </div> <div class="verse"><span>26</span>You blind Pharisee, first clean the inside of the cup and of the platter, that its outside may become clean also. </div> <div class="verse"><span>27</span>“Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you are like whitened tombs, which outwardly appear beautiful, but inwardly are full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness. </div> <div class="verse"><span>28</span>Even so you also outwardly appear righteous to men, but inwardly you are full of hypocrisy and iniquity. </div> <div class="verse"><span>29</span>“Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you build the tombs of the prophets, and decorate the tombs of the righteous, </div> <div class="verse"><span>30</span>and say, ‘If we had lived in the days of our fathers, we wouldn’t have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.’ </div> <div class="verse"><span>31</span>Therefore you testify to yourselves that you are children of those who killed the prophets. </div> <div class="verse"><span>32</span>Fill up, then, the measure of your fathers. </div> <div class="verse"><span>33</span>You serpents, you offspring of vipers, how will you escape the judgment of Gehenna? </div> <div class="verse"><span>34</span>Therefore behold, I send to you prophets, wise men, and scribes. Some of them you will kill and crucify; and some of them you will scourge in your synagogues, and persecute from city to city; </div> <div class="verse"><span>35</span>that on you may come all the righteous blood shed on the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zachariah son of Barachiah, whom you killed between the sanctuary and the altar. </div> <div class="verse"><span>36</span>Most certainly I tell you, all these things will come upon this generation. </div> <div class="verse"><span>37</span>“Jerusalem, Jerusalem, who kills the prophets, and stones those who are sent to her! How often I would have gathered your children together, even as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings, and you would not! </div> <div class="verse"><span>38</span>Behold, your house is left to you desolate. </div> <div class="verse"><span>39</span>For I tell you, you will not see me from now on, until you say, ‘Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord!’” </div>
