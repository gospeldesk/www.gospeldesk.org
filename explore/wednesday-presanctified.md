<div class="verse"><span>6</span>Now when Jesus was in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper, </div> <div class="verse"><span>7</span>a woman came to him having an alabaster jar of very expensive ointment, and she poured it on his head as he sat at the table. </div> <div class="verse"><span>8</span>But when his disciples saw this, they were indignant, saying, “Why this waste? </div> <div class="verse"><span>9</span>For this ointment might have been sold for much, and given to the poor.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>10</span>However, knowing this, Jesus said to them, “Why do you trouble the woman? She has done a good work for me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>11</span>For you always have the poor with you; but you don’t always have me. </div> <div class="verse"><span>12</span>For in pouring this ointment on my body, she did it to prepare me for burial. </div> <div class="verse"><span>13</span>Most certainly I tell you, wherever this Good News is preached in the whole world, what this woman has done will also be spoken of as a memorial of her.” </div> <div class="verse"><span>14</span>Then one of the twelve, who was called Judas Iscariot, went to the chief priests, </div> <div class="verse"><span>15</span>and said, “What are you willing to give me, that I should deliver him to you?” They weighed out for him thirty pieces of silver. </div> <div class="verse"><span>16</span>From that time he sought opportunity to betray him. </div>
