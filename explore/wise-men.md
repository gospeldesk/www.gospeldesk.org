<div class="passage">
<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">1</span>Now when Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of King Herod, behold, wise men from the east came to Jerusalem, saying, </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">2</span>“Where is he who is born King of the Jews? For we saw his star in the east, and have come to worship him.” </span></p>
<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">3</span>When King Herod heard it, he was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">4</span>Gathering together all the chief priests and scribes of the people, he asked them where the Christ would be born. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">5</span>They said to him, “In Bethlehem of Judea, for this is written through the prophet, </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">6</span>‘You Bethlehem, land of Judah, are in no way least among the princes of Judah: for out of you shall come a governor, who shall shepherd my people, Israel.’” </span></p>
<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">7</span>Then Herod secretly called the wise men, and learned from them exactly what time the star appeared. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">8</span>He sent them to Bethlehem, and said, “Go and search diligently for the young child. When you have found him, bring me word, so that I also may come and worship him.” </span></p>
<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">9</span>They, having heard the king, went their way; and behold, the star, which they saw in the east, went before them, until it came and stood over where the young child was. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">10</span>When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceedingly great joy. </span> <span class="verse"><span class="v">11</span>They came into the house and saw the young child with Mary, his mother, and they fell down and worshiped him. Opening their treasures, they offered to him gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh. </span></p>
<p><span class="verse"><span class="v">12</span>Being warned in a dream that they shouldn’t return to Herod, they went back to their own country another way. </span></p>
</div>
