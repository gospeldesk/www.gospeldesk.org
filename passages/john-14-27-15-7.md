<div class="passage">
<p><span class="v">27</span>Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, give I to you. Don’t let your heart be troubled, neither let it be fearful.  <span class="v">28</span>You heard how I told you, ‘I go away, and I come to you.’ If you loved me, you would have rejoiced, because I said ‘I am going to my Father;’ for the Father is greater than I.  <span class="v">29</span>Now I have told you before it happens so that, when it happens, you may believe.  <span class="v">30</span>I will no more speak much with you, for the prince of the world comes, and he has nothing in me.  <span class="v">31</span>But that the world may know that I love the Father, and as the Father commanded me, even so I do. Arise, let us go from here.  <span class="v">1</span>“I am the true vine, and my Father is the farmer.  <span class="v">2</span>Every branch in me that doesn’t bear fruit, he takes away. Every branch that bears fruit, he prunes, that it may bear more fruit.  <span class="v">3</span>You are already pruned clean because of the word which I have spoken to you.  <span class="v">4</span>Remain in me, and I in you. As the branch can’t bear fruit by itself, unless it remains in the vine, so neither can you, unless you remain in me.  <span class="v">5</span>I am the vine. You are the branches. He who remains in me, and I in him, the same bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing.  <span class="v">6</span>If a man doesn’t remain in me, he is thrown out as a branch, and is withered; and they gather them, throw them into the fire, and they are burned.  <span class="v">7</span>If you remain in me, and my words remain in you, you will ask whatever you desire, and it will be done for you. </p>
