<div class="passage">
<p><span class="v">31</span>On that same day, some Pharisees came, saying to him, “Get out of here, and go away, for Herod wants to kill you.”  <span class="v">32</span>He said to them, “Go and tell that fox, ‘Behold, I cast out demons and perform cures today and tomorrow, and the third day I complete my mission.  <span class="v">33</span>Nevertheless I must go on my way today and tomorrow and the next day, for it can’t be that a prophet would perish outside of Jerusalem.’  <span class="v">34</span>“Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that kills the prophets, and stones those who are sent to her! How often I wanted to gather your children together, like a hen gathers her own brood under her wings, and you refused!  <span class="v">35</span>Behold, your house is left to you desolate. I tell you, you will not see me, until you say, ‘Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord!’” </p>
