<div class="passage">
<p><span class="v">1</span>Now in those days, a decree went out from Caesar Augustus that all the world should be enrolled.  <span class="v">2</span>This was the first enrollment made when Quirinius was governor of Syria.  <span class="v">3</span>All went to enroll themselves, everyone to his own city.  <span class="v">4</span>Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of the city of Nazareth, into Judea, to David’s city, which is called Bethlehem, because he was of the house and family of David;  <span class="v">5</span>to enroll himself with Mary, who was pledged to be married to him as wife, being pregnant.  <span class="v">6</span>While they were there, the day had come for her to give birth.  <span class="v">7</span>She gave birth to her firstborn son. She wrapped him in bands of cloth, and laid him in a feeding trough, because there was no room for them in the inn.  <span class="v">8</span>There were shepherds in the same country staying in the field, and keeping watch by night over their flock.  <span class="v">9</span>Behold, an angel of the Lord stood by them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were terrified.  <span class="v">10</span>The angel said to them, “Don’t be afraid, for behold, I bring you good news of great joy which will be to all the people.  <span class="v">11</span>For there is born to you today, in David’s city, a Savior, who is Christ the Lord.  <span class="v">12</span>This is the sign to you: you will find a baby wrapped in strips of cloth, lying in a feeding trough.”  <span class="v">13</span>Suddenly, there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly army praising God, and saying,  <span class="v">14</span>“Glory to God in the highest, on earth peace, good will toward men.”  <span class="v">15</span>When the angels went away from them into the sky, the shepherds said to one another, “Let’s go to Bethlehem, now, and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord has made known to us.”  <span class="v">16</span>They came with haste, and found both Mary and Joseph, and the baby was lying in the feeding trough.  <span class="v">17</span>When they saw it, they publicized widely the saying which was spoken to them about this child.  <span class="v">18</span>All who heard it wondered at the things which were spoken to them by the shepherds.  <span class="v">19</span>But Mary kept all these sayings, pondering them in her heart.  <span class="v">20</span>The shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things that they had heard and seen, just as it was told them. </p>
