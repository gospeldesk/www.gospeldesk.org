<p><span class="v">11</span>Jesus entered into the temple in Jerusalem. When he had looked around at everything, it being now evening, he went out to Bethany with the twelve.  <span class="v">12</span>The next day, when they had come out from Bethany, he was hungry.  <span class="v">13</span>Seeing a fig tree afar off having leaves, he came to see if perhaps he might find anything on it. When he came to it, he found nothing but leaves, for it was not the season for figs.  <span class="v">14</span>Jesus told it, “May no one ever eat fruit from you again!” and his disciples heard it.  <span class="v">15</span>They came to Jerusalem, and Jesus entered into the temple, and began to throw out those who sold and those who bought in the temple, and overthrew the money changers’ tables, and the seats of those who sold the doves.  <span class="v">16</span>He would not allow anyone to carry a container through the temple.  <span class="v">17</span>He taught, saying to them, “Isn’t it written, ‘My house will be called a house of prayer for all the nations?’ But you have made it a den of robbers!”  <span class="v">18</span>The chief priests and the scribes heard it, and sought how they might destroy him. For they feared him, because all the multitude was astonished at his teaching.  <span class="v">19</span>When evening came, he went out of the city.  <span class="v">20</span>As they passed by in the morning, they saw the fig tree withered away from the roots.  <span class="v">21</span>Peter, remembering, said to him, “Rabbi, look! The fig tree which you cursed has withered away.”  <span class="v">22</span>Jesus answered them, “Have faith in God.  <span class="v">23</span>For most certainly I tell you, whoever may tell this mountain, ‘Be taken up and cast into the sea,’ and doesn’t doubt in his heart, but believes that what he says is happening; he shall have whatever he says. </p>
