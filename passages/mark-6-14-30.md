<p><span class="v">14</span>King Herod heard this, for his name had become known, and he said, “John the Baptizer has risen from the dead, and therefore these powers are at work in him.”  <span class="v">15</span>But others said, “He is Elijah.” Others said, “He is a prophet, or like one of the prophets.”  <span class="v">16</span>But Herod, when he heard this, said, “This is John, whom I beheaded. He has risen from the dead.”  <span class="v">17</span>For Herod himself had sent out and arrested John, and bound him in prison for the sake of Herodias, his brother Philip’s wife, for he had married her.  <span class="v">18</span>For John said to Herod, “It is not lawful for you to have your brother’s wife.”  <span class="v">19</span>Herodias set herself against him, and desired to kill him, but she couldn’t,  <span class="v">20</span>for Herod feared John, knowing that he was a righteous and holy man, and kept him safe. When he heard him, he did many things, and he heard him gladly.  <span class="v">21</span>Then a convenient day came, that Herod on his birthday made a supper for his nobles, the high officers, and the chief men of Galilee.  <span class="v">22</span>When the daughter of Herodias herself came in and danced, she pleased Herod and those sitting with him. The king said to the young lady, “Ask me whatever you want, and I will give it to you.”  <span class="v">23</span>He swore to her, “Whatever you shall ask of me, I will give you, up to half of my kingdom.”  <span class="v">24</span>She went out, and said to her mother, “What shall I ask?” She said, “The head of John the Baptizer.”  <span class="v">25</span>She came in immediately with haste to the king, and asked, “I want you to give me right now the head of John the Baptizer on a platter.”  <span class="v">26</span>The king was exceedingly sorry, but for the sake of his oaths, and of his dinner guests, he didn’t wish to refuse her.  <span class="v">27</span>Immediately the king sent out a soldier of his guard, and commanded to bring John’s head, and he went and beheaded him in the prison,  <span class="v">28</span>and brought his head on a platter, and gave it to the young lady; and the young lady gave it to her mother.  <span class="v">29</span>When his disciples heard this, they came and took up his corpse, and laid it in a tomb.  <span class="v">30</span>The apostles gathered themselves together to Jesus, and they told him all things, whatever they had done, and whatever they had taught. </p>
