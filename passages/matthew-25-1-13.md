<div class="passage">
<p><span class="v">1</span>“Then the Kingdom of Heaven will be like ten virgins, who took their lamps, and went out to meet the bridegroom.  <span class="v">2</span>Five of them were foolish, and five were wise.  <span class="v">3</span>Those who were foolish, when they took their lamps, took no oil with them,  <span class="v">4</span>but the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps.  <span class="v">5</span>Now while the bridegroom delayed, they all slumbered and slept.  <span class="v">6</span>But at midnight there was a cry, ‘Behold! The bridegroom is coming! Come out to meet him!’  <span class="v">7</span>Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lamps.  <span class="v">8</span>The foolish said to the wise, ‘Give us some of your oil, for our lamps are going out.’  <span class="v">9</span>But the wise answered, saying, ‘What if there isn’t enough for us and you? You go rather to those who sell, and buy for yourselves.’  <span class="v">10</span>While they went away to buy, the bridegroom came, and those who were ready went in with him to the marriage feast, and the door was shut.  <span class="v">11</span>Afterward the other virgins also came, saying, ‘Lord, Lord, open to us.’  <span class="v">12</span>But he answered, ‘Most certainly I tell you, I don’t know you.’  <span class="v">13</span>Watch therefore, for you don’t know the day nor the hour in which the Son of Man is coming. </p>
