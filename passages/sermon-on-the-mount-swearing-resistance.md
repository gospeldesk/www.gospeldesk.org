<div class="passage">
<p><span class="v">33</span>“Again you have heard that it was said to them of old time, ‘You shall not make false vows, but shall perform to the Lord your vows,’  <span class="v">34</span>but I tell you, don’t swear at all: neither by heaven, for it is the throne of God;  <span class="v">35</span>nor by the earth, for it is the footstool of his feet; nor by Jerusalem, for it is the city of the great King.  <span class="v">36</span>Neither shall you swear by your head, for you can’t make one hair white or black.  <span class="v">37</span>But let your ‘Yes’ be ‘Yes’ and your ‘No’ be ‘No.’ Whatever is more than these is of the evil one.  <span class="v">38</span>“You have heard that it was said, ‘An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth.’  <span class="v">39</span>But I tell you, don’t resist him who is evil; but whoever strikes you on your right cheek, turn to him the other also.  <span class="v">40</span>If anyone sues you to take away your coat, let him have your cloak also.  <span class="v">41</span>Whoever compels you to go one mile, go with him two. </p>
