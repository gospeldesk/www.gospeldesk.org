<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">8</span>“In this is my Father glorified, that you bear much fruit; and so you will be my disciples.  <span class="v">9</span>Even as the Father has loved me, I also have loved you. Remain in my love.  <span class="v">10</span>If you keep my commandments, you will remain in my love; even as I have kept my Father’s commandments, and remain in his love.  <span class="v">11</span>I have spoken these things to you, that my joy may remain in you, and that your joy may be made full.  <span class="v">12</span>“This is my commandment, that you love one another, even as I have loved you.  <span class="v">13</span>Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friends.  <span class="v">14</span>You are my friends, if you do whatever I command you.  <span class="v">15</span>No longer do I call you servants, for the servant doesn’t know what his lord does. But I have called you friends, for everything that I heard from my Father, I have made known to you.  <span class="v">16</span>You didn’t choose me, but I chose you, and appointed you, that you should go and bear fruit, and that your fruit should remain; that whatever you will ask of the Father in my name, he may give it to you. </p>
