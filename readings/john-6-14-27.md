<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">14</span>When therefore the people saw the sign which Jesus did, they said, “This is truly the prophet who comes into the world.”  <span class="v">15</span>Jesus therefore, perceiving that they were about to come and take him by force, to make him king, withdrew again to the mountain by himself.  <span class="v">16</span>When evening came, his disciples went down to the sea,  <span class="v">17</span>and they entered into the boat, and were going over the sea to Capernaum. It was now dark, and Jesus had not come to them.  <span class="v">18</span>The sea was tossed by a great wind blowing.  <span class="v">19</span>When therefore they had rowed about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they saw Jesus walking on the sea, and drawing near to the boat; and they were afraid.  <span class="v">20</span>But he said to them, “It is I. Don’t be afraid.”  <span class="v">21</span>They were willing therefore to receive him into the boat. Immediately the boat was at the land where they were going.  <span class="v">22</span>On the next day, the multitude that stood on the other side of the sea saw that there was no other boat there, except the one in which his disciples had embarked, and that Jesus hadn’t entered with his disciples into the boat, but his disciples had gone away alone.  <span class="v">23</span>However boats from Tiberias came near to the place where they ate the bread after the Lord had given thanks.  <span class="v">24</span>When the multitude therefore saw that Jesus wasn’t there, nor his disciples, they themselves got into the boats, and came to Capernaum, seeking Jesus.  <span class="v">25</span>When they found him on the other side of the sea, they asked him, “Rabbi, when did you come here?”  <span class="v">26</span>Jesus answered them, “Most certainly I tell you, you seek me, not because you saw signs, but because you ate of the loaves, and were filled.  <span class="v">27</span>Don’t work for the food which perishes, but for the food which remains to eternal life, which the Son of Man will give to you. For God the Father has sealed him.” </p>
