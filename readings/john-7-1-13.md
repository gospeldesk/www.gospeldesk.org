<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">1</span>After these things, Jesus was walking in Galilee, for he wouldn’t walk in Judea, because the Jews sought to kill him.  <span class="v">2</span>Now the feast of the Jews, the Feast of Booths, was at hand.  <span class="v">3</span>His brothers therefore said to him, “Depart from here, and go into Judea, that your disciples also may see your works which you do.  <span class="v">4</span>For no one does anything in secret, and himself seeks to be known openly. If you do these things, reveal yourself to the world.”  <span class="v">5</span>For even his brothers didn’t believe in him.  <span class="v">6</span>Jesus therefore said to them, “My time has not yet come, but your time is always ready.  <span class="v">7</span>The world can’t hate you, but it hates me, because I testify about it, that its works are evil.  <span class="v">8</span>You go up to the feast. I am not yet going up to this feast, because my time is not yet fulfilled.”  <span class="v">9</span>Having said these things to them, he stayed in Galilee.  <span class="v">10</span>But when his brothers had gone up to the feast, then he also went up, not publicly, but as it were in secret.  <span class="v">11</span>The Jews therefore sought him at the feast, and said, “Where is he?”  <span class="v">12</span>There was much murmuring among the multitudes concerning him. Some said, “He is a good man.” Others said, “Not so, but he leads the multitude astray.”  <span class="v">13</span>Yet no one spoke openly of him for fear of the Jews. </p>
