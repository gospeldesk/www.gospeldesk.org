<div class="passage">
<p><span class="v">21</span>Jesus said therefore again to them, “I am going away, and you will seek me, and you will die in your sins. Where I go, you can’t come.”  <span class="v">22</span>The Jews therefore said, “Will he kill himself, because he says, ‘Where I am going, you can’t come’ ?”  <span class="v">23</span>He said to them, “You are from beneath. I am from above. You are of this world. I am not of this world.  <span class="v">24</span>I said therefore to you that you will die in your sins; for unless you believe that I am he, you will die in your sins.”  <span class="v">25</span>They said therefore to him, “Who are you?” Jesus said to them, “Just what I have been saying to you from the beginning.  <span class="v">26</span>I have many things to speak and to judge concerning you. However he who sent me is true; and the things which I heard from him, these I say to the world.”  <span class="v">27</span>They didn’t understand that he spoke to them about the Father.  <span class="v">28</span>Jesus therefore said to them, “When you have lifted up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am he, and I do nothing of myself, but as my Father taught me, I say these things.  <span class="v">29</span>He who sent me is with me. The Father hasn’t left me alone, for I always do the things that are pleasing to him.”  <span class="v">30</span>As he spoke these things, many believed in him. </p>
