<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">51</span>Most certainly, I tell you, if a person keeps my word, he will never see death.”  <span class="v">52</span>Then the Jews said to him, “Now we know that you have a demon. Abraham died, and the prophets; and you say, ‘If a man keeps my word, he will never taste of death.’  <span class="v">53</span>Are you greater than our father, Abraham, who died? The prophets died. Who do you make yourself out to be?”  <span class="v">54</span>Jesus answered, “If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing. It is my Father who glorifies me, of whom you say that he is our God.  <span class="v">55</span>You have not known him, but I know him. If I said, ‘I don’t know him,’ I would be like you, a liar. But I know him, and keep his word.  <span class="v">56</span>Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day. He saw it, and was glad.”  <span class="v">57</span>The Jews therefore said to him, “You are not yet fifty years old, and have you seen Abraham?”  <span class="v">58</span>Jesus said to them, “Most certainly, I tell you, before Abraham came into existence, I AM. ”  <span class="v">59</span>Therefore they took up stones to throw at him, but Jesus was hidden, and went out of the temple, having gone through the middle of them, and so passed by. </p>
