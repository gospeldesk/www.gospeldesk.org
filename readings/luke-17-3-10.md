<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">3</span>Be careful. If your brother sins against you, rebuke him. If he repents, forgive him.  <span class="v">4</span>If he sins against you seven times in the day, and seven times returns, saying, ‘I repent,’ you shall forgive him.”  <span class="v">5</span>The apostles said to the Lord, “Increase our faith.”  <span class="v">6</span>The Lord said, “If you had faith like a grain of mustard seed, you would tell this sycamore tree, ‘Be uprooted, and be planted in the sea,’ and it would obey you.  <span class="v">7</span>But who is there among you, having a servant plowing or keeping sheep, that will say, when he comes in from the field, ‘Come immediately and sit down at the table,’  <span class="v">8</span>and will not rather tell him, ‘Prepare my supper, clothe yourself properly, and serve me, while I eat and drink. Afterward you shall eat and drink’?  <span class="v">9</span>Does he thank that servant because he did the things that were commanded? I think not.  <span class="v">10</span>Even so you also, when you have done all the things that are commanded you, say, ‘We are unworthy servants. We have done our duty.’” </p>
