<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">1</span>He also spoke a parable to them that they must always pray, and not give up,  <span class="v">2</span>saying, “There was a judge in a certain city who didn’t fear God, and didn’t respect man.  <span class="v">3</span>A widow was in that city, and she often came to him, saying, ‘Defend me from my adversary!’  <span class="v">4</span>He wouldn’t for a while, but afterward he said to himself, ‘Though I neither fear God, nor respect man,  <span class="v">5</span>yet because this widow bothers me, I will defend her, or else she will wear me out by her continual coming.’”  <span class="v">6</span>The Lord said, “Listen to what the unrighteous judge says.  <span class="v">7</span>Won’t God avenge his chosen ones, who are crying out to him day and night, and yet he exercises patience with them?  <span class="v">8</span>I tell you that he will avenge them quickly. Nevertheless, when the Son of Man comes, will he find faith on the earth?” </p>
