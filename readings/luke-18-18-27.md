<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">18</span>A certain ruler asked him, saying, “Good Teacher, what shall I do to inherit eternal life?”  <span class="v">19</span>Jesus asked him, “Why do you call me good? No one is good, except one—God.  <span class="v">20</span>You know the commandments: ‘Don’t commit adultery,’ ‘Don’t murder,’ ‘Don’t steal,’ ‘Don’t give false testimony,’ ‘Honor your father and your mother.’”  <span class="v">21</span>He said, “I have observed all these things from my youth up.”  <span class="v">22</span>When Jesus heard these things, he said to him, “You still lack one thing. Sell all that you have, and distribute it to the poor. You will have treasure in heaven. Come, follow me.”  <span class="v">23</span>But when he heard these things, he became very sad, for he was very rich.  <span class="v">24</span>Jesus, seeing that he became very sad, said, “How hard it is for those who have riches to enter into God’s Kingdom!  <span class="v">25</span>For it is easier for a camel to enter in through a needle’s eye, than for a rich man to enter into God’s Kingdom.”  <span class="v">26</span>Those who heard it said, “Then who can be saved?”  <span class="v">27</span>But he said, “The things which are impossible with men are possible with God.” </p>
