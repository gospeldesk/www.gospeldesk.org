<div class="passage">
<p><span class="v">27</span>Some of the Sadducees came to him, those who deny that there is a resurrection.  <span class="v">28</span>They asked him, “Teacher, Moses wrote to us that if a man’s brother dies having a wife, and he is childless, his brother should take the wife, and raise up children for his brother.  <span class="v">29</span>There were therefore seven brothers. The first took a wife, and died childless.  <span class="v">30</span>The second took her as wife, and he died childless.  <span class="v">31</span>The third took her, and likewise the seven all left no children, and died.  <span class="v">32</span>Afterward the woman also died.  <span class="v">33</span>Therefore in the resurrection whose wife of them will she be? For the seven had her as a wife.”  <span class="v">34</span>Jesus said to them, “The children of this age marry, and are given in marriage.  <span class="v">35</span>But those who are considered worthy to attain to that age and the resurrection from the dead, neither marry, nor are given in marriage.  <span class="v">36</span>For they can’t die any more, for they are like the angels, and are children of God, being children of the resurrection.  <span class="v">37</span>But that the dead are raised, even Moses showed at the bush, when he called the Lord ‘The God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.’  <span class="v">38</span>Now he is not the God of the dead, but of the living, for all are alive to him.”  <span class="v">39</span>Some of the scribes answered, “Teacher, you speak well.”  <span class="v">40</span>They didn’t dare to ask him any more questions.  <span class="v">41</span>He said to them, “Why do they say that the Christ is David’s son?  <span class="v">42</span>David himself says in the book of Psalms, ‘The Lord said to my Lord, “Sit at my right hand,  <span class="v">43</span>until I make your enemies the footstool of your feet.”’  <span class="v">44</span>“David therefore calls him Lord, so how is he his son?” </p>
</div>
