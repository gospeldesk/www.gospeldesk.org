<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">5</span>As some were talking about the temple and how it was decorated with beautiful stones and gifts, he said,  <span class="v">6</span>“As for these things which you see, the days will come, in which there will not be left here one stone on another that will not be thrown down.”  <span class="v">7</span>They asked him, “Teacher, so when will these things be? What is the sign that these things are about to happen?”  <span class="v">10</span>Then he said to them, “Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom.  <span class="v">11</span>There will be great earthquakes, famines, and plagues in various places. There will be terrors and great signs from heaven.  <span class="v">20</span>“But when you see Jerusalem surrounded by armies, then know that its desolation is at hand.  <span class="v">21</span>Then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains. Let those who are in the middle of her depart. Let those who are in the country not enter therein.  <span class="v">22</span>For these are days of vengeance, that all things which are written may be fulfilled.  <span class="v">23</span>Woe to those who are pregnant and to those who nurse infants in those days! For there will be great distress in the land, and wrath to this people.  <span class="v">24</span>They will fall by the edge of the sword, and will be led captive into all the nations. Jerusalem will be trampled down by the Gentiles, until the times of the Gentiles are fulfilled. </p>
