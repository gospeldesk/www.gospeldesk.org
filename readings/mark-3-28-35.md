<div class="passage">
<p><span class="v">28</span>Most certainly I tell you, all sins of the descendants of man will be forgiven, including their blasphemies with which they may blaspheme;  <span class="v">29</span>but whoever may blaspheme against the Holy Spirit never has forgiveness, but is subject to eternal condemnation.”  <span class="v">30</span>—because they said, “He has an unclean spirit.”  <span class="v">31</span>His mother and his brothers came, and standing outside, they sent to him, calling him.  <span class="v">32</span>A multitude was sitting around him, and they told him, “Behold, your mother, your brothers, and your sisters are outside looking for you.”  <span class="v">33</span>He answered them, “Who are my mother and my brothers?”  <span class="v">34</span>Looking around at those who sat around him, he said, “Behold, my mother and my brothers!  <span class="v">35</span>For whoever does the will of God, the same is my brother, and my sister, and mother.” </p>
