<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">1</span>Again he began to teach by the seaside. A great multitude was gathered to him, so that he entered into a boat in the sea, and sat down. All the multitude were on the land by the sea.  <span class="v">2</span>He taught them many things in parables, and told them in his teaching,  <span class="v">3</span>“Listen! Behold, the farmer went out to sow,  <span class="v">4</span>and as he sowed, some seed fell by the road, and the birds came and devoured it.  <span class="v">5</span>Others fell on the rocky ground, where it had little soil, and immediately it sprang up, because it had no depth of soil.  <span class="v">6</span>When the sun had risen, it was scorched; and because it had no root, it withered away.  <span class="v">7</span>Others fell among the thorns, and the thorns grew up, and choked it, and it yielded no fruit.  <span class="v">8</span>Others fell into the good ground, and yielded fruit, growing up and increasing. Some produced thirty times, some sixty times, and some one hundred times as much.”  <span class="v">9</span>He said, “Whoever has ears to hear, let him hear.” </p>
