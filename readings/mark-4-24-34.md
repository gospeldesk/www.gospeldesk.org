<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">24</span>He said to them, “Take heed what you hear. With whatever measure you measure, it will be measured to you, and more will be given to you who hear.  <span class="v">25</span>For whoever has, to him will more be given, and he who doesn’t have, even that which he has will be taken away from him.”  <span class="v">26</span>He said, “God’s Kingdom is as if a man should cast seed on the earth,  <span class="v">27</span>and should sleep and rise night and day, and the seed should spring up and grow, though he doesn’t know how.  <span class="v">28</span>For the earth bears fruit: first the blade, then the ear, then the full grain in the ear.  <span class="v">29</span>But when the fruit is ripe, immediately he puts in the sickle, because the harvest has come.”  <span class="v">30</span>He said, “How will we liken God’s Kingdom? Or with what parable will we illustrate it?  <span class="v">31</span>It’s like a grain of mustard seed, which, when it is sown in the earth, though it is less than all the seeds that are on the earth,  <span class="v">32</span>yet when it is sown, grows up, and becomes greater than all the herbs, and puts out great branches, so that the birds of the sky can lodge under its shadow.”  <span class="v">33</span>With many such parables he spoke the word to them, as they were able to hear it.  <span class="v">34</span>Without a parable he didn’t speak to them; but privately to his own disciples he explained everything. </p>
