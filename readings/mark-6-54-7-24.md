<p><span class="v">54</span>When they had come out of the boat, immediately the people recognized him,  <span class="v">55</span>and ran around that whole region, and began to bring those who were sick, on their mats, to where they heard he was.  <span class="v">56</span>Wherever he entered, into villages, or into cities, or into the country, they laid the sick in the marketplaces, and begged him that they might just touch the fringe of his garment; and as many as touched him were made well.  <span class="v">1</span>Then the Pharisees and some of the scribes gathered together to him, having come from Jerusalem.  <span class="v">2</span>Now when they saw some of his disciples eating bread with defiled, that is unwashed, hands, they found fault.  <span class="v">3</span>(For the Pharisees and all the Jews, don’t eat unless they wash their hands and forearms, holding to the tradition of the elders.  <span class="v">4</span>They don’t eat when they come from the marketplace unless they bathe themselves, and there are many other things, which they have received to hold to: washings of cups, pitchers, bronze vessels, and couches.)  <span class="v">5</span>The Pharisees and the scribes asked him, “Why don’t your disciples walk according to the tradition of the elders, but eat their bread with unwashed hands?”  <span class="v">6</span>He answered them, “Well did Isaiah prophesy of you hypocrites, as it is written, ‘This people honors me with their lips, but their heart is far from me.  <span class="v">7</span>But they worship me in vain, teaching as doctrines the commandments of men.’  <span class="v">8</span>“For you set aside the commandment of God, and hold tightly to the tradition of men—the washing of pitchers and cups, and you do many other such things.”  <span class="v">9</span>He said to them, “Full well do you reject the commandment of God, that you may keep your tradition.  <span class="v">10</span>For Moses said, ‘Honor your father and your mother;’ and, ‘He who speaks evil of father or mother, let him be put to death.’  <span class="v">11</span>But you say, ‘If a man tells his father or his mother, “Whatever profit you might have received from me is Corban, that is to say, given to God”;’  <span class="v">12</span>then you no longer allow him to do anything for his father or his mother,  <span class="v">13</span>making void the word of God by your tradition, which you have handed down. You do many things like this.”  <span class="v">14</span>He called all the multitude to himself, and said to them, “Hear me, all of you, and understand.  <span class="v">15</span>There is nothing from outside of the man, that going into him can defile him; but the things which proceed out of the man are those that defile the man.  <span class="v">16</span>If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear!”  <span class="v">17</span>When he had entered into a house away from the multitude, his disciples asked him about the parable.  <span class="v">18</span>He said to them, “Are you also without understanding? Don’t you perceive that whatever goes into the man from outside can’t defile him,  <span class="v">19</span>because it doesn’t go into his heart, but into his stomach, then into the latrine, thus purifying all foods ?”  <span class="v">20</span>He said, “That which proceeds out of the man, that defiles the man.  <span class="v">21</span>For from within, out of the hearts of men, proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, sexual sins, murders, thefts,  <span class="v">22</span>covetings, wickedness, deceit, lustful desires, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, and foolishness.  <span class="v">23</span>All these evil things come from within, and defile the man.”  <span class="v">24</span>From there he arose, and went away into the borders of Tyre and Sidon. He entered into a house, and didn’t want anyone to know it, but he couldn’t escape notice. </p>
