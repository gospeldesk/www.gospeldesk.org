<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">54</span>Coming into his own country, he taught them in their synagogue, so that they were astonished, and said, “Where did this man get this wisdom, and these mighty works?  <span class="v">55</span>Isn’t this the carpenter’s son? Isn’t his mother called Mary, and his brothers, James, Joses, Simon, and Judas?  <span class="v">56</span>Aren’t all of his sisters with us? Where then did this man get all of these things?”  <span class="v">57</span>They were offended by him. But Jesus said to them, “A prophet is not without honor, except in his own country, and in his own house.”  <span class="v">58</span>He didn’t do many mighty works there because of their unbelief. </p>
