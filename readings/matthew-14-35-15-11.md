<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">35</span>When the people of that place recognized him, they sent into all that surrounding region, and brought to him all who were sick;  <span class="v">36</span>and they begged him that they might just touch the fringe of his garment. As many as touched it were made whole.  <span class="v">1</span>Then Pharisees and scribes came to Jesus from Jerusalem, saying,  <span class="v">2</span>“Why do your disciples disobey the tradition of the elders? For they don’t wash their hands when they eat bread.”  <span class="v">3</span>He answered them, “Why do you also disobey the commandment of God because of your tradition?  <span class="v">4</span>For God commanded, ‘Honor your father and your mother,’ and, ‘He who speaks evil of father or mother, let him be put to death.’  <span class="v">5</span>But you say, ‘Whoever may tell his father or his mother, “Whatever help you might otherwise have gotten from me is a gift devoted to God,”  <span class="v">6</span>he shall not honor his father or mother.’ You have made the commandment of God void because of your tradition.  <span class="v">7</span>You hypocrites! Well did Isaiah prophesy of you, saying,  <span class="v">8</span>‘These people draw near to me with their mouth, and honor me with their lips; but their heart is far from me.  <span class="v">9</span>And in vain do they worship me, teaching as doctrine rules made by men.’”  <span class="v">10</span>He summoned the multitude, and said to them, “Hear, and understand.  <span class="v">11</span>That which enters into the mouth doesn’t defile the man; but that which proceeds out of the mouth, this defiles the man.” </p>
