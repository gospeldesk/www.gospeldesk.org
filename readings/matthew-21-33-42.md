<p><span class="v">33</span>“Hear another parable. There was a man who was a master of a household, who planted a vineyard, set a hedge about it, dug a wine press in it, built a tower, leased it out to farmers, and went into another country.  <span class="v">34</span>When the season for the fruit came near, he sent his servants to the farmers, to receive his fruit.  <span class="v">35</span>The farmers took his servants, beat one, killed another, and stoned another.  <span class="v">36</span>Again, he sent other servants more than the first: and they treated them the same way.  <span class="v">37</span>But afterward he sent to them his son, saying, ‘They will respect my son.’  <span class="v">38</span>But the farmers, when they saw the son, said among themselves, ‘This is the heir. Come, let’s kill him, and seize his inheritance.’  <span class="v">39</span>So they took him, and threw him out of the vineyard, and killed him.  <span class="v">40</span>When therefore the lord of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those farmers?”  <span class="v">41</span>They told him, “He will miserably destroy those miserable men, and will lease out the vineyard to other farmers, who will give him the fruit in its season.”  <span class="v">42</span>Jesus said to them, “Did you never read in the Scriptures, ‘The stone which the builders rejected, the same was made the head of the corner. This was from the Lord. It is marvelous in our eyes?’ </p>
