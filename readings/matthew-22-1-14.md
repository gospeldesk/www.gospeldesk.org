<div class="reading">
<p><span class="v">1</span>Jesus answered and spoke to them again in parables, saying,  <span class="v">2</span>“The Kingdom of Heaven is like a certain king, who made a marriage feast for his son,  <span class="v">3</span>and sent out his servants to call those who were invited to the marriage feast, but they would not come.  <span class="v">4</span>Again he sent out other servants, saying, ‘Tell those who are invited, “Behold, I have prepared my dinner. My cattle and my fatlings are killed, and all things are ready. Come to the marriage feast!”’  <span class="v">5</span>But they made light of it, and went their ways, one to his own farm, another to his merchandise,  <span class="v">6</span>and the rest grabbed his servants, and treated them shamefully, and killed them.  <span class="v">7</span>When the king heard that, he was angry, and sent his armies, destroyed those murderers, and burned their city.  <span class="v">8</span>“Then he said to his servants, ‘The wedding is ready, but those who were invited weren’t worthy.  <span class="v">9</span>Go therefore to the intersections of the highways, and as many as you may find, invite to the marriage feast.’  <span class="v">10</span>Those servants went out into the highways, and gathered together as many as they found, both bad and good. The wedding was filled with guests.  <span class="v">11</span>But when the king came in to see the guests, he saw there a man who didn’t have on wedding clothing,  <span class="v">12</span>and he said to him, ‘Friend, how did you come in here not wearing wedding clothing?’ He was speechless.  <span class="v">13</span>Then the king said to the servants, ‘Bind him hand and foot, take him away, and throw him into the outer darkness. That is where the weeping and grinding of teeth will be.’  <span class="v">14</span>For many are called, but few chosen.” </p>
