<p><span class="v">17</span>Now on the first day of unleavened bread, the disciples came to Jesus, saying to him, “Where do you want us to prepare for you to eat the Passover?”  <span class="v">18</span>He said, “Go into the city to a certain person, and tell him, ‘The Teacher says, “My time is at hand. I will keep the Passover at your house with my disciples.”’”  <span class="v">19</span>The disciples did as Jesus commanded them, and they prepared the Passover.  <span class="v">20</span>Now when evening had come, he was reclining at the table with the twelve disciples.  <span class="v">21</span>As they were eating, he said, “Most certainly I tell you that one of you will betray me.”  <span class="v">22</span>They were exceedingly sorrowful, and each began to ask him, “It isn’t me, is it, Lord?”  <span class="v">23</span>He answered, “He who dipped his hand with me in the dish, the same will betray me.  <span class="v">24</span>The Son of Man goes, even as it is written of him, but woe to that man through whom the Son of Man is betrayed! It would be better for that man if he had not been born.”  <span class="v">25</span>Judas, who betrayed him, answered, “It isn’t me, is it, Rabbi?” He said to him, “You said it.”  <span class="v">26</span>As they were eating, Jesus took bread, gave thanks for it, and broke it. He gave to the disciples, and said, “Take, eat; this is my body.”  <span class="v">27</span>He took the cup, gave thanks, and gave to them, saying, “All of you drink it,  <span class="v">28</span>for this is my blood of the new covenant, which is poured out for many for the remission of sins.  <span class="v">29</span>But I tell you that I will not drink of this fruit of the vine from now on, until that day when I drink it anew with you in my Father’s Kingdom.”  <span class="v">30</span>When they had sung a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.  <span class="v">31</span>Then Jesus said to them, “All of you will be made to stumble because of me tonight, for it is written, ‘I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.’  <span class="v">32</span>But after I am raised up, I will go before you into Galilee.”  <span class="v">33</span>But Peter answered him, “Even if all will be made to stumble because of you, I will never be made to stumble.”  <span class="v">34</span>Jesus said to him, “Most certainly I tell you that tonight, before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times.”  <span class="v">35</span>Peter said to him, “Even if I must die with you, I will not deny you.” All of the disciples also said likewise.  <span class="v">36</span>Then Jesus came with them to a place called Gethsemane, and said to his disciples, “Sit here, while I go there and pray.”  <span class="v">37</span>He took with him Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, and began to be sorrowful and severely troubled.  <span class="v">38</span>Then he said to them, “My soul is exceedingly sorrowful, even to death. Stay here, and watch with me.”  <span class="v">39</span>He went forward a little, fell on his face, and prayed, saying, “My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass away from me; nevertheless, not what I desire, but what you desire.”  <span class="v">40</span>He came to the disciples, and found them sleeping, and said to Peter, “What, couldn’t you watch with me for one hour?  <span class="v">41</span>Watch and pray, that you don’t enter into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.”  <span class="v">42</span>Again, a second time he went away, and prayed, saying, “My Father, if this cup can’t pass away from me unless I drink it, your desire be done.”  <span class="v">43</span>He came again and found them sleeping, for their eyes were heavy.  <span class="v">44</span>He left them again, went away, and prayed a third time, saying the same words.  <span class="v">45</span>Then he came to his disciples, and said to them, “Sleep on now, and take your rest. Behold, the hour is at hand, and the Son of Man is betrayed into the hands of sinners.  <span class="v">46</span>Arise, let’s be going. Behold, he who betrays me is at hand.”  <span class="v">47</span>While he was still speaking, behold, Judas, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great multitude with swords and clubs, from the chief priests and elders of the people.  <span class="v">48</span>Now he who betrayed him gave them a sign, saying, “Whoever I kiss, he is the one. Seize him.”  <span class="v">49</span>Immediately he came to Jesus, and said, “Hail, Rabbi!” and kissed him.  <span class="v">50</span>Jesus said to him, “Friend, why are you here?” Then they came and laid hands on Jesus, and took him.  <span class="v">51</span>Behold, one of those who were with Jesus stretched out his hand, and drew his sword, and struck the servant of the high priest, and struck off his ear.  <span class="v">52</span>Then Jesus said to him, “Put your sword back into its place, for all those who take the sword will die by the sword.  <span class="v">53</span>Or do you think that I couldn’t ask my Father, and he would even now send me more than twelve legions of angels?  <span class="v">54</span>How then would the Scriptures be fulfilled that it must be so?”  <span class="v">55</span>In that hour Jesus said to the multitudes, “Have you come out as against a robber with swords and clubs to seize me? I sat daily in the temple teaching, and you didn’t arrest me.  <span class="v">56</span>But all this has happened, that the Scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled.” Then all the disciples left him, and fled. </p>
